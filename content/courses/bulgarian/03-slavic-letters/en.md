# 3. The Slavic letters

The last nine letters write sounds Greek did not have: ж, ц, ч, ш, щ, ъ, ь, ю, я. Among them is ъ, a vowel few alphabets have a letter for. This lesson completes the 30 letters and the everyday words of politeness.

## Nine letters for Slavic sounds

| Letter | Sound | Transliteration |
|---|---|---|
| Ж ж | s in *pleasure* | zh |
| Ц ц | ts in *cats* | ts |
| Ч ч | ch in *church* | ch |
| Ш ш | sh in *ship* | sh |
| Щ щ | sht in *ashtray* | sht |
| Ъ ъ | a in *about* | a |
| Ь ь | y before о | y |
| Ю ю | yu in *you* | yu |
| Я я | ya in *yard* | ya |

- Щ is *sht* in Bulgarian, not the *shch* of Russian: щастие *shtastie*, "happiness".
- Ъ is a full vowel, the sound of the *a* in "about": добър *dobar*, "good". The official transliteration writes it *a*, as а; you may also type *ă* or *ǎ*.
- Ь is only found before о, to soften the consonant: шофьор *shofyor*, "driver".
- Ж is *zh*, Ч *ch*, Ш *sh*: you may also type *ž*, *č*, *š*.

## Politeness

| Bulgarian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| добър ден | *dobar den* | good day, hello |
| благодаря | *blagodarya* | thank you |
| довиждане | *dovizhdane* | goodbye |
| чай | *chay* | tea |
| хляб | *hlyab* | bread |
| жена | *zhena* | woman, wife |
| къща | *kashta* | house |

- Добър ден is said in the day; добро утро *dobro utro* in the morning, добър вечер *dobar vecher* in the evening.
- Благодаря is also said мерси *mersi*, from French.
- Довиждане is "until we see each other": до "until", виждане "seeing".
