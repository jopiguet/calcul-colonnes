# 2. Letters from Greek

Nine more letters come from Greek or look new: Б, Г, Д, З, И, Й, Л, П, Ф. With them and the first twelve you can read most Bulgarian words, and greet someone.

## Nine letters

| Letter | Sound | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Б б | b | not the same as В |
| Г г | g | always hard, as in *go* |
| Д д | d | |
| З з | z | looks like the digit 3 |
| И и | i | as in *machine* |
| Й й | y | as in *boy*, after or before a vowel |
| Л л | l | |
| П п | p | Greek pi |
| Ф ф | f | Greek phi |

- Б and В are two letters: б is *b*, в is *v*. Бира is *bira*, "beer"; вода is *voda*, "water".
- И is a vowel, *i*; Й, "short i", is the consonant *y*: здравей *zdravey*.
- In handwriting and in italics some letters change shape: т looks like a Latin m, п like n, и like u. Print, as here, keeps the shapes of the table.

## Words and a greeting

| Bulgarian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| вода | *voda* | water |
| бира | *bira* | beer |
| лимон | *limon* | lemon |
| град | *grad* | town, city |
| ден | *den* | day |
| здравей | *zdravey* | hello |

- Здравей is "hello" to one person you call *ти*; to several people, or politely, say здравейте *zdraveyte*.
- Град is the *-grad* of Belgrade and of old town names: "a walled place".
