# 4. The city: the article and "to be"

Bulgarian has no word for "the": like Romanian and Albanian, its neighbours in the Balkans, it puts the article at the end of the word. This lesson gives the article, the verb "to be" and the question with ли.

## The article at the end

| Without | With the article | Gender |
|---|---|---|
| град *grad* "a town" | градът *gradat*, града *grada* "the town" | masculine |
| жена *zhena* "a woman" | жената *zhenata* "the woman" | feminine |
| село *selo* "a village" | селото *seloto* "the village" | neuter |
| градове *gradove* "towns" | градовете *gradovete* "the towns" | plural |

- Masculine: -ът when the noun is the subject (градът е голям, "the town is big"), -а otherwise (в града, "in the town"). Both are said *-a*; the spelling keeps the rule.
- Feminine -та, neuter -то, most plurals -те.
- A word ending in -а or -я is usually feminine, in -о or -е neuter, in a consonant masculine.

## "To be", and the question

| Bulgarian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| аз съм | *az sam* | I am |
| ти си | *ti si* | you are (one person) |
| той, тя, то е | *toy, tya, to e* | he, she, it is |
| ние сме | *nie sme* | we are |
| вие сте | *vie ste* | you are (several, or polite) |
| те са | *te sa* | they are |

- Аз съм студент. *Az sam student.* "I am a student." The pronoun can go: Студент съм.
- Ли makes a question; it follows the word asked about: Студент ли си? "Are you a student?"
- Да is "yes", не "no". Many Bulgarians shake the head for "yes" and nod for "no", the other way round from most of Europe.

::: exercise be
Which form of "to be"?

- аз … → ((съм* / си / е))
- ти … → ((си* / сме / са))
- ние … → ((сме* / сте / съм))
- те … → ((са* / е / сте))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- Аз съм студент. → {{I am a student. / I'm a student.}}

:::

::: summary
- The article is an ending: градът / града, жената, селото, градовете.
- съм, си, е, сме, сте, са: "to be".
- ли asks: Студент ли си? Да "yes", не "no".
:::
