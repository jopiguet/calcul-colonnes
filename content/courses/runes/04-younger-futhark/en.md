# 4. The Younger Futhark

Between about 700 and 800, as the Viking Age began, Scandinavia cut the row down to sixteen runes. The language had gained sounds, yet the row lost eight: each rune now stood for several sounds. This is the row of the runestones, raised by the thousand in Sweden, Denmark and Norway, most of them in the 11th century.

## Sixteen runes

| Rune | Sound | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ᚠ | f | *fé* | wealth |
| ᚢ | u, o, v, w, y | *úr* | drizzle, slag |
| ᚦ | þ, ð | *þurs* | giant |
| ᚬ | ą, o | *óss* | god, estuary |
| ᚱ | r | *reið* | ride |
| ᚴ | k, g | *kaun* | ulcer |
| ᚼ | h | *hagall* | hail |
| ᚾ | n | *nauðr* | need |
| ᛁ | i, e, j | *íss* | ice |
| ᛅ | a, æ, e | *ár* | year, plenty |
| ᛋ | s | *sól* | sun |
| ᛏ | t, d | *týr* | the god Týr |
| ᛒ | b, p | *bjarkan* | birch |
| ᛘ | m | *maðr* | man |
| ᛚ | l | *lǫgr* | water, sea |
| ᛦ | R | *ýr* | yew |

- One rune, several sounds: ᚴ is *k* and *g*, ᛏ *t* and *d*, ᛒ *b* and *p*, ᚢ every rounded vowel and *v*. The reader guessed from the word.
- ᚼ, ᛅ, ᛘ and ᛦ are new shapes. ᛦ *R* is the old ᛉ turned upside down; it marks the old *z*, by now a kind of *r*.
- Runes are transliterated rune by rune, in bold in scholarly editions: ᚴᚢᚾᛅᚱ is *kunar*, the name *Gunnarr*.
- Sweden and Norway also used "short-twig" runes, quicker to cut, with shorter strokes. This course uses the common "long-branch" forms.

## A runestone

Most runestones say the same thing: who raised the stone, in memory of whom, and how they were related. This one is written for the course, on the model of the Swedish stones.

ᚴᚢᚾᛅᚱ ᚱᛅᛁᛋᚦᛁ ᛋᛏᛅᛁᚾ ᚦᛅᚾᛋᛁ ᛅᚠᛏ ᚢᛚᛅᚠ ᚠᛅᚦᚢᚱ ᛋᛁᚾ

*kunar raisþi stain þansi aft ulaf faþur sin*: "Gunnarr raised this stone in memory of Ólafr, his father."

| Runes | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ᚱᛅᛁᛋᚦᛁ | raisþi | raised |
| ᛋᛏᛅᛁᚾ | stain | stone |
| ᚦᛅᚾᛋᛁ | þansi | this |
| ᛅᚠᛏ | aft | after, in memory of |
| ᚠᛅᚦᚢᚱ | faþur | father |
| ᛋᚢᚾ | sun | son |
| ᛋᛁᚾ | sin | his |

Many stones end with a prayer, ᚴᚢᚦ ᚼᛁᛅᛚᛒᛁ ᛅᚾᛏ ᚼᛅᚾᛋ *kuþ hialbi ant hans*, "God help his soul": the runestones were raised as the North became Christian. Other inscriptions name the old gods: ᚦᚢᚱ *þur* Thor, ᚢᚦᛁᚾ *uþin* Odin.

::: summary
- Between 700 and 800 the row fell to sixteen runes, and each stood for several sounds: ᚴ *k, g*, ᛏ *t, d*, ᛒ *b, p*, ᚢ *u, o, v, w*.
- New shapes: ᚼ *h*, ᛅ *a*, ᛘ *m*, ᛦ *R*.
- The runestone formula: ᚴᚢᚾᛅᚱ ᚱᛅᛁᛋᚦᛁ ᛋᛏᛅᛁᚾ ᚦᛅᚾᛋᛁ ᛅᚠᛏ ᚢᛚᛅᚠ ᚠᛅᚦᚢᚱ ᛋᛁᚾ, "Gunnarr raised this stone in memory of Ólafr, his father."
- ᛋᛏᛅᛁᚾ *stain* stone, ᚠᛅᚦᚢᚱ *faþur* father, ᛋᚢᚾ *sun* son, ᚦᚢᚱ *þur* Thor, ᚢᚦᛁᚾ *uþin* Odin.
:::
