# 17. Reading: Ferdowsi and Saadi

Persian has changed so little in a thousand years that an Iranian schoolchild reads the classical poets with only a few footnotes. Ferdowsi finished the *Shâhnâme*, the *Book of Kings*, around 1010; Saadi wrote the *Golestân*, the *Rose Garden*, in 1258 in Shiraz. Both are quoted every day. With the grammar of this course and a handful of old forms, you can read them too.

## Ferdowsi

| Persian | Translation |
|---|---|
| توانا بود هر که دانا بود | Strong is whoever is wise; |
| ز دانش دل پیر برنا بود | through knowledge an old heart is young. |

*tavânâ bovad har ke dânâ bovad / ze dânesh del-e pir bornâ bovad.* بود here is the classical present "is", used for general truths; ز is the poetic form of از.

## Saadi

| Persian | Translation |
|---|---|
| بنی‌آدم اعضای یک پیکرند | The children of Adam are limbs of one body, |
| که در آفرینش ز یک گوهرند | for in creation they are of one essence. |
| چو عضوی به درد آورد روزگار | When fate brings pain to one limb, |
| دگر عضوها را نماند قرار | the other limbs can find no rest. |
| تو کز محنت دیگران بی‌غمی | You who feel no grief at others' suffering, |
| نشاید که نامت نهند آدمی | it is not fitting that they call you human. |

These lines from the *Golestân* are among the best known in the language; an older reading of the first line has اعضای یکدیگرند "limbs of one another".

## Classical forms

| Classical | Modern | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ز | از | from, of, through |
| چو | چون | when; like |
| دگر | دیگر | other |
| کز | که از | who … from, that … of |
| بود (present) | است | is (general truth) |
| نماند، نشاید | نمی‌ماند، شایسته نیست | does not remain; is not fitting |

::: exercise sense
What does it mean?

- برنا → ((young* / old / wise))
- گوهر → ((essence, jewel* / body / grief))
- آفرینش → ((creation* / knowledge / rest))
- بی‌غم → ((without grief* / full of grief / without a body))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- توانا بود هر که دانا بود → {{Strong is whoever is wise. / He who is wise is powerful.}}
- بنی‌آدم اعضای یک پیکرند → {{The children of Adam are limbs of one body. / Human beings are members of one body.}}
- نشاید که نامت نهند آدمی → {{It is not fitting that they call you human. / You do not deserve to be called human.}}
:::

::: summary
- Ferdowsi, *Shâhnâme*: توانا بود هر که دانا بود.
- Saadi, *Golestân*: بنی‌آدم اعضای یک پیکرند.
- Classical forms: ز = از، چو = چون، دگر = دیگر، کز = که از; بود as a gnomic present.
:::
