# 13. Numbers, time and dates

Persian numbers are regular, and they have their own digits, written left to right inside right-to-left text: ۱۴۰۵ is 1405. Iran also has its own calendar, the Solar Hijri, which starts each year at the spring equinox with نوروز *Nowruz*, the New Year. This lesson gives the numbers, the clock and the calendar.

## Numbers

| Digit | Number | Digit | Number |
|---|---|---|---|
| ۱ | یک *yek* | ۶ | شش *shesh* |
| ۲ | دو *do* | ۷ | هفت *haft* |
| ۳ | سه *se* | ۸ | هشت *hasht* |
| ۴ | چهار *chahâr* | ۹ | نه *noh* |
| ۵ | پنج *panj* | ۱۰ | ده *dah* |

| Number | Persian |
|---|---|
| 11, 12 | یازده *yâzdah*, دوازده *davâzdah* |
| 20, 30 | بیست *bist*, سی *si* |
| 25 | بیست و پنج *bist-o panj* |
| 100, 1000 | صد *sad*, هزار *hezâr* |

Compound numbers are joined by و, pronounced *-o*: صد و بیست و سه *sad-o bist-o se* 123. Ordinals add ـم *-om*: دوم *dovvom* second, سوم *sevvom* third; "first" is اول *avval*. Before a noun, ـمین *-omin*: سومین روز "the third day".

## The time

ساعت چند است؟ *sâ'at chand ast?* "What time is it?" – ساعت سه است "It is three o'clock". و نیم *-o nim* is "half past", و ربع *-o rob'* "quarter past", ربع کم *rob' kam* "quarter to": ساعت پنج و نیم "half past five".

## The calendar

| Day | Persian | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | شنبه *shanbe* | first day of the week |
| Sunday | یکشنبه *yekshanbe* | "one-after-Saturday" |
| Monday | دوشنبه *doshanbe* | |
| Friday | جمعه *jom'e* | weekly day off |

The Solar Hijri year counts from 622, the Hijra, in solar years. The year 1405 began on 21 March 2026. The first month is فروردین *Farvardin*, and the last day of the year is followed by thirteen days of Nowruz holidays.
