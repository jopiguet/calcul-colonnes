# 12. The future and conditions

Persian has a future tense, but it belongs mostly to writing, news and formal speech: in conversation the present does the job, فردا می‌روم "I'm going tomorrow". Conditions use the tenses you know, combined with اگر *agar* "if"; one combination, with the past continuous, gives the "would" of English.

## The future

| Person | خواستن (present, no می) + short past stem | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| من | خواهم رفت *khâham raft* | I will go |
| تو | خواهی رفت *khâhi raft* | you will go |
| او | خواهد رفت *khâhad raft* | he will go |
| ما | خواهیم رفت *khâhim raft* | we will go |
| شما | خواهید رفت *khâhid raft* | you will go |
| آنها | خواهند رفت *khâhand raft* | they will go |

The negative is نخواهم رفت *nakhâham raft*. In compound verbs the noun comes first: کار خواهم کرد "I will work".

## Conditions

| Kind | "If" clause | Main clause | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| possible | اگر + subjunctive | present or imperative | اگر بیایی، خوشحال می‌شوم if you come, I'll be glad |
| once it happens | اگر + simple past | imperative or present | اگر رسیدی، زنگ بزن if (when) you arrive, phone |
| unreal | اگر + past continuous | past continuous | اگر پول داشتم، ماشین می‌خریدم if I had money, I would buy a car |
| unreal, past | اگر + past perfect | past continuous or past perfect | اگر دیده بودم، می‌گفتم if I had seen it, I would have said |

The past continuous is the Persian "would": می‌خریدم is "I used to buy" and "I would buy". کاش *kâsh* "if only" takes the same tenses: کاش اینجا بودی "if only you were here".

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- فردا به شیراز خواهیم رفت. → {{Tomorrow we will go to Shiraz. / We shall go to Shiraz tomorrow.}}
- اگر وقت داشتم، می‌آمدم. → {{If I had time, I would come.}}
- اگر رسیدی، به من زنگ بزن. → {{When you arrive, call me. / If you arrive, phone me.}}
- کاش اینجا بودی. → {{If only you were here. / I wish you were here.}}
:::

::: summary
- Future: خواهم، خواهی، خواهد… + short past stem: خواهم رفت; speech uses the present.
- اگر + subjunctive (possible), + simple past (once it happens), + past continuous (unreal).
- The past continuous is "would": می‌خریدم; کاش "if only".
:::
