# 14. Questions and relative clauses

A Persian question keeps the order of a statement; the question word usually stands where the answer would. The relative clause is just as simple: one word, که *ke*, after a noun marked with ـی *-i*: مردی که دیدم *mardi ke didam* "the man that I saw".

## Question words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| چه *che*, چی *chi* | what | چه می‌خواهید؟ what do you want? |
| کی *ki* | who | کی آمد؟ who came? |
| کی *key*, چه وقت | when | کی می‌آیی؟ when are you coming? |
| کجا *kojâ* | where | کجا زندگی می‌کنید؟ where do you live? |
| چرا *cherâ* | why | چرا نیامدی؟ why didn't you come? |
| چطور *chetor* | how | چطور رفتید؟ how did you go? |
| چند *chand* | how many, how much | چند سال دارید؟ how old are you? |
| کدام *kodâm* | which | کدام کتاب؟ which book? |
| آیا *âyâ* | (yes/no question, formal) | آیا او را می‌شناسید؟ do you know him? |

کی is written the same for "who" (*ki*) and "when" (*key*); the context decides. Answers: بله *bale* (spoken آره *âre*) "yes", نه *na* "no".

## Relative clauses

| Persian | Meaning |
|---|---|
| مردی که دیدم | the man (that) I saw |
| کتابی که خواندی | the book you read |
| شهری که در آن زندگی می‌کنیم | the city we live in (in which we live) |
| دوستی که با او رفتم | the friend I went with |

The noun takes ـی *-i* (after a vowel ـیی or ‌ای) and که follows. When the noun is not the subject or object of the clause, a pronoun repeats it inside: در آن "in it", با او "with him".

که also introduces what is said, known or thought: می‌دانم که خسته‌ای "I know (that) you are tired".

::: exercise relative
Which relative form?

- "the man I saw" → ((مرد که دیدم / مردی که دیدم*))
- "the city we live in" → ((شهری که در آن زندگی می‌کنیم* / شهری که زندگی می‌کنیم آن))
- "I know that…" → ((می‌دانم که* / می‌دانم چه))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- کجا زندگی می‌کنید؟ → {{Where do you live?}}
- کتابی که خواندی خوب بود؟ → {{Was the book you read good? / Was the book that you read good?}}
- چرا دیر آمدی؟ → {{Why did you come late? / Why are you late?}}
- می‌دانم که خسته‌ای. → {{I know that you are tired. / I know you're tired.}}
:::

::: summary
- چه، کی (who), کی (when), کجا، چرا، چطور، چند، کدام; آیا formal; بله / نه.
- Relative: noun + ی + که + clause: مردی که دیدم; a pronoun repeats it when needed (در آن، با او).
- که also means "that" after know, say, think.
:::
