# 1. The alphabet

Persian is written from right to left in an Arabic alphabet with four letters of its own: پ p, چ ch, ژ zh, گ g. Letters join to the next one inside a word, and most change shape slightly depending on their place. The alphabet looks harder than it is: there are 32 letters, and you will read your first words at the end of this lesson. Persian in this course is written in its script, followed by a transliteration in italics: کتاب *ketâb* "book".

Several letters, taken from Arabic, sound the same in Persian: س ص ث are all *s*; ز ذ ض ظ are all *z*; ت ط are *t*; ه ح are *h*; غ ق are *gh*. Spelling has to be learnt word by word, as in English.

## Joining

Every letter joins to the letter before it (on its right). Seven letters never join to the letter after them: ا د ذ ر ز ژ و. After them a new group of letters begins, even inside a word: دارد *dârad* "he has" is written د ا ر د with four separate letters.

| Letter | Alone | Initial | Medial | Final |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ب | ب | بـ | ـبـ | ـب |
| ه | ه | هـ | ـهـ | ـه |
| ع | ع | عـ | ـعـ | ـع |
| د | د | د | ـد | ـد |

## Reading direction

A line starts on the right. Numbers, though, are written from left to right, as in English.
