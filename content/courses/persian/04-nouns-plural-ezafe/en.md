# 4. Nouns, the plural and the ezafe

Persian nouns are simple: no gender, no cases, no definite article. کتاب is "book" or "the book", and the context decides. What Persian has instead is a small vowel, the **ezafe**, which joins a noun to whatever describes it: کتابِ من *ketâb-e man* "my book", literally "book-of me". It is everywhere, and it is not written.

## The plural

| Ending | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ـها *-hâ* | any noun; the usual plural in speech | کتاب‌ها *ketâb-hâ* books; خانه‌ها *khâne-hâ* houses |
| ـان *-ân* | people and living beings, more formal | دوستان *dustân* friends; مردان *mardân* men |
| ـگان *-gân* | nouns in ـه *-e* for people | بچگان *bachchegân* children (also بچه‌ها) |
| ـیان *-yân* | nouns in ـو *-u* and ـا *-â* | دانشجویان *dâneshjuyân* students |

After a number the noun stays singular: سه کتاب *se ketâb* "three books".

## "A", "one"

یک *yek* "one, a" stands before the noun; the unstressed suffix ـی *-i* after it also means "a, some": یک کتاب *yek ketâb*, کتابی *ketâbi*, or both together, یک کتابی. There is no word for "the".

## The ezafe

| Written | Read | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| کتاب من | *ketâb-e man* | my book |
| کتاب خوب | *ketâb-e khub* | the good book |
| خانه‌ی پدر | *khâne-ye pedar* | the father's house |
| دانشجوی ایرانی | *dâneshju-ye irâni* | the Iranian student |

After a consonant the ezafe is *-e*, unwritten. After a vowel it is *-ye*: written ی after ا and و (دانشجوی), and ـهٔ or ـه‌ی after a final ه (خانه‌ی). It can chain: کتاب خوب دوست من *ketâb-e khub-e dust-e man* "my friend's good book".

::: summary
- No gender, no cases, no "the"; plural ـها (any noun) or ـان (people).
- "a": یک before, ـی after the noun; after numbers the noun is singular.
- Ezafe *-e* / *-ye* joins noun + adjective or possessor: کتاب من ketâb-e man.
:::
