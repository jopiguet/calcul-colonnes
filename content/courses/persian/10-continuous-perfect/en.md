# 10. Continuous and perfect tenses

The simple past says that something happened. Persian has other past tenses for how it happened: over time or repeatedly, with a result that still holds, or before another past event. All are built from the two pieces you know: the prefix می‌ and the past stem, plus the **past participle**, the past stem with ـه *-e*: رفته *rafte* "gone".

## The tenses

| Tense | Built from | رفتن go | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| past continuous | می + past stem + ending | می‌رفتم *miraftam* | I was going, I used to go |
| present perfect | participle + short "to be" | رفته‌ام *rafte-am* | I have gone |
| past perfect | participle + بودن in the past | رفته بودم *rafte budam* | I had gone |
| present progressive | داشتن + present | دارم می‌روم *dâram miravam* | I am going (right now) |
| past progressive | داشتن in the past + past continuous | داشتم می‌رفتم *dâshtam miraftam* | I was (just) going |

## The present perfect

| Person | Form |
|---|---|
| من | رفته‌ام *rafte-am* |
| تو | رفته‌ای *rafte-i* |
| او | رفته است *rafte ast* |
| ما | رفته‌ایم *rafte-im* |
| شما | رفته‌اید *rafte-id* |
| آنها | رفته‌اند *rafte-and* |

The present perfect is used, more than in English, for past events whose effect still counts, and for things one has heard: گویا رفته است "apparently he has left". Negatives: نرفته‌ام "I haven't gone", نمی‌رفتم "I wasn't going", نرفته بودم "I hadn't gone". The progressive with داشتن has no negative: use the plain present or past continuous instead.

::: exercise write
Write in Persian.

- "I used to go" → [[می‌رفتم]]
- "he was writing" → [[می‌نوشت]]
- "I had gone" → [[رفته بودم]]
- "I am going (right now)" → [[دارم می‌روم]]
:::

::: exercise tense
Which tense?

- دیده‌ام → ((simple past / present perfect* / past perfect))
- می‌خواندیم → ((past continuous* / present / present perfect))
- رفته بودند → ((present perfect / past perfect* / past continuous))
- دارد می‌خورد → ((present progressive* / past progressive / simple past))
:::

::: exercise meaning
What does it mean?

- تا حالا ایران را دیده‌ای؟ → ((did you see Iran yesterday? / have you ever seen Iran?* / were you seeing Iran?))
- هر روز به مدرسه می‌رفتم. → ((I went to school one day / I used to go to school every day* / I have gone to school))
- وقتی رسیدم، رفته بود. → ((when I arrived, he had left* / when I arrive, he leaves / when I arrived, he was leaving))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- داشتم غذا می‌خوردم. → {{I was eating. / I was having a meal.}}
- این کتاب را خوانده‌ام. → {{I have read this book.}}
- بچه بودم، در شیراز زندگی می‌کردیم. → {{When I was a child, we lived in Shiraz. / As a child, we used to live in Shiraz.}}
- هنوز نیامده است. → {{He hasn't come yet. / She has not arrived yet.}}
:::

::: summary
- Past participle: past stem + ه: رفته، دیده، خوانده.
- می + past: past continuous; participle + ام، ای، است…: present perfect; participle + بودم: past perfect.
- داشتن + present or past continuous: progressive, "right now"; no negative.
:::
