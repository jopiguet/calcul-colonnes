# 15. The passive and polite speech

Two things that make Persian sound like Persian. The **passive** is built like an English one, with the past participle and شدن *shodan* "to become": نوشته شد *neveshte shod* "it was written". **Polite speech**, تعارف *ta'ârof*, is a whole register: you raise the other person with respectful verbs and lower yourself with humble ones, and you offer and refuse by ritual before accepting.

## The passive

| Tense | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| present | ساخته می‌شود *sâkhte mishavad* | it is (being) built |
| simple past | ساخته شد *sâkhte shod* | it was built |
| present perfect | ساخته شده است *sâkhte shode ast* | it has been built |
| future | ساخته خواهد شد *sâkhte khâhad shod* | it will be built |

Only transitive verbs have a passive. Speech avoids naming the agent; formal writing uses توسط *tavassot-e* "by": این کتاب توسط سعدی نوشته شد "this book was written by Saadi". "They say", می‌گویند, is often preferred to a passive.

## Polite verbs

| Plain | For the other person (raising) | For oneself (humble) |
|---|---|---|
| گفتن say | فرمودن *farmudan*: چه فرمودید؟ what did you say? | عرض کردن *arz kardan*: عرض کردم I said |
| آمدن come | تشریف آوردن *tashrif âvordan*: تشریف بیاورید please come | خدمت رسیدن *khedmat residan*: خدمت می‌رسم I'll come to see you |
| بودن be | تشریف داشتن *tashrif dâshtan*: تشریف دارند he is here | |

## Everyday courtesy

| Persian | Use |
|---|---|
| ببخشید *bebakhshid* | excuse me, sorry |
| خواهش می‌کنم *khâhesh mikonam* | please; you're welcome |
| خسته نباشید *khaste nabâshid* | "may you not be tired": thanks to someone working |
| قابل ندارد *ghâbel nadârad* | "it's not worthy of you": said by a seller or host, a ritual refusal of payment |
| دستتان درد نکند *dastetân dard nakonad* | "may your hand not hurt": thanks for food or a service |

When a shopkeeper says قابل ندارد, you insist and pay; when a host offers food, you refuse once or twice before accepting.

::: summary
- Passive: past participle + شدن: ساخته می‌شود، ساخته شد، ساخته شده است; agent توسط (formal).
- Raising verbs فرمودن، تشریف آوردن; humble عرض کردن، خدمت رسیدن.
- ببخشید، خواهش می‌کنم، خسته نباشید، دستتان درد نکند; قابل ندارد is a ritual offer: insist and pay.
:::
