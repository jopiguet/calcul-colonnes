# 5. Possessive suffixes and comparison

"My book" can be said two ways: with the ezafe and the pronoun, کتاب من *ketâb-e man*, or with a short suffix, کتابم *ketâbam*. The suffix is the everyday form. Adjectives compare with endings, as in English: بزرگ *bozorg* "big", بزرگتر *bozorgtar* "bigger", بزرگترین *bozorgtarin* "biggest".

## Possessive suffixes

| Person | Suffix | کتاب book | خانه house |
|---|---|---|---|
| my | ـم *-am* | کتابم *ketâbam* | خانه‌ام *khâne-am* |
| your (sg.) | ـت *-at* | کتابت *ketâbat* | خانه‌ات *khâne-at* |
| his, her | ـش *-ash* | کتابش *ketâbash* | خانه‌اش *khâne-ash* |
| our | ـمان *-emân* | کتابمان *ketâbemân* | خانه‌مان *khâne-mân* |
| your (pl.) | ـتان *-etân* | کتابتان *ketâbetân* | خانه‌تان *khâne-tân* |
| their | ـشان *-eshân* | کتابشان *ketâbeshân* | خانه‌شان *khâne-shân* |

After a final ه, the singular suffixes take ا: خانه‌ام. The suffix comes after an adjective joined by the ezafe: کتاب خوبم *ketâb-e khubam* "my good book".

## Comparison

| Degree | Ending | Example |
|---|---|---|
| comparative | ـتر *-tar* | بزرگتر *bozorgtar* bigger |
| superlative | ـترین *-tarin* | بزرگترین *bozorgtarin* biggest |
| irregular | | خوب → بهتر *behtar*, بهترین *behtarin* |

"Than" is از *az*: تهران از شیراز بزرگتر است *Tehrân az Shirâz bozorgtar ast* "Tehran is bigger than Shiraz". The comparative follows the noun with the ezafe; the superlative stands **before** the noun, without it: بزرگترین شهر *bozorgtarin shahr* "the biggest city".

::: summary
- Possessive suffixes ـم ـت ـش ـمان ـتان ـشان; after ه: خانه‌ام.
- ـتر comparative, ـترین superlative (before the noun, no ezafe); خوب بهتر بهترین.
- "than" is از: از شیراز بزرگتر.
:::
