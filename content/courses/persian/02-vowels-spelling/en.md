# 2. Vowels and spelling

Persian has six vowels: three long ones, *â*, *i*, *u*, which are written, and three short ones, *a*, *e*, *o*, which usually are not. دختر is *dokhtar* "girl": the *o* and the *a* are not on the page, and you read them because you know the word. This is the real difficulty of the script, and it shrinks quickly: the vocabulary list of every lesson gives the pronunciation.

## The vowels

| Vowel | Written | At the start of a word | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| â (long, as in *father*) | ا | آ | آب *âb* water; بابا *bâbâ* dad |
| i (long) | ی | ای | ایران *Irân*; سیب *sib* apple |
| u (long) | و | او | دوست *dust* friend; او *u* he, she |
| a (short) | not written | ا | اسب *asb* horse; شب *shab* night |
| e (short) | not written; ه at the end | ا | اسم *esm* name; خانه *khâne* house |
| o (short) | not written; sometimes و | ا | امید *omid* hope; تو *to* you |

In schoolbooks and poetry the short vowels can be marked: a small stroke above for *a* (َ), below for *e* (ِ), a small و above for *o* (ُ). Everyday texts leave them out.

## Spelling notes

| Sign | Name | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| ‌ | half-space | separates parts of a word without a space | کتاب‌ها *ketâb-hâ* books; می‌روم *miravam* I go |
| ّ | tashdid | doubles a consonant | اوّل *avval* first |
| ء | hamze | a catch in the voice | مسئله *mas'ale* problem |
| خوا | silent و | *khâ*: the و is not read | خواهر *khâhar* sister |

The final ه of خانه is not pronounced *h*: it carries the vowel *-e*. In ماه *mâh* "moon" it is a real *h*.

::: summary
- Long vowels written: â ا (آ at the start), i ی, u و; short a, e, o not written.
- A word-initial vowel is carried by ا: اسب asb, اسم esm, امید omid; final -e is written ه.
- Half-space ‌ (ketâb‌hâ, mi‌ravam), tashdid ّ, silent و in خوا.
:::
