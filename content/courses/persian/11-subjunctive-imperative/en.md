# 11. The subjunctive, the imperative and modal verbs

"I want **to go**", "you must **come**", "maybe he **knows**": Persian uses a special form here, the **subjunctive**, made with the prefix بـ *be-* in place of می‌: بروم *beravam* "that I go". The imperative is the same form without an ending: برو *boro* "go!". With these two, Persian expresses wishes, obligations, possibilities and commands.

## The present subjunctive

| Person | رفتن go (رو) | کردن do (کن) | خوردن eat (خور) |
|---|---|---|---|
| من | بروم *beravam* | بکنم *bokonam* | بخورم *bokhoram* |
| تو | بروی *beravi* | بکنی *bokoni* | بخوری *bokhori* |
| او | برود *beravad* | بکند *bokonad* | بخورد *bokhorad* |
| ما | برویم *beravim* | بکنیم *bokonim* | بخوریم *bokhorim* |
| شما | بروید *beravid* | بکنید *bokonid* | بخورید *bokhorid* |
| آنها | بروند *beravand* | بکنند *bokonand* | بخورند *bokhorand* |

بـ is pronounced *bo-* before a stem with *o*. The negative replaces it with نـ: نروم *naravam*. Compound verbs usually drop it: کار کنم *kâr konam*. بودن has باشم *bâsham*, داشتن has داشته باشم.

## Where it is used

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| می‌خواهم | I want | می‌خواهم بروم I want to go |
| باید *bâyad* | must | باید بروم I must go |
| می‌توانم *mitavânam* | I can | می‌توانم بیایم I can come |
| شاید *shâyad* | maybe | شاید بداند maybe he knows |
| که *ke* | so that | آمدم که ببینم I came to see |
| بیا | let's (with 1st pl.) | بیا برویم let's go |

باید and شاید do not change for person; the subjunctive after them does.

## The imperative

| Verb | Singular | Plural, polite | Negative |
|---|---|---|---|
| رفتن | برو *boro* | بروید *beravid* | نرو *naro* |
| آمدن | بیا *biyâ* | بیایید *biyâyid* | نیا *nayâ* |
| نوشتن | بنویس *benevis* | بنویسید *benevisid* | ننویس *nanevis* |
| نشستن | بنشین *beneshin* | بنشینید *beneshinid* | ننشین *naneshin* |

بفرمایید *befarmâyid* "please, go ahead, help yourself" is the polite imperative heard everywhere.

- شاید … "he knows" → ((می‌داند / بداند*))

::: exercise meaning
What does it mean?

- بفرمایید. → ((please, go ahead* / thank you / goodbye))
- بیا برویم. → ((come and go / let's go* / he came and went))
- نمی‌توانم بیایم. → ((I can't come* / I don't want to come / I didn't come))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- می‌خواهم ایران را ببینم. → {{I want to see Iran.}}
- باید کار کنیم. → {{We must work. / We have to work.}}
- لطفاً بنشینید. → {{Please sit down. / Please have a seat.}}
- شاید فردا بیاید. → {{Maybe he will come tomorrow. / Perhaps she comes tomorrow.}}
:::

::: summary
- Subjunctive: بـ + present stem + endings: بروم، بکنم؛ negative نروم؛ باشم.
- After خواستن، باید، توانستن، شاید، که.
- Imperative: برو، بیا، بنویس; plural -id; negative نرو؛ بفرمایید.
:::
