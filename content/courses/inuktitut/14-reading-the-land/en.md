# 14. Reading: Saali of Iqaluit

The last lesson reads a short text written for this course, in which Saali, a girl from Iqaluit, introduces her family. It uses nearly everything taught so far: endings, cases, postbases, number, "a" and "the". Then it explains two words that every visitor to Nunavut meets: *inuksuk* and *Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit*.

## Saali

ᐊᐃ! ᓵᓕᒥᒃ ᐊᑎᖃᖅᑐᖓ. ᓄᓇᕗᒻᒥᐅᔪᖓ, ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂ ᓇᔪᒐᖃᖅᑐᖓ.

ᐊᓈᓇᒐ ᐊᑖᑕᒐᓗ ᓴᓇᔫᒃ. ᐊᑖᑕᒐ ᓇᑦᓯᕐᓯᐅᖅᑐᖅ. ᐃᒃᐸᒃᓴᖅ ᓯᑯᒥ ᓇᓄᕐᒥᒃ ᑕᑯᓚᐅᖅᑐᖅ!

ᐅᕙᒍᑦ ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂᒃ ᓂᕆᔪᒍᑦ. ᐃᓄᒃᓱᓐᓂᒃ ᓄᓇᒥ ᑕᑯᒍᓐᓇᖅᑐᓯ.

ᐃᓄᒃᑐᑦ ᖃᓪᓗᓈᑐᓪᓗ ᐅᖃᕈᓐᓇᖅᑐᖓ. ᑕᕝᕙᐅᕗᓯ!

## The words of the text

| Inuktitut | Pieces | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ᓄᓇᕗᒻᒥᐅᔪᖓ | nunavut-miu-junga | I am from Nunavut |
| ᓇᔪᒐᖃᖅᑐᖓ | najuga-qaq-tunga | I live (have a dwelling) |
| ᓴᓇᔫᒃ | sana-juuk | they two work |
| ᓇᑦᓯᕐᓯᐅᖅᑐᖅ | natsiq-siuq-tuq | he is hunting seals |
| ᓯᑯᒥ | siku-mi | on the sea ice |
| ᓇᓄᕐᒥᒃ ᑕᑯᓚᐅᖅᑐᖅ | nanuq-mik taku-lauq-tuq | he saw a polar bear |
| ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂᒃ | iqaluit-nik | fish (some) |
| ᐃᓄᒃᓱᓐᓂᒃ | inuksuit-nik | inuksuit (some) |
| ᑕᑯᒍᓐᓇᖅᑐᓯ | taku-gunnaq-tusi | you all can see |

*-nik* is the plural of *-mik*: "some fish", "some inuksuit".

## Inuksuk and Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit

An ᐃᓄᒃᓱᒃ *inuksuk*, plural ᐃᓄᒃᓱᐃᑦ *inuksuit*, is a figure of piled stones, "something that acts as a person": it marks a route, a good fishing place, a place to remember. One stands on Nunavut's flag.

ᐃᓄᐃᑦ ᖃᐅᔨᒪᔭᑐᖃᖏᑦ *inuit qaujimajatuqangit*, "what Inuit have long known", is the name of Inuit traditional knowledge: of land, ice, animals and living together. The Government of Nunavut has made its principles part of how it works; among them, ᐋᔩᖃᑎᒌᓐᓂᖅ *aajiiqatigiinniq*, deciding through discussion, and ᐱᓕᕆᖃᑎᒌᓐᓂᖅ *piliriqatigiinniq*, working together for a common cause.

::: summary
- The text uses endings (*-junga*, *-juuk*), cases (*-mi*, *-mik*, *-nik*), postbases (*-siuq-*, *-lauq-*, *-gunnaq-*) and *-lu*.
- *-nik* is the plural of *-mik*: ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂᒃ *iqalunnik* "some fish".
- ᐃᓄᒃᓱᒃ *inuksuk*, piled stones that act as a person; ᐃᓄᐃᑦ ᖃᐅᔨᒪᔭᑐᖃᖏᑦ *inuit qaujimajatuqangit*, Inuit traditional knowledge.
:::
