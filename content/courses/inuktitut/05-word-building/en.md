# 5. One word, one sentence

An Inuktitut word is built from a base, any number of postbases, and an ending, in that order. What English says in a sentence, Inuktitut often says in one word: ᐃᒡᓗᓕᐅᖅᑐᖓ *igluliuqtunga*, "I am building a house". This lesson takes words apart and puts them together.

## Base, postbase, ending

| Word | Base | Postbase | Ending | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ᐃᒡᓗᓕᐅᖅᑐᖓ | iglu house | -liuq- make | -tunga I | I am building a house |
| ᑐᒃᑐᓯᐅᖅᑐᖓ | tuktu caribou | -siuq- look for | -tunga I | I am hunting caribou |
| ᕿᒻᒥᖃᖅᑐᖓ | qimmiq dog | -qaq- have | -tunga I | I have a dog |
| ᐊᑎᖃᖅᑐᖓ | atiq name | -qaq- have | -tunga I | I have a name |

- The base comes first and gives the subject of talk: a thing or an action.
- Postbases add meaning, one after another; there are several hundred.
- The ending comes last and says who: *-tunga* "I", *-tuq* "he, she".
- Sounds meet and change at the joins: *qimmiq* + *-qaq-* drops a *q*, *qimmiqaq-*; *qimmiq* + *-siuq-* turns it to *r*, *qimmirsiuq-*.

::: exercise parts
What does the postbase add?

- ᐃᒡᓗᓕᐅᖅᑐᖓ: -liuq- → ((make* / have / look for))
- ᑐᒃᑐᓯᐅᖅᑐᖓ: -siuq- → ((make / have / look for*))
- ᕿᒻᒥᖃᖅᑐᖓ: -qaq- → ((make / have* / look for))
- -tunga → ((I* / you / he, she))
:::

## Postbases on nouns

Some postbases make new nouns: *-jjuaq* "big", *-apik* "small, dear", *-miut* "the people of".

| Word | Pieces | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ᐃᒡᓗᔾᔪᐊᖅ | iglu-jjuaq | a big building |
| ᕿᒻᒥᐊᐱᒃ | qimmi-apik | a little dog, a puppy |
| ᓄᓇᕗᒻᒥᐅᑦ | nunavut-miut | the people of Nunavut |

## A famous long word

ᑐᓵᑦᓯᐊᕈᓐᓇᙱᑦᑐᐊᓘᔪᖓ *tusaatsiarunnanngittualuujunga* "I really cannot hear very well":

| Piece | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tusaa- | hear |
| -tsiaq- | well |
| -junnaq- | be able |
| -nngit- | not |
| -tualuu- | very much |
| -junga | I |

The pieces join with changes of sound: *-tsiaq-* and *-junnaq-* meet as *-tsiarunnaq-*.

::: exercise long-word
Which piece means it?

- not → ((-tsiaq- / -nngit-* / -junga))
- well → ((-tsiaq-* / -tualuu- / tusaa-))
- hear → ((-junnaq- / tusaa-* / -nngit-))
- I → ((-junga* / -tsiaq- / -tualuu-))
:::

::: summary
- A word is a base, postbases, an ending: *iglu-liuq-tunga*.
- Postbases: *-liuq-* make, *-siuq-* look for, *-qaq-* have, *-jjuaq* big, *-apik* small, *-miut* people of.
- Sounds change at the joins: *qimmiq-qaq-* is *qimmiqaq-*, *qimmiq-siuq-* is *qimmirsiuq-*.
- ᑐᓵᑦᓯᐊᕈᓐᓇᙱᑦᑐᐊᓘᔪᖓ *tusaatsiarunnanngittualuujunga* "I really cannot hear very well".
:::
