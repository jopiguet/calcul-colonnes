# 11. Want, can, well, and

Many meanings that English gives to separate words, "want", "can", "well", are postbases in Inuktitut, set inside the verb and combined freely. This lesson gives three of the most used, how they stack with *-nngit-*, and the little ending *-lu* "and".

## Three postbases

| Postbase | Meaning | Example | |
|---|---|---|---|
| -juma- | want to | ᓂᕆᔪᒪᔪᖓ | I want to eat |
| -gunnaq- | be able to | ᓂᕆᒍᓐᓇᖅᑐᖓ | I can eat |
| -tsiaq- | well, nicely | ᑕᑯᑦᓯᐊᖅᑐᖓ | I see well |

- After a vowel, *-juma-* and *-gunnaq-* join directly: *niri-juma-*, *ani-gunnaq-*.
- After *q* they begin with *r*: *qimmiqaq-* "have a dog" with *-juma-* is ᕿᒻᒥᖃᕈᒪᔪᖓ *qimmiqarumajunga* "I want a dog"; *uqaq-* "speak" with *-gunnaq-* is ᐅᖃᕈᓐᓇᖅᑐᖓ *uqarunnaqtunga* "I can speak".
- *-juma-* ends in a vowel, so the ending is *-junga*; *-gunnaq-* and *-tsiaq-* end in *q*, so it is *-tunga*.

- I want to see → [[ᑕᑯᔪᒪᔪᖓ]]

## Stacking

Postbases follow each other in the order of meaning, each changing what comes before it.

| Word | Pieces | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ᓂᕆᔪᒪᙱᑦᑐᖓ | niri-juma-nngit-tunga | I don't want to eat |
| ᑕᑯᔪᒪᕕᑦ | taku-juma-vit | do you want to see? |
| ᑕᑯᒍᓐᓇᙱᑦᑐᖓ | taku-gunna-nngit-tunga | I can't see |
| ᑕᑯᔪᒪᓚᐅᖅᑐᖓ | taku-juma-lauq-tunga | I wanted to see |

*-gunnaq-* loses its *q* before *-nngit-*, as bases do.

- I can't see → ((ᑕᑯᒍᓐᓇᙱᑦᑐᖓ* / ᑕᑯᙱᑦᒍᓐᓇᖅᑐᖓ))
- I wanted to see → ((ᑕᑯᔪᒪᓚᐅᖅᑐᖓ* / ᑕᑯᓚᐅᖅᔪᒪᔪᖓ))
:::

## And: -lu

*-lu* "and" goes on the second word, or on both: ᐊᓈᓇᒐ ᐊᑖᑕᒐᓗ *anaanaga ataatagalu* "my mother and my father"; ᐃᓄᒃᑐᑦ ᖃᓪᓗᓈᑐᓪᓗ *inuktut qallunaatullu* "in Inuktut and in English". A *t* before it becomes *l*: *qallunaatut* gives *qallunaatullu*.

::: exercise lu
Write it with -lu.

- my mother and my father → [[ᐊᓈᓇᒐ ᐊᑖᑕᒐᓗ]]
- dogs and people → [[ᕿᒻᒦᑦ ᐃᓄᐃᓪᓗ]]
- houses and sleds → [[ᐃᒡᓗᑦ ᖃᒧᑏᓪᓗ]]
- in Inuktut and in English → [[ᐃᓄᒃᑐᑦ ᖃᓪᓗᓈᑐᓪᓗ]]
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- ᓂᕆᔪᒪᕕᑦ → {{Do you want to eat?}}
- ᐃᓄᒃᑐᑦ ᐅᖃᕈᓐᓇᖅᑐᖓ → {{I can speak Inuktut. / I am able to speak Inuktut.}}
- ᑕᑯᑦᓯᐊᖅᑐᖓ → {{I see well. / I can see well.}}
- ᐊᓂᔪᒪᙱᑦᑐᖓ → {{I don't want to go out. / I do not want to go out.}}
:::

::: summary
- *-juma-* want, *-gunnaq-* can, *-tsiaq-* well, inside the verb.
- They stack in the order of meaning: *niri-juma-nngit-tunga* "I don't want to eat".
- *-lu* "and" on the last word: ᐊᓈᓇᒐ ᐊᑖᑕᒐᓗ *anaanaga ataatagalu*.
:::
