# 9. In, to, from, through, like

Where English uses prepositions, Inuktitut adds a case ending to the noun: ᐃᒡᓗᒥ *iglumi* "in the house", ᐃᒡᓗᒧᑦ *iglumut* "to the house". The endings meet the noun's last sound and change it, which is why the capital, *Iqaluit*, becomes *Iqalunni* "in Iqaluit". This lesson gives five cases.

## Five cases

| Ending | Meaning | Example | |
|---|---|---|---|
| -mi | in, at, on | ᐃᒡᓗᒥ | in the house |
| -mut | to, towards | ᐃᒡᓗᒧᑦ | to the house |
| -mit | from | ᐃᒡᓗᒥᑦ | from the house |
| -kkut | through, by way of | ᐃᒐᓛᒃᑯᑦ | through the window |
| -tut | like, in the manner of | ᐃᓄᒃᑐᑦ | like the Inuit, in Inuktut |

- ᖃᓪᓗᓈᑐᑦ *qallunaatut* "like the *qallunaat*", the people from the south, is "in English".
- *Iqaluit* is the plural of *iqaluk* "fish": "the fish", for the Arctic char caught there.

::: exercise which-case
Which case fits?

- I walk to the house: ᐃᒡᓗ… → ((-mi / -mut* / -mit))
- I work in the house: ᐃᒡᓗ… → ((-mi* / -mut / -kkut))
- I come from the house: ᐃᒡᓗ… → ((-mut / -mit* / -tut))
- I speak like the Inuit: ᐃᓄᒃ… → ((-mi / -kkut / -tut*))
:::

## When the sounds meet

| Last sound | With -mi | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| a vowel | -mi | ᓄᓇᒥ | on the land |
| t | -nni | ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂ | in Iqaluit |
| q | -rmi | ᐃᒪᕐᒥ | in the water |
| k | -ngmi | ᖃᒧᑎᖕᒥ | on the sled |

The same changes come with *-mut* and *-mit*: ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓄᑦ *iqalunnut* "to Iqaluit", ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂᑦ *iqalunnit* "from Iqaluit".

::: exercise meet
Which form is right?

- to Iqaluit → ((ᐃᖃᓗᐃᑦᒧᑦ / ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓄᑦ* / ᐃᖃᓗᒻᒧᑦ))
- in the water (imaq) → ((ᐃᒪᖅᒥ / ᐃᒪᕐᒥ* / ᐃᒪᒥ))
- on the sled (qamutik) → ((ᖃᒧᑎᒃᒥ / ᖃᒧᑎᖕᒥ* / ᖃᒧᑎᒥ))
- on the land → ((ᓄᓇᒥ* / ᓄᓇᓐᓂ / ᓄᓇᕐᒥ))
:::

## In a sentence

| Inuktitut | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ᐃᒡᓗᒥ ᓴᓇᔪᖓ | I work in the house. |
| ᐃᒡᓗᒧᑦ ᐱᓱᒃᑐᖓ | I walk to the house. |
| ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂᑦ ᑎᑭᑦᑐᖓ | I arrive from Iqaluit. |
| ᐃᓄᒃᑐᑦ ᐅᖃᖅᑐᖓ | I speak Inuktut. |

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂ ᓴᓇᔪᖓ → {{I work in Iqaluit.}}
- ᐃᒡᓗᒧᑦ ᐱᓱᒃᑐᖅ → {{He walks to the house. / She walks to the house. / She is walking home.}}
- ᖃᓪᓗᓈᑐᑦ ᐅᖃᖅᑐᑎᑦ → {{You speak English.}}
- ᓄᓇᒥ ᐱᓱᒃᑐᒍᑦ → {{We walk on the land. / We are walking on the land.}}
:::

::: summary
- *-mi* in, *-mut* to, *-mit* from, *-kkut* through, *-tut* like.
- The noun's last sound changes: *Iqaluit*, ᐃᖃᓗᓐᓂ *iqalunni*; *imaq*, ᐃᒪᕐᒥ *imarmi*; *qamutik*, ᖃᒧᑎᖕᒥ *qamutingmi*.
- ᐃᓄᒃᑐᑦ *inuktut* in Inuktut, ᖃᓪᓗᓈᑐᑦ *qallunaatut* in English.
:::
