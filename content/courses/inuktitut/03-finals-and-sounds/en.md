# 3. Long sounds, double sounds

Inuktitut tells words apart by length: a vowel or a consonant held twice as long is a different sound. Syllabics show a long vowel with a dot and a long consonant with a final before the syllable. This lesson reads and writes both, with the two combinations that are spelt in a special way.

## Long vowels

A long vowel lasts about twice as long. Syllabics put a dot over the syllable; Roman spelling writes the vowel twice.

| Short | Long | Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ᓇ *na* | ᓈ *naa* | ᐊᓈᓇ *anaana* | mother |
| ᑕ *ta* | ᑖ *taa* | ᐊᑖᑕ *ataata* | father |
| ᓴ *sa* | ᓵ *saa* | ᑐᓵᔪᖓ *tusaajunga* | I hear |
| ᐃ *i* | ᐄ *ii* | ᐄ *ii* | yes |

::: exercise long
Long or short?

- ᒪ → ((short* / long))
- ᒫ → ((short / long*))
- ᑏ → ((short / long*))
- ᑯ → ((short* / long))
:::

## Double consonants

A doubled consonant is held: *ll* in ᐅᓪᓛᒃᑯᑦ *ullaakkut* "good morning". Syllabics write the first half as a final: ᓪ then ᓛ.

| Roman | Syllabics | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| qimmiq | ᕿᒻᒥᖅ | dog |
| ullaakkut | ᐅᓪᓛᒃᑯᑦ | good morning |
| ilinniaqtunga | ᐃᓕᓐᓂᐊᖅᑐᖓ | I study |
| unnusakkut | ᐅᓐᓄᓴᒃᑯᑦ | good afternoon |

Two doubles are spelt differently:

- *qq* is written with the final *q* and the *k* row: *taqqiq* "moon, month" is ᑕᖅᑭᖅ, ᖅ ᑭ.
- *nng* has a row of its own, a small ᓐ joined to the *ng* shape: ᙱ *nngi*, as in ᖃᓄᐃᙱᑦᑐᖓ *qanuinngittunga* "I am fine".

::: exercise write
Write in syllabics or in Roman spelling.

- mother → [[ᐊᓈᓇ]]
- father → [[ᐊᑖᑕ]]
- moon, month → [[ᑕᖅᑭᖅ]]
- good morning → [[ᐅᓪᓛᒃᑯᑦ]]
:::

::: exercise doubles

- ᑕᖅᑭᖅ → ((takkiq / taqqiq* / taqiq))

:::

## Vowels side by side

Two vowels may follow each other, each with its own syllable: *ai* "hello" is ᐊᐃ, *a-i*; *Iqaluit*, the capital of Nunavut, is ᐃᖃᓗᐃᑦ, *i-qa-lu-i-t*. Nunavik writes *ai* with a single sign, ᐁ; Nunavut writes two.

::: exercise vowels
How many syllables?

- ᐊᐃ → ((one / two*))
- ᐃᖃᓗᐃᑦ → ((three / four* / five))
- ᐃᓄᐃᑦ → ((two / three* / four))
- ᐃᒡᓗ → ((two* / three))
:::

::: summary
- A dot makes a long vowel: ᑕ *ta*, ᑖ *taa*; Roman writes *aa*.
- A doubled consonant is a final then a syllable: ᕿᒻᒥᖅ *qimmiq*.
- *qq* is ᖅ with the *k* row: ᑕᖅᑭᖅ; *nng* has its own row: ᙱ.
- Two vowels side by side are two syllables: ᐊᐃ, ᐃᖃᓗᐃᑦ.
:::
