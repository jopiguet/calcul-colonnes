# 10. Earlier today, before today, later

Inuktitut can leave time unsaid, as the endings of lesson 6 do. When it says it, it uses postbases that also tell how far away the time is: earlier today or before today, later on. This lesson gives three of them and the words for yesterday, today and tomorrow.

## Three time postbases

| Postbase | Time | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -qqau- | earlier today | ᓂᕆᖅᑲᐅᔪᖓ | I ate (this morning) |
| -lauq- | before today | ᓂᕆᓚᐅᖅᑐᖓ | I ate (yesterday or before) |
| -niaq- | later, to come | ᓂᕆᓂᐊᖅᑐᖓ | I will eat |

- The postbase comes between the base and the ending: *niri-lauq-tunga*.
- *-qqau-* ends in a vowel, so the ending starts with *j*: ᓂᕆᖅᑲᐅᔪᖓ *niriqqaujunga*; *-lauq-* and *-niaq-* end in *q*, so it starts with *t*.
- Communities differ in the details; some say -ᓛᖅ- *-laaq-* for "tomorrow or later". This lesson keeps to common forms.

## Words of time

| Inuktitut | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ᐃᒃᐸᒃᓴᖅ | yesterday |
| ᐅᓪᓗᒥ | today |
| ᖃᐅᑉᐸᑦ | tomorrow |
| ᐅᓪᓛᒃᑯᑦ | in the morning |
| ᐅᓐᓄᐊᒥ | at night |

ᐅᓪᓗᒥ *ullumi* is *ulluq* "day" with *-mi* "in": "on this day".

::: exercise when
Which postbase fits?

- ᐃᒃᐸᒃᓴᖅ … I saw → ((-qqau- / -lauq-* / -niaq-))
- ᖃᐅᑉᐸᑦ … I will work → ((-qqau- / -lauq- / -niaq-*))
- This morning I ate, now it is noon → ((-qqau-* / -lauq- / -niaq-))
- Last year we lived there → ((-qqau- / -lauq-* / -niaq-))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- ᐃᒃᐸᒃᓴᖅ ᑕᑯᓚᐅᖅᑐᖓ → {{I saw it yesterday. / Yesterday I saw.}}
- ᖃᐅᑉᐸᑦ ᓴᓇᓂᐊᖅᑐᖓ → {{Tomorrow I will work. / I will work tomorrow.}}
- ᓂᕆᖅᑲᐅᔪᖅ → {{She ate. / He ate. / She ate earlier today.}}
- ᐅᓪᓗᒥ ᐊᓂᓂᐊᖅᑐᒍᑦ → {{Today we will go out. / We will go out today.}}
:::

::: summary
- *-qqau-* earlier today, *-lauq-* before today, *-niaq-* later: ᓂᕆᖅᑲᐅᔪᖓ *niriqqaujunga*, ᓂᕆᓚᐅᖅᑐᖓ *nirilauqtunga*, ᓂᕆᓂᐊᖅᑐᖓ *niriniaqtunga*.
- The postbase sits between base and ending.
- ᐃᒃᐸᒃᓴᖅ *ikpaksaq* yesterday, ᐅᓪᓗᒥ *ullumi* today, ᖃᐅᑉᐸᑦ *qauppat* tomorrow, ᐅᓐᓄᐊᒥ *unnuami* at night.
:::
