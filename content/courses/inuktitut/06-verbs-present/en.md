# 6. Verbs: who does it

An Inuktitut verb is a base with an ending that names the person: ᓂᕆᔪᖓ *nirijunga* "I eat", ᓂᕆᔪᖅ *nirijuq* "she eats". The endings count one, two and many, like the pronouns. This lesson gives the endings of the everyday present.

## The endings

| Person | After a vowel: niri- eat | After a consonant: pisuk- walk |
|---|---|---|
| I | ᓂᕆᔪᖓ | ᐱᓱᒃᑐᖓ |
| you | ᓂᕆᔪᑎᑦ | ᐱᓱᒃᑐᑎᑦ |
| he, she | ᓂᕆᔪᖅ | ᐱᓱᒃᑐᖅ |
| we two | ᓂᕆᔪᒍᒃ | ᐱᓱᒃᑐᒍᒃ |
| they two | ᓂᕆᔫᒃ | ᐱᓱᒃᑑᒃ |
| we | ᓂᕆᔪᒍᑦ | ᐱᓱᒃᑐᒍᑦ |
| you all | ᓂᕆᔪᓯ | ᐱᓱᒃᑐᓯ |
| they | ᓂᕆᔪᑦ | ᐱᓱᒃᑐᑦ |

- After a vowel the ending starts with *j*; after a consonant, with *t*.
- The tense is not marked: ᓂᕆᔪᖓ *nirijunga* is "I eat" or "I am eating".
- Some bases change their last sound before *t*: *sinik-* "sleep", ᓯᓂᑦᑐᖓ *sinittunga* "I sleep".
- Writing also uses a second set of endings, ᓂᕆᕗᖓ *nirivunga*, ᓂᕆᕗᑎᑦ *nirivutit*, ᓂᕆᕗᖅ *nirivuq*, with the same meaning.

## Everyday verbs

| Base | Meaning | I … |
|---|---|---|
| niri- | eat | ᓂᕆᔪᖓ |
| imiq- | drink | ᐃᒥᖅᑐᖓ |
| taku- | see | ᑕᑯᔪᖓ |
| uqaq- | speak | ᐅᖃᖅᑐᖓ |
| ilinniaq- | study, learn | ᐃᓕᓐᓂᐊᖅᑐᖓ |
| ani- | go out | ᐊᓂᔪᖓ |
| isiq- | come in | ᐃᓯᖅᑐᖓ |
| sana- | work, make | ᓴᓇᔪᖓ |

::: exercise who
Who does it?

- ᐃᒥᖅᑐᑎᑦ → ((I / you* / they))
- ᑕᑯᔫᒃ → ((they two* / we two / you all))
- ᐅᖃᖅᑐᓯ → ((we / you all* / they))
- ᐊᓂᔪᒍᒃ → ((we two* / they two / we))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- ᐃᓕᓐᓂᐊᖅᑐᒍᑦ → {{We study. / We are studying. / We learn. / We are learning.}}

:::

::: summary
- Endings: *-junga, -jutit, -juq*; two: *-juguk, -juuk*; many: *-jugut, -jusi, -jut*.
- After a consonant they start with *t*: ᐱᓱᒃᑐᖓ *pisuktunga*.
- No tense: ᓂᕆᔪᖓ *nirijunga* "I eat, I am eating".
- Verbs: *niri-* eat, *imiq-* drink, *taku-* see, *uqaq-* speak, *ilinniaq-* study, *ani-* go out, *isiq-* come in, *sana-* work.
:::
