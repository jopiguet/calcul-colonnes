# 10. Nominal and adjectival sentences

Lesson 9 built sentences that say **where** something is. Two more kinds of sentence without a verb say **what** something is and **how** it is. In a **nominal sentence** the predicate is a noun: "he is my son". In an **adjectival sentence** the predicate is an adjective, and it comes **first**: "good is he", meaning "he is good". With the adverbial sentence, these three cover every "to be" of English.

## The nominal sentence

| Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| independent pronoun + noun | *jnk sꜣ.k* | I am your son |
| noun + *pw* | *sꜣ.j pw* | it is my son; this is my son |
| noun + *pw* + noun | *nṯr pw rꜥ* | Ra is a god (literally "a god, that is Ra") |

The little word *pw* 𓊪𓅱 "this" works as a copula: it points back to what comes before it. In *A pw B*, *A* is the predicate and *B* the subject: what is said about *B* comes first.

A question asks with *pw* too: *ptr pw?* "what is it?", *nm pw?* "who is it?".

## The adjectival sentence

The adjective stands first, unchanged (no agreement), followed by its subject:

| Subject | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| a noun | *nfr nswt* | the king is good |
| a noun with a suffix | *wr ḥqꜣ.n* | our ruler is great |
| a dependent pronoun | *nfr sw* | he is good |
| a dependent pronoun | *ꜥꜣ sj* | she is great |

The exclamation "how…!" adds *wj* to the adjective: *nfr wj* "how beautiful!", *nfr wj ḥr.k* "how beautiful is your face!".

## Three sentences, three questions

| Question | Sentence | Predicate | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| where? | adverbial | prepositional phrase or adverb | *jw.f m pr* he is in the house |
| what? | nominal | noun | *jnk sꜣ.k* I am your son |
| how? | adjectival | adjective, first | *nfr sw* he is good |

::: summary
- Nominal: *jnk* + noun; noun + *pw* "it is …"; *A pw B* "B is A".
- Adjectival: adjective first, invariable, then the subject: *nfr sw*, *wr nswt*. *nfr wj* how beautiful!
- Where: adverbial; what: nominal; how: adjectival. *r* after an adjective: "more than" (*wr r*).
:::
