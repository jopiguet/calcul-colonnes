# 19. Royal names and titles

A king of Egypt had five names. Two of them, written in **cartouches**, are the ones on museum labels: the throne name and the birth name. Each name was a short statement about the reign, and the god Ra, written first out of respect, is the most frequent word in them. This lesson teaches the five-part titulary, how to read a cartouche, and the epithets that surround royal names on monuments.

## The five names

| Name | Introduced by | Meaning | Example (Senusret I) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horus name | 𓅃 *Ḥrw*, in a palace façade (*serekh*) | the king as the falcon god | *ꜥnḫ-mswt* "living of births" |
| Two Ladies name | *nbtj* "the Two Ladies" (vulture and cobra) | under the goddesses of Upper and Lower Egypt | *ꜥnḫ-mswt* |
| Golden Horus name | *Ḥrw nbw* | the king as eternal | — |
| Throne name | 𓇓𓆤 *nswt-bjtj* "king of Upper and Lower Egypt", in a cartouche | taken at accession | *Ḫpr-kꜣ-Rꜥ* "the ka of Ra comes into being" |
| Birth name | 𓅭𓇳 *sꜣ Rꜥ* "son of Ra", in a cartouche | the personal name | *S-n-wsrt* "man of (the goddess) Wosret" |

The cartouche (*šnw*) is a loop of rope, the sign of all that the sun encircles: the king's name ruled the world.

## Reading a cartouche

The name of a god is written first, by honorific transposition, but read in its grammatical place:

| Written | Read | King | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 𓇳𓏠𓆣 | *Mn-ḫpr-Rꜥ* | Thutmose III (throne name) | "the manifestation of Ra endures" |
| 𓇳𓆣𓏥𓎟 | *Nb-ḫprw-Rꜥ* | Tutankhamun (throne name) | "the lord of manifestations is Ra" |
| 𓇋𓏠𓈖𓏏𓅱𓏏𓋹 | *Twt-ꜥnḫ-Jmn* | Tutankhamun (birth name) | "living image of Amun" |
| 𓇳𓄟𓋴𓋴 | *Rꜥ-ms-sw* | Ramesses | "Ra is the one who bore him" |
| 𓐍𓅱𓆑𓅱 | *Ḫwfw* | Khufu, the Great Pyramid | short for "(Khnum) protects me" |

## Epithets

| Epithet | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *nb tꜣwj* | lord of the Two Lands |
| *nb ḫꜥw* | lord of appearances (in glory) |
| *nṯr nfr* | the perfect god (the living king) |
| *dj ꜥnḫ* | given life |
| *ꜥnḫ ḏt* | alive for ever |
| *mrj Jmn* | beloved of Amun |
| *ꜥnḫ wḏꜣ snb* | life, prosperity, health (after "Pharaoh") |

*Pharaoh* comes from *pr-ꜥꜣ* "the great house", the palace, used for the king himself from the New Kingdom on.

::: summary
- Five names: Horus, Two Ladies, Golden Horus, throne name (*nswt-bjtj*, cartouche), birth name (*sꜣ Rꜥ*, cartouche).
- In a name the god is written first and read in place: 𓇳𓏠𓆣 *Mn-ḫpr-Rꜥ*.
- Epithets: *nb tꜣwj*, *nṯr nfr*, *dj ꜥnḫ*, *ꜥnḫ ḏt*, *mrj Jmn*; *pr-ꜥꜣ* the great house, Pharaoh.
