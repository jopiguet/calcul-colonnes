# 20. Symbols, gods and the afterlife

Many hieroglyphs are also **symbols**: the ankh held by the gods, the djed pillar, the eye of Horus worn as an amulet. They are not decoration added to the writing; they are words, and their meaning comes from their reading. This lesson gathers the great symbols, the names of the main gods, the parts of a person that survived death, and the judgement of the dead, so that you can read the scenes as well as the texts.

## The great symbols

| Sign | Reading | Meaning | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 𓋹 | *ꜥnḫ* | life | held by gods to the king's nose: the breath of life |
| 𓊽 | *ḏd* | stability, endurance | the backbone of Osiris; amulets, temple friezes |
| 𓌀 | *wꜣs* | dominion, power | the gods' sceptre |
| 𓂀 | *wḏꜣt* | the sound eye, wholeness | the eye of Horus, torn out by Seth and healed |
| 𓆣 | *ḫpr* | to come into being, become | the scarab rolling the sun as its ball of dung |
| 𓍶 | *šn* | encircling, eternity | the loop of rope; stretched, it is the cartouche |
| 𓎬 | *tjt* | protection | the knot of Isis |
| 𓄥 | *smꜣ* | union | *smꜣ tꜣwj* the union of the Two Lands, on thrones |
| 𓆄 | *mꜣꜥt* | truth, right order | the feather of the goddess Maat |

*ꜥnḫ ḏd wꜣs* "life, stability, dominion" is the standard gift of the gods to the king.

## The crowns and colours

| Sign | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 𓋑 | *ḥḏt* | the white crown of Upper Egypt |
| 𓋔 | *dšrt* | the red crown of Lower Egypt |
| 𓋖 | *sḫmtj* | the double crown, "the two powerful ones" (Greek *pschent*) |
| 𓆗 | *jꜥrt* | the uraeus, the rearing cobra on the king's brow |

Egypt is *kmt* "the black land", the dark silt of the Nile valley; the desert is *dšrt* "the red land". Green, *wꜣḏ*, is the colour of growth and of Osiris reborn.

## The gods

| God | Transliteration | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ra | *Rꜥ* | the sun, creator |
| Amun | *Jmn* | "the hidden one", god of Thebes; with Ra: Amun-Ra |
| Osiris | *Wsjr* | the murdered king, ruler of the dead |
| Isis | *Ꜣst* 𓊨𓏏𓁐 | his wife, the great magician; her sign is the throne |
| Horus | *Ḥrw* | their son, the falcon, the living king |
| Seth | *Stẖ* | Osiris's brother and murderer, god of storm and desert |
| Anubis | *Jnpw* | the jackal, god of embalming |
| Thoth | *Ḏḥwtj* 𓅝 | the ibis, god of writing and of the moon |
| Hathor | *Ḥwt-Ḥr* 𓉡 | "house of Horus", goddess of love, music, the sky |
| Ptah | *Ptḥ* | god of Memphis, of craftsmen |

## What survived death

| Part | Reading | Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ka | *kꜣ* | 𓂓 | the life force, fed by offerings |
| ba | *bꜣ* | 𓅽 | the personality, a human-headed bird that leaves the tomb by day |
| akh | *ꜣḫ* | 𓅜 | the transfigured spirit, the dead who passed the judgement |
| name | *rn* | 𓂋𓈖 | the name, kept alive by being spoken |
| shadow | *šwt* | | the shadow |

In the judgement (*Book of the Dead*, chapter 125), the heart (*jb*) is weighed against the feather of Maat. Thoth writes the result, Anubis holds the scales; a heart heavy with wrongdoing is eaten by the monster Ammit. The one who passes is *mꜣꜥ-ḫrw*, "true of voice", and becomes an *ꜣḫ*.
