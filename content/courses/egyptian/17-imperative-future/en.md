# 17. The imperative and the future

Two last verb constructions complete the grammar of this course. The **imperative** gives orders, and a few of the most common verbs have irregular imperatives worth knowing by sight. The **future** is not a tense ending but a construction you already half know: the adverbial sentence with *r* "towards" + infinitive, *jw.f r sḏm* "he is towards hearing", "he will hear".

## The imperative

| Verb | Imperative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *sḏm* hear | *sḏm* | hear!, listen! |
| *ḏd* say | *ḏd* | say! |
| *jrj* do | *jr* | do!, make! |
| *jj* come | *mj* (irregular) | come! |
| *rdj* give | *jmj* (irregular) | give!, cause! |
| *jnj* bring | *jn* | bring! |
| *šm* go | *šm* | go! |

The plural adds *-w*, usually unwritten. An imperative is often followed by a dative of the person spoken to: *mj n.j* "come to me", *jmj n.j* "give me", *sḏm r.k* "listen, you!".

**Do not**: the negative imperative is *m* + the negatival complement (written like the infinitive): *m ḏd* "do not say", *m snḏ* "do not fear". *m* is the imperative of a negative verb, "do not do".

*jmj* + subjunctive means "let …, cause that …": *jmj jr.f st* "let him do it".

## The future

| Construction | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *jw* + subject + *r* + infinitive | *jw.f r sḏm* | he will hear |
| with a noun subject | *jw nswt r jwt* | the king will come |
| after *mk* | *mk wj r ḏd* | look, I am going to say |
| negative | *nn sḏm.f* | he will not hear (lesson 15) |

The future is the adverbial sentence again: *ḥr* + infinitive "in the course of" gives the progressive (lesson 12), *r* + infinitive "towards" gives the future, *m* + infinitive with verbs of motion "in the act of".

| Preposition + infinitive | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *ḥr* | is doing | *jw.f ḥr sḏm* he is hearing |
| *m* | is in the act of (motion) | *jw.f m jwt* he is coming |
| *r* | will do | *jw.f r sḏm* he will hear |

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- *mj n.j* → {{Come to me!}}
- *m snḏ* → {{Do not fear! / Do not be afraid!}}
- *jw.j r ḏd n.k* → {{I will say to you. / I will tell you.}}
- *jmj jr.f st* → {{Let him do it! / Cause him to do it!}}
:::

::: summary
- Imperative: the stem (*sḏm*, *jr*, *jn*); irregular *mj* come!, *jmj* give!; *m* + complement: do not (*m ḏd*).
- Future: *jw.f r* + infinitive (*jw.f r sḏm*); negative *nn sḏm.f*.
- *ḥr* + infinitive: is doing; *m* + infinitive: in the act of (motion); *r* + infinitive: will do.
:::
