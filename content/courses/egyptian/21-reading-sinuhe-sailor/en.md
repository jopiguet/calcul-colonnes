# 21. Reading: Sinuhe and the Shipwrecked Sailor

The Middle Kingdom left Egypt's classical literature, copied by scribes for centuries as models of style. Two works open almost every Egyptology course: the **Story of Sinuhe**, an official who flees Egypt at the death of King Amenemhat I and returns in old age, and the **Shipwrecked Sailor**, a tale within a tale told to cheer up a worried official. This lesson reads their opening lines with the grammar of the course. The transliterations follow the manuscripts; the translations are ours.

## Sinuhe: the opening

*jrj-pꜥt ḥꜣtj-ꜥ sꜣb ꜥḏ-mr … Sꜣ-nht ḏd.f:*

*jnk šmsw šms nb.f, bꜣk n jpt nswt.*

"The hereditary noble and count, judge and district administrator … Sinuhe, he says: I was a follower who followed his lord, a servant of the royal harem."

| Phrase | Grammar |
|---|---|
| *jrj-pꜥt*, *ḥꜣtj-ꜥ* | titles: "one who belongs to the elite", "foremost in action", count |
| *sꜣb ꜥḏ-mr* | titles: "judge", "administrator of a district" |
| *ḏd.f* | *sḏm.f*: "he says" |
| *jnk šmsw* | nominal sentence with the independent pronoun: "I am (was) a follower" |
| *šms nb.f* | participle with object: "who followed his lord" |
| *bꜣk n jpt nswt* | indirect genitive *n* after a masculine noun |

## Sinuhe: the death of the king

*ḥꜣt-sp 30 ꜣbd 3 ꜣḫt sw 7, ꜥr nṯr r ꜣḫt.f, nswt-bjtj Sḥtp-jb-Rꜥ.*

"Regnal year 30, month 3 of Inundation, day 7: the god ascended to his horizon, the king of Upper and Lower Egypt Sehetepibre."

| Phrase | Grammar |
|---|---|
| *ḥꜣt-sp 30 ꜣbd 3 ꜣḫt sw 7* | a date (lesson 11) |
| *ꜥr nṯr* | *sḏm.f* with a noun subject: "the god ascended" |
| *r ꜣḫt.f* | preposition *r* + noun + suffix: "to his horizon" |
| *Sḥtp-jb-Rꜥ* | throne name of Amenemhat I: "Ra is the one who satisfies the heart", *Rꜥ* written first |

The king does not die: "the god ascended to his horizon", like the sun setting.

## The Shipwrecked Sailor

*ḏd jn šmsw jqr: wḏꜣ jb.k ḥꜣtj-ꜥ. mk pḥ.n.n ẖnw.*

"Then the excellent follower said: May your heart be well, count! Look, we have reached home."

| Phrase | Grammar |
|---|---|
| *ḏd jn šmsw jqr* | "said by the excellent follower": verb + *jn* + noun subject, a narrative form |
| *wḏꜣ jb.k* | subjunctive *sḏm.f* of a wish: "may your heart be sound" |
| *mk* | particle "look" |
| *pḥ.n.n ẖnw* | *sḏm.n.f* with the suffix *.n* "we": "we have reached the Residence, home" |

::: summary
- Sinuhe: *jnk šmsw šms nb.f* "I was a follower who followed his lord": independent pronoun, participle with object.
- *ꜥr nṯr r ꜣḫt.f*: the king's death as the sun reaching its horizon; a date in regnal years.
- The Shipwrecked Sailor: *ḏd jn* + subject; *wḏꜣ jb.k* (subjunctive wish); *mk pḥ.n.n ẖnw* (*sḏm.n.f*).
- Every form of the course appears in the first lines of the classics.
:::
