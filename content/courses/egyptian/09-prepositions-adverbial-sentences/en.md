# 9. Prepositions and the adverbial sentence

Egyptian has no verb "to be" in the present: "the king is in the palace" is simply *nswt m pr nswt*, "king in palace". A subject followed by a prepositional phrase or an adverb makes a complete sentence, the **adverbial sentence**. This lesson gives the common prepositions and the three ways to build that sentence: with a noun subject, with *jw*, and with *mk* "look".

## Prepositions

| Preposition | Meanings | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *m* 𓅓 | in, at, with (a tool), from, as | *m pr* in the house; *m sš* as a scribe |
| *n* 𓈖 | to, for (a person) | *n nb.f* for his lord |
| *r* 𓂋 | to, towards (a place), against, more than | *r nwt* to the town |
| *ḥr* 𓁷 | on, upon, because of | *ḥr mw* on the water |
| *ḫr* | with, under (someone's authority), near | *ḫr nswt* with the king |
| *ẖr* | under, carrying | *ẖr jnw* carrying tribute |
| *ḥnꜥ* | together with | *ḥnꜥ snt.f* with his sister |
| *mj* | like | *mj rꜥ* like Ra |
| *jn* | by (the agent) | *jn nswt* by the king |
| *ḏr* | since | *ḏr rk nṯr* since the time of the god |
| *m-ꜥ* | from (a person), in the hand of | *m-ꜥ ḥqꜣ* from the ruler |
| *m-ḫt* | after | *m-ḫt hrw* after days |

Before a suffix, *m* becomes *jm* and *r* becomes *jr*: *jm.f* in it, *jr.f* to it.

## The adverbial sentence

| Subject | Construction | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| a noun | noun + predicate | *nswt m pr.f* | the king is in his house |
| a noun or a suffix | *jw* + subject + predicate | *jw.f m pr.f* | he is in his house |
| a dependent pronoun | *mk* + subject + predicate | *mk sw m pr.f* | look, he is in his house |

A pronoun subject needs a word to lean on: the particle *jw* takes a **suffix** (*jw.f*, *jw.s*, *jw.j*), the particle *mk* "look" a **dependent** pronoun (*mk wj*, *mk sw*). *jw* presents a statement as a fact of the situation; *mk* points to it, as in "here I am".

The predicate can also be an adverb: *jw.f ꜥꜣ* "he is here", *jw.f jm* "he is there".

::: summary
- *m* in, *n* to (a person), *r* to (a place), *ḥr* on, *ḫr* with, *ẖr* under, *ḥnꜥ* with, *mj* like, *jn* by, *ḏr* since, *m-ꜥ* from, *m-ḫt* after. *jm.f*, *jr.f* before a suffix.
- No "to be" in the present: subject + prepositional phrase or adverb.
- Noun subject alone; *jw* + suffix (*jw.f m pr*); *mk* + dependent (*mk sw m pr*).
:::
