# 11. Numbers and dates

Egyptian counts in tens, with a sign for each power of ten repeated as often as needed: 325 is three coils of rope, two hobbles and five strokes. There is no zero and no place value, so a number reads like a pile of coins. The calendar is just as practical: three seasons of four months of thirty days, plus five extra days. This lesson teaches the signs, the order of a number after its noun, and how a date is written.

## The number signs

| Sign | Value | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 𓏤 | 1 | stroke |
| 𓎆 | 10 | hobble for cattle |
| 𓍢 | 100 | coil of rope |
| 𓆼 | 1,000 | lotus plant |
| 𓂭 | 10,000 | finger |
| 𓆐 | 100,000 | tadpole |
| 𓁨 | 1,000,000 | the god Heh, arms raised |

Each sign is repeated up to nine times, the largest first: 𓍢𓍢𓍢𓎆𓎆𓏤𓏤𓏤𓏤𓏤 = 100 + 100 + 100 + 10 + 10 + 5 = 325. The god Heh also means "millions", "eternity".

## Numbers with nouns

| Rule | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| the number follows the noun | *sꜣ 3* | three sons |
| the noun is usually singular | *rnpt 10* | ten years |
| "one" is *wꜥ*, after the noun | *sꜣ wꜥ* | one son, a single son |
| "two" is *snwj*, or the noun in the dual | *sꜣwj* | two sons |
| ordinal "first" is *tpj* | *hrw tpj* | the first day |
| ordinals 2–9 add *-nw* | *snnw* second, *ḫmtnw* third | *hrw ḫmtnw* the third day |

## Dates

The civil year has 365 days:

| Season | Transliteration | Meaning | Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inundation | *ꜣḫt* | the Nile in flood | 4 × 30 days |
| Growing | *prt* | "coming out" of the crops | 4 × 30 days |
| Harvest | *šmw* | summer, the dry season | 4 × 30 days |
| Five extra days | *ḥrjw rnpt* | "those upon the year" | 5 days |

A date counts the king's years of reign:

*ḥꜣt-sp 5 ꜣbd 2 ꜣḫt sw 10* — "regnal year 5, month 2 of Inundation, day 10".

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *ḥꜣt-sp* (*rnpt-sp*) | regnal year |
| *ꜣbd* 𓇹 | month (the moon) |
| *sw* | day (in dates) |

The year started in theory with the flood and the rising of the star Sirius; because the civil year was a quarter-day short, it drifted round the seasons once in 1,460 years.

::: summary
- 𓏤 1, 𓎆 10, 𓍢 100, 𓆼 1,000, 𓂭 10,000, 𓆐 100,000, 𓁨 1,000,000; largest first, each repeated.

- Three seasons *ꜣḫt, prt, šmw* of four 30-day months, plus 5 days. *ḥꜣt-sp* year, *ꜣbd* month, *sw* day.
