# 13. The sḏm.f

Egyptian verbs are named after a model verb, *sḏm* "hear": the form *sḏm.f* is "the form like *sḏm.f*", whatever the verb. It is the verb stem followed directly by its subject, a suffix pronoun or a noun. It has no tense of its own: depending on its shape and its context it expresses a general present ("he hears"), a wish ("may he hear") or a purpose ("so that he hears"). This lesson covers its forms and two of its uses: the **imperfective** and the **subjunctive**.

## The forms

| Subject | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *sḏm.j* | I hear |
| 2nd sg. m. | *sḏm.k* | you hear |
| 3rd sg. m. | *sḏm.f* | he hears |
| 3rd sg. f. | *sḏm.s* | she hears |
| 1st pl. | *sḏm.n* | we hear |
| 3rd pl. | *sḏm.sn* | they hear |
| a noun | *sḏm sꜣ* | the son hears |

A noun subject takes the place of the suffix: *ḏd.f* "he says", *ḏd nswt* "the king says". The object follows the subject: *sḏm.f mdw* "he hears the words"; a pronoun object is dependent: *sḏm.f sw* "he hears him".

## Imperfective and subjunctive

With strong verbs the two look alike. The weak and doubled verbs separate them: the **imperfective** doubles the last consonant, the **subjunctive** does not.

| Verb | Imperfective (repeated, general) | Subjunctive (wish, purpose) |
|---|---|---|
| *jrj* do | *jrr.f* he does | *jr.f* may he do, that he do |
| *mrj* love | *mrr.f* he loves | *mr.f* may he love |
| *mꜣꜣ* see | *mꜣꜣ.f* he sees | *mꜣ.f* may he see |
| *rdj* give | *dd.f* he gives | *dj.f* may he give |
| *sḏm* hear | *sḏm.f* he hears | *sḏm.f* may he hear |

| Use | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| imperfective: what happens habitually | *jrr.f mrrt nṯr* | he does what the god loves |
| subjunctive: wish | *dj.f ꜥnḫ* | may he give life |
| subjunctive: purpose | *jj.n.j mꜣ.j nfrw.k* | I have come so that I may see your beauty |
| subjunctive after *rdj* "cause" | *rdj.n.f jr.j st* | he caused me to do it, he let me do it |

*jw* + *sḏm.f* states a general truth: *jw sḏm.f* "he (always) hears".

::: summary
- *sḏm.f*: stem + subject (suffix or noun); object after the subject; pronoun object dependent (*sḏm.f sw*).
- Imperfective: general, repeated; weak verbs double (*jrr.f*, *mrr.f*, *dd.f*).
- Subjunctive: wish, purpose, after *rdj* "cause"; no doubling (*jr.f*, *mr.f*, *dj.f*).
:::
