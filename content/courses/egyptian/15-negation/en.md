# 15. Negation

Egyptian has no single word for "not". It has two negative words, *n* and *nn*, a negative verb *tm*, and the pair *nj … js* for sentences without a verb. Which one is used depends on what is negated, and two of the combinations mean the opposite of what you expect: *n sḏm.f* is a **past** ("he did not hear"), *n sḏm.n.f* a **present** ("he does not, cannot hear"). Learn this lesson's table well; negation decides the meaning of whole sentences.

## Negating verbs

| Negation | Meaning | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| *n sḏm.f* | past | *n sḏm.f* | he did not hear |
| *n sḏm.n.f* | general, ability | *n sḏm.n.f* | he does not hear, he cannot hear |
| *nn sḏm.f* | future | *nn sḏm.f* | he will not hear |
| *tm* + negatival complement | "not to …" inside a clause | *r tm sḏm* | so as not to hear |

The crossing of *n sḏm.f* and *n sḏm.n.f* has a history: the old past form after *n* survived as a past, and the general meaning moved to the other one. A mnemonic: **n + no .n = no past action**; **n + .n = never, not at all**.

## Negating existence and the adverbial sentence

| Construction | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *nn* + noun | *nn mw* | there is no water |
| *nn* + subject + predicate | *nn sw m pr* | he is not in the house |
| *nn* + noun + *n* + person | *nn sꜣ n.f* | he has no son |

## Negating nominal and adjectival sentences

*nj* comes first and *js* after the first word or phrase:

| Construction | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| nominal | *nj jnk js sš* | I am not a scribe |
| nominal with *pw* | *nj sꜣ.j js pw* | he is not my son |
| adjectival | *nj nfr js* | it is not good |

## Negative words worth knowing

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *nn* … *nb* | no …, none: *nn rmṯ nb* nobody |
| *n zp* | never: *n zp jr.j st* I never did it |
| *nj-sw* | he belongs to |
| *jwtj* | who has not, without (negative nisbe): *jwtj sꜣ.f* who has no son |

What does it mean?

- n sḏm.f → ((he did not hear* / he cannot hear / he will not hear))
- n sḏm.n.f → ((he did not hear / he cannot hear* / he will not hear))
- nn sḏm.f → ((he did not hear / he cannot hear / he will not hear*))
- nn mw → ((there is no water* / he is not water / do not drink water))
:::

::: summary
- *n sḏm.f* past "did not"; *n sḏm.n.f* general "does not, cannot"; *nn sḏm.f* future "will not".
- *nn* + noun: "there is no"; *nn sw m pr*: "he is not in the house"; *nn … nb*: nobody, nothing.
- *nj … js* negates nominal and adjectival sentences; *tm* negates inside clauses (*r tm sḏm*).
- *n zp* never; *jwtj* who has not.
:::
