# 14. The sḏm.n.f and the stative

To say that something **happened**, Egyptian inserts *.n* between the verb and its subject: *sḏm.n.f* "he heard, he has heard". To say that something **is in a state** that results from an action, it uses a separate form with its own endings, the **stative**: *jj.kj* "I have come, I am here", *ꜥnḫ.w* "alive". With these two forms you can read narrative and most biographical inscriptions.

## The sḏm.n.f

| Subject | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *sḏm.n.j* | I heard |
| 3rd sg. m. | *sḏm.n.f* | he heard |
| 3rd sg. f. | *sḏm.n.s* | she heard |
| 3rd pl. | *sḏm.n.sn* | they heard |
| a noun | *sḏm.n nswt* | the king heard |

The *.n* is written 𓈖 before the suffix. Weak verbs never double here:

| Verb | sḏm.n.f | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *jrj* do | *jr.n.f* | he did |
| *mꜣꜣ* see | *mꜣ.n.f* | he saw |
| *rdj* give | *rdj.n.f* | he gave |
| *jnj* bring | *jn.n.f* | he brought |
| *jj* come | *jj.n.f* | he came |

| Construction | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| alone, in narrative | *ḏd.n.f n.j* | he said to me |
| with *jw*: the result counts now | *jw sḏm.n.j mdw.k* | I have heard your words |
| with an object | *rdj.n.f n.j jnw* | he gave me tribute |

## The stative

The stative has its own endings and follows its subject:

| Person | Ending | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *.kj* | *jj.kj* | I have come |
| 2nd sg. | *.tj* | *jj.tj* | you have come |
| 3rd sg. m. | *.w* (often unwritten) | *jj.w* | he has come |
| 3rd sg. f. | *.tj* | *jj.tj* | she has come |
| 1st pl. | *.wjn* | *jj.wjn* | we have come |
| 3rd pl. | *.w* | *jj.w* | they have come |

Its meaning depends on the verb:

| Verb | Stative means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| of motion | completed, "has come" | *jw.f jj.w* he has come |
| transitive | passive state, "is (has been) done" | *jw pr qd.w* the house is built |
| of quality | "is …" | *jw.f nfr.w* he is good |
| *ꜥnḫ* live | "alive" | *jw.f ꜥnḫ.w* he is alive |

A famous wish ends many royal names: *ꜥnḫ ḏt* "alive for ever".

::: summary
- *sḏm.n.f*: past, completed action; *.n* before the subject; weak verbs without doubling (*jr.n.f*, *mꜣ.n.f*). *jw sḏm.n.f* "he has heard".
- Stative: *.kj, .tj, .w, .tj; .wjn, .tjwnj, .w*; a state: completed with motion verbs, passive with transitives, "is" with qualities.
- *ꜥnḫ.w* alive; *ꜥnḫ ḏt* alive for ever.
:::
