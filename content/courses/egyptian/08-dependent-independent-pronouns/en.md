# 8. Dependent and independent pronouns

Besides the suffixes, Egyptian has two more sets of personal pronouns. The **dependent pronouns** are light words that cannot begin a sentence: they serve as the object of a verb ("he sees **him**") and as the subject after an adjective or a particle. The **independent pronouns** are heavy, stressed words that stand first: "**I** am your son", "it is **he** who…". Knowing which set a slot needs is half of reading Egyptian sentences.

## Dependent pronouns

| Person | Form | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *wj* | *mꜣꜣ.f wj* | he sees me |
| 2nd sg. m. | *tw* | *mꜣꜣ.f tw* | he sees you |
| 2nd sg. f. | *ṯn* | *mꜣꜣ.f ṯn* | he sees you (a woman) |
| 3rd sg. m. | *sw* | *mꜣꜣ.f sw* | he sees him |
| 3rd sg. f. | *sj*, *st* | *mꜣꜣ.f sj* | he sees her |
| 1st pl. | *n* | *mꜣꜣ.f n* | he sees us |
| 2nd pl. | *ṯn* | *mꜣꜣ.f ṯn* | he sees you (several) |
| 3rd pl. | *sn*, *st* | *mꜣꜣ.f sn* | he sees them |

*st* also means "it" for a thing or a situation. Three uses:

| Use | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| object of a verb | *rḫ.n.j sw* | I knew him |
| subject of an adjectival sentence | *nfr sw* | he is good |
| subject after a particle | *mk wj m pr* | look, I am in the house |

## Independent pronouns

| Person | Form | Person | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *jnk* | 1st pl. | *jnn* |
| 2nd sg. m. | *ntk* | 2nd pl. | *nttn* |
| 2nd sg. f. | *ntt* | 3rd pl. | *ntsn* |
| 3rd sg. m. | *ntf* | | |
| 3rd sg. f. | *nts* | | |

They begin a sentence and carry emphasis:

| Sentence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *jnk sꜣ.k* | I am your son |
| *ntf nb.j* | he is my lord |
| *jnk ḏd sw* | it is I who said it |

The 1st person *jnk* is written 𓇋𓈖𓎡; the others start with 𓈖𓏏 *nt*: *ntf* 𓈖𓏏𓆑, *nts* 𓈖𓏏𓋴.

::: summary
- Dependent: *wj, tw, ṯn, sw, sj/st; n, ṯn, sn/st*. Object of a verb, subject after an adjective or a particle (*nfr sw*, *mk wj*).
- Independent: *jnk, ntk, ntt, ntf, nts; jnn, nttn, ntsn*. First in the sentence, emphatic: *jnk sꜣ.k*.
- Suffix after a noun, preposition or verb; dependent never first; independent first.
:::
