# 5. Nouns: gender and number

Egyptian nouns have two genders, masculine and feminine, and three numbers: singular, **dual** and plural. There is no case ending and, in Middle Egyptian, no article: *sꜣ* is "son", "a son" or "the son". The endings are few and regular, and the writing marks them clearly. This lesson also introduces the demonstrative "this", which follows its noun.

## Gender

A feminine noun ends in *-t*, written with the loaf 𓏏. Many pairs differ only by it:

| Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|
| *sn* 𓌢𓈖𓀀 brother | *snt* 𓌢𓈖𓏏𓁐 sister |
| *sꜣ* 𓅭𓀀 son | *sꜣt* 𓅭𓏏𓁐 daughter |
| *nb* 𓎟 lord | *nbt* 𓎟𓏏 lady, mistress |
| *nṯr* 𓊹 god | *nṯrt* 𓊹𓏏 goddess |
| *ḥm* servant | *ḥmt* woman, wife |

Masculine nouns have no ending. Abstract ideas are often feminine: *mꜣꜥt* truth, order (Maat).

## Number

| Number | Masculine | Feminine | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Singular | — | *-t* | *sn*, *snt* |
| Dual | *-wj* | *-tj* | *snwj* two brothers, *sntj* two sisters |
| Plural | *-w* | *-wt* | *snw* brothers, *snwt* sisters |

The writing shows the number:

| Plural or dual written as | Example |
|---|---|
| three strokes 𓏥 after the word | 𓌢𓈖𓀀𓏥 *snw* brothers |
| the sign written three times | 𓊹𓊹𓊹 *nṯrw* gods |
| the sign written twice, or two strokes 𓏭 | 𓇾𓇾 *tꜣwj* the Two Lands (Egypt) |

The endings *-w* and *-wt* are often left unwritten: the strokes do the work. The dual is used for natural pairs: *rdwj* the two legs, *ꜥwj* the two arms, *tꜣwj* the Two Lands of Upper and Lower Egypt.

## The demonstrative "this"

| | Masculine | Feminine | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| this, these | *pn* | *tn* | *nn* (+ *n*) |
| that, those | *pf* | *tf* | *nf* (+ *n*) |

*pn* and *tn* follow the noun and agree with it: *sꜣ pn* this son, *sꜣt tn* this daughter. The plural *nn* comes **before** the noun, linked by *n*: *nn n sꜣw* these sons. In Late Egyptian a new article appears: *pꜣ*, *tꜣ*, *nꜣ*, the ancestor of the Coptic article.

::: summary
- Two genders: masculine without ending, feminine in *-t* 𓏏. No case, no article.
- Dual *-wj* (m.), *-tj* (f.); plural *-w* (m.), *-wt* (f.). Written with 𓏥, a tripled or doubled sign, or 𓏭.
- *pn, tn* follow the noun; *nn n* precedes it. *tꜣwj* the Two Lands.
:::
