# 4. Logograms and determinatives

Sounds alone would make Egyptian hard to read: without vowels, *pr* could be "house" or "go out", *mn* "remain" or "suffer". Two devices solve this. A **logogram** writes a whole word with one picture, marked by a stroke. A **determinative**, placed at the end of a word, is not read at all: it tells the class of meaning and, as a bonus, shows where the word ends. This lesson teaches both, and the thirty or so determinatives that cover most texts.

## Logograms

A sign used for the word it pictures takes a short stroke 𓏤, read as nothing:

| Spelling | Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 𓉐𓏤 | *pr* | house |
| 𓂋𓏤 | *r* | mouth |
| 𓁷𓏤 | *ḥr* | face |
| 𓁶𓏤 | *tp* | head |
| 𓇳𓏤 | *rꜥ* | sun, the god Ra |
| 𓄣𓏤 | *jb* | heart |

The same sign without the stroke is a phonogram: 𓉐 in *prj* "go out" is just *pr*.

## Determinatives

| Sign | Class | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 𓀀 | man, male person | *sꜣ* son |
| 𓁐 | woman | *ḥmt* woman, wife |
| 𓀀𓁐𓏥 | people | *rmṯ* people |
| 𓀁 | mouth, eating, speaking, thinking | *wnm* eat; *mdw* speak |
| 𓀭 | god, king | *Wsjr* Osiris |
| 𓂻 | walking legs: movement | *šm* go; *prj* go out |
| 𓂡 | arm with a stick: force, effort | *nḫt* strong |
| 𓁹 | eye: seeing | *mꜣꜣ* see |
| 𓇳 | sun: time, light | *hrw* day |
| 𓇰 | sky with a sceptre: night | *grḥ* night |
| 𓊖 | crossroads in a wall: town | *nwt* town |
| 𓈉 | three hills: foreign land, desert | *ḫꜣst* foreign land |
| 𓉐 | house plan: building | *pr* house |
| 𓏛 | rolled papyrus: abstract idea, writing | *rḫ* know |
| 𓅪 | sparrow: small, bad | *bjn* bad |
| 𓏥 | three strokes: plural | *nṯrw* gods |

A word may take two determinatives, or none. A **personal name** ends with 𓀀 or 𓁐; a god's name with 𓀭 or the falcon on a standard 𓅆.

## Reading a whole word

| Spelling | Phonograms | Determinative | Word |
|---|---|---|---|
| 𓃹𓈖𓅓𓀁 | wn + n + m | 𓀁 eating | *wnm* eat |
| 𓈙𓅓𓂻 | š + m | 𓂻 movement | *šm* go |
| 𓉐𓂋𓂻 | pr + r | 𓂻 movement | *prj* go out |
| 𓉔𓂋𓅱𓇳 | h + r + w | 𓇳 time | *hrw* day |
| 𓎼𓂋𓎛𓇰 | g + r + ḥ | 𓇰 night | *grḥ* night |
| 𓈞𓏏𓁐 | ḥm + t | 𓁐 woman | *ḥmt* woman |

The hare 𓃹 is the biliteral *wn*; the *j* of *prj* is not written, as often for weak consonants.

::: summary
- Logogram: the word pictured, with a stroke 𓏤: 𓉐𓏤 *pr*, 𓇳𓏤 *rꜥ*.
- Determinative: silent class sign at the end of a word; it also shows where the word ends.
- 𓀀 man, 𓁐 woman, 𓀁 mouth, 𓀭 god, 𓂻 movement, 𓂡 force, 𓇳 time, 𓇰 night, 𓊖 town, 𓈉 foreign land, 𓏛 abstract, 𓅪 bad, 𓏥 plural.
:::
