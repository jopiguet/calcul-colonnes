# 18. The subjunctive

So far every verb has stated a fact. The **subjunctive** presents an action as willed, expected or possible: "let us go", "so that he may learn", "if ever he comes". Its forms are easy: the thematic vowel is lengthened, ο to ω and ε to η. It has no augment, and its tenses differ in aspect only.

## The forms

| Person | Present active | Aorist active | Present middle | εἰμί |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | λύω | λύσω | λύωμαι | ὦ |
| 2nd sg. | λύῃς | λύσῃς | λύῃ | ᾖς |
| 3rd sg. | λύῃ | λύσῃ | λύηται | ᾖ |
| 1st pl. | λύωμεν | λύσωμεν | λυώμεθα | ὦμεν |
| 2nd pl. | λύητε | λύσητε | λύησθε | ἦτε |
| 3rd pl. | λύωσι(ν) | λύσωσι(ν) | λύωνται | ὦσι(ν) |

Compare the indicative λύομεν with the subjunctive λύωμεν, λύει with λύῃ. The aorist passive subjunctive is λυθῶ, λυθῇς, λυθῇ.

## The uses

| Use | Construction | Example |
|---|---|---|
| exhortation | 1st person plural | ἴωμεν "let us go" |
| deliberation | in a question | τί ποιῶμεν; "what are we to do?" |
| prohibition | μή + aorist subjunctive | μὴ ποιήσῃς τοῦτο "don't do this" |
| purpose | ἵνα, ὅπως + subjunctive | ἔρχεται ἵνα μάθῃ "he comes in order to learn" |
| fear | φοβοῦμαι μή + subjunctive | φοβοῦμαι μὴ ἔλθῃ "I am afraid that he may come" |
| condition, future or general | ἐάν + subjunctive | ἐὰν ἔλθῃ, ὄψεται "if he comes, he will see" |

ἐάν is εἰ + ἄν. The particle ἄν, which has no translation, marks a possibility or a generality; it returns with the optative.

::: exercise translate

- μανθάνομεν ἵνα σοφοὶ γιγνώμεθα. → {{We learn so that we may become wise. / We learn in order to become wise.}}

- τί ποιῶμεν; → {{What are we to do? / What should we do?}}
- φοβοῦμαι μὴ ὁ πόλεμος ἔλθῃ. → {{I am afraid that war may come. / I fear that the war will come.}}
:::

::: summary
- Subjunctive: lengthened thematic vowel: λύω, λύῃς, λύῃ, λύωμεν, λύητε, λύωσι; aorist λύσω; ὦ, ᾖς, ᾖ.
- No augment; tenses differ in aspect.
- Exhortation, deliberation, μή + aorist (prohibition), ἵνα (purpose), φοβοῦμαι μή (fear), ἐάν (condition).
:::
