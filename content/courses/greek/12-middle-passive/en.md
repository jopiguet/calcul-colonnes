# 12. The middle and the passive

Greek has a third voice between active and passive, the **middle**: the subject acts for itself or on itself. λύω τὸν ἵππον "I untie the horse"; λύομαι τὸν υἱόν "I free my son for myself", that is, "I ransom him". In the present and imperfect the passive has the same forms as the middle: λύεται is "he ransoms" or "he is freed", and the context decides.

## Present and imperfect, middle and passive

| Person | Present | Imperfect |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | λύομαι | ἐλυόμην |
| 2nd sg. | λύῃ, λύει | ἐλύου |
| 3rd sg. | λύεται | ἐλύετο |
| 1st pl. | λυόμεθα | ἐλυόμεθα |
| 2nd pl. | λύεσθε | ἐλύεσθε |
| 3rd pl. | λύονται | ἐλύοντο |

Infinitive λύεσθαι. The future middle adds σ: λύσομαι; the first aorist middle is ἐλυσάμην, -σω, -σατο, -σάμεθα, -σασθε, -σαντο.

## What the middle means

| Active | Middle | The difference |
|---|---|---|
| λύω untie | λύομαι ransom | for oneself |
| λούω wash (someone) | λούομαι wash (oneself) | on oneself |
| παύω stop (someone) | παύομαι stop, cease | on oneself |
| πείθω persuade | πείθομαι obey (+ dat.) | let oneself be persuaded |

## Deponents

Some verbs exist only in the middle with an active meaning: βούλομαι "I want", γίγνομαι "I become", μάχομαι "I fight", ἔρχομαι "I come". They are called **deponent** verbs.

## The agent

With a passive, the person by whom something is done is ὑπό + genitive; the instrument is the plain dative: ἡ πόλις ὑπὸ τῶν πολεμίων λύεται "the city is being destroyed by the enemy"; βάλλεται λίθῳ "he is hit by a stone".

::: summary
- Middle-passive: -ομαι, -ῃ, -εται, -όμεθα, -εσθε, -ονται; imperfect -όμην, -ου, -ετο, -όμεθα, -εσθε, -οντο.
- Middle: for or on oneself (λύομαι ransom, παύομαι cease); passive: same forms in the present system.
- Deponents βούλομαι, γίγνομαι, μάχομαι; agent ὑπό + gen., instrument dative.
:::
