# 10. The future and the first aorist

Two tenses built with σ. The **future** is the present with σ added to the stem: λύ-σ-ω "I shall loose". The **aorist** is the main past tense of narrative: ἔ-λυ-σα "I loosed". The aorist brings in the idea that runs through the whole Greek verb, **aspect**: the aorist presents an action as a single whole, the present and imperfect as something going on.

## The future

| Person | λύω | γράφω (γραφ-σ → ψ) | ἄγω (ἀγ-σ → ξ) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | λύσω | γράψω | ἄξω |
| 2nd sg. | λύσεις | γράψεις | ἄξεις |
| 3rd sg. | λύσει | γράψει | ἄξει |
| 1st pl. | λύσομεν | γράψομεν | ἄξομεν |
| 2nd pl. | λύσετε | γράψετε | ἄξετε |
| 3rd pl. | λύσουσι(ν) | γράψουσι(ν) | ἄξουσι(ν) |

The endings are those of the present. The σ meets the stem as in the dative plural: labials give ψ (πέμπω → πέμψω), gutturals ξ (ἄγω → ἄξω), dentals fall (πείθω → πείσω). Infinitive: λύσειν.

## The first aorist

| Person | Ending | λύω |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | -σα | ἔλυσα I loosed |
| 2nd sg. | -σας | ἔλυσας |
| 3rd sg. | -σε(ν) | ἔλυσε(ν) |
| 1st pl. | -σαμεν | ἐλύσαμεν |
| 2nd pl. | -σατε | ἐλύσατε |
| 3rd pl. | -σαν | ἔλυσαν |

The augment, as in the imperfect: ἔγραψα, ἦξα, ἔπεμψα. Only the indicative has it; the infinitive λῦσαι and the imperative λῦσον do not.

## Aspect

| Form | Aspect | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ἔγραφε | imperfect: process | he was writing, he used to write |
| ἔγραψε | aorist: whole act | he wrote |
| γράφειν | present infinitive: process | to be writing, to write (habitually) |
| γράψαι | aorist infinitive: whole act | to write (once) |

Outside the indicative, the aorist has no past meaning: it marks aspect only. A dictionary gives a verb's **principal parts**, present, future, aorist and more, because each tense may be built differently: γράφω, γράψω, ἔγραψα.

::: summary
- Future: stem + σ + present endings: λύσω; γράψω, ἄξω, πείσω.
- First aorist: augment + stem + σα: ἔλυσα, -σας, -σε(ν), -σαμεν, -σατε, -σαν; λῦσαι.
- Aspect: aorist = the act as a whole; present/imperfect = process; principal parts γράφω, γράψω, ἔγραψα.
:::
