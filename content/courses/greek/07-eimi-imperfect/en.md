# 7. "To be" and the imperfect

Two things in one lesson: the verb εἰμί "I am", irregular as in every language, and the first past tense, the **imperfect**, which describes an action going on, repeated or attempted in the past. The imperfect introduces the **augment**, the ε- that marks the past of the indicative and that you will see in front of every past tense from now on.

## εἰμί, "I am"

| Person | Present | Imperfect |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | εἰμί I am | ἦ, ἦν I was |
| 2nd sg. | εἶ | ἦσθα |
| 3rd sg. | ἐστί(ν) | ἦν |
| 1st pl. | ἐσμέν | ἦμεν |
| 2nd pl. | ἐστέ | ἦτε |
| 3rd pl. | εἰσί(ν) | ἦσαν |

The infinitive is εἶναι "to be". At the head of a sentence ἔστι means "there is" or "it is possible": ἔστι θεός "there is a god". The predicate noun or adjective is in the nominative: Σωκράτης σοφός ἐστιν "Socrates is wise".

## The imperfect

| Person | Ending | λύω |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | -ον | ἔλυον I was loosing |
| 2nd sg. | -ες | ἔλυες |
| 3rd sg. | -ε(ν) | ἔλυε(ν) |
| 1st pl. | -ομεν | ἐλύομεν |
| 2nd pl. | -ετε | ἐλύετε |
| 3rd pl. | -ον | ἔλυον |

The imperfect is built on the present stem: augment + stem + thematic vowel + ending.

## The augment

| Verb begins with | Augment | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a consonant | ε- before it (*syllabic*) | γράφω → ἔγραφον |
| a vowel | the vowel lengthened (*temporal*): α, ε → η; ο → ω | ἄγω → ἦγον; ἐθέλω → ἤθελον |
| a prefix | the augment after the prefix | ἀποπέμπω → ἀπέπεμπον |

The imperfect is the tense of description and of process: ἔγραφεν "he was writing", "he used to write", "he tried to write". For a single completed act Greek uses another past, the aorist (lesson 10).

::: summary
- εἰμί, εἶ, ἐστί(ν), ἐσμέν, ἐστέ, εἰσί(ν); imperfect ἦ, ἦσθα, ἦν, ἦμεν, ἦτε, ἦσαν; εἶναι.
- Imperfect: augment + present stem + -ον, -ες, -ε(ν), -ομεν, -ετε, -ον.
- Augment: ε- before a consonant, a lengthened first vowel, after a prefix; the imperfect is ongoing or repeated.
:::
