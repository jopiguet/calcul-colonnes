# 6. Adjectives and prepositions

The adjectives of the first two declensions take their endings from the nouns you know: -ος for the masculine, -η or -α for the feminine, -ον for the neuter. What is new is where they stand. The place of the article decides whether "good" describes the man or says something about him, and Greek needs no verb "to be" for the second.

## ἀγαθός, ἀγαθή, ἀγαθόν

| Case | m. | f. | n. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nom. sg. | ἀγαθός | ἀγαθή | ἀγαθόν |
| Gen. sg. | ἀγαθοῦ | ἀγαθῆς | ἀγαθοῦ |
| Dat. sg. | ἀγαθῷ | ἀγαθῇ | ἀγαθῷ |
| Acc. sg. | ἀγαθόν | ἀγαθήν | ἀγαθόν |
| Nom. pl. | ἀγαθοί | ἀγαθαί | ἀγαθά |
| Gen. pl. | ἀγαθῶν | ἀγαθῶν | ἀγαθῶν |
| Dat. pl. | ἀγαθοῖς | ἀγαθαῖς | ἀγαθοῖς |
| Acc. pl. | ἀγαθούς | ἀγαθάς | ἀγαθά |

After ε, ι, ρ the feminine has α: δίκαιος, δικαία, δίκαιον "just". Compound adjectives have one form for masculine and feminine: ἄδικος, ἄδικον "unjust", ἡ ἄδικος πόλις.

## Attributive and predicate position

| Greek | Position | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ὁ ἀγαθὸς ἄνθρωπος | attributive: after the article | the good man |
| ὁ ἄνθρωπος ὁ ἀγαθός | attributive, repeated article | the good man |
| ὁ ἄνθρωπος ἀγαθός | predicate: outside the article | the man is good |
| ἀγαθὸς ὁ ἄνθρωπος | predicate | the man is good |

With the article and no noun, the adjective becomes a noun: οἱ ἀγαθοί "good people", τὰ καλά "fine things". The adverb ends in -ως: καλῶς "well", δικαίως "justly".

## Prepositions

| Preposition | Case | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ἐν | dative | in, among |
| εἰς | accusative | into, to |
| ἐκ, ἐξ | genitive | out of |
| ἀπό | genitive | away from |
| πρός | accusative | towards, against |
| μετά | genitive / accusative | with / after |
| διά | genitive / accusative | through / because of |

The case after a preposition carries meaning too: μετὰ τῶν φίλων "with the friends", μετὰ τὸν πόλεμον "after the war".

::: summary
- ἀγαθός, -ή, -όν; δίκαιος, -α, -ον; ἄδικος, -ον for m. and f.
- After the article: description (ὁ ἀγαθὸς ἄνθρωπος); outside it: statement (ὁ ἄνθρωπος ἀγαθός).
- ἐν + dat., εἰς + acc., ἐκ + gen.; adverbs in -ως.
:::
