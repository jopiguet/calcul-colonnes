# 12. Respectful words

Tibetan has a second vocabulary for speaking of others with respect: for their body, their name, what they eat and where they go. It is used for elders, guests, teachers and lamas, and never for oneself. Children in Lhasa learn it early. This lesson gives the most common pairs.

## Plain and respectful

| Plain | Respectful | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| མིང *ming* | མཚན *mtshan* | name |
| མགོ *mgo* | དབུ *dbu* | head |
| ལག་པ *lag pa* | ཕྱག *phyag* | hand |
| ཁ *kha* | ཞལ *zhal* | mouth, face |
| ལུས *lus* | སྐུ་གཟུགས *sku gzugs* | body, health |
| ཁ་ལག *kha lag* | ཞལ་ལག *zhal lag* | food |
| ཟ *za* | བཞེས *bzhes* | eat, drink |
| འགྲོ *'gro* | ཕེབས *phebs* | go, come |
| སྡོད *sdod* | བཞུགས *bzhugs* | stay, sit |
| བྱེད *byed* | གནང *gnang* | do |

- One speaks of oneself plainly and of the other respectfully: ངའི་མིང "my name", ཁྱེད་རང་གི་མཚན "your name".
- ཕེབས *phebs* is both "go" and "come": ག་ལེ་ཕེབས "go gently", and *phep* "come in".
- Many respectful words are seen in the greetings: སྐུ་གཟུགས in "how are you?", བཞུགས in ག་ལེ་བཞུགས.

## Polite requests

A polite request adds རོགས་གནང *rogs gnang*, "please do the favour", after the verb.

| Script | Wylie | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| བཞུགས་རོགས་གནང་། | bzhugs rogs gnang | Please sit down. |
| ཇ་བཞེས་རོགས་གནང་། | ja bzhes rogs gnang | Please have some tea. |
| ཁྱེད་རང་ག་པར་ཕེབས་ཀྱི་ཡིན། | khyed rang ga par phebs kyi yin | Where will you go? |

Names and family words take ལགས *lags*: ཨ་མ་ལགས *a ma lags* "mother", པ་ལགས *pa lags* "father", *Tenzin la*.

::: exercise request

- Please sit down. → ((བཞུགས་རོགས་གནང་།* / ག་ལེ་བཞུགས།))
- Please have some tea. → ((ཇ་བཞེས་རོགས་གནང་།* / ཇ་འཐུང་གི་ཡོད།))
- Where will you go? → ((ཁྱེད་རང་ག་པར་ཕེབས་ཀྱི་ཡིན།* / ང་ག་པར་འགྲོ་གི་ཡིན།))
- father, politely → ((པ་ལགས* / ཕ))
:::

::: summary
- A respectful word for others, a plain one for oneself: མིང *ming* / མཚན *mtshan*, འགྲོ *'gro* / ཕེབས *phebs*, ཟ *za* / བཞེས *bzhes*, སྡོད *sdod* / བཞུགས *bzhugs*.
- རོགས་གནང *rogs gnang* after the verb makes a request: བཞུགས་རོགས་གནང "please sit".
- ལགས *lags* after names and family words: ཨ་མ་ལགས *a ma lags*, པ་ལགས *pa lags*.
:::
