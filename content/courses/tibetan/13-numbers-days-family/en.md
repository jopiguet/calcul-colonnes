# 13. Numbers, days, family

Tibetan numbers are decimal and have their own digits, ༡ ༢ ༣, used in books and on calendars. The days of the week are named after the sun, the moon and the five planets. This lesson gives numbers, days and family words.

## One to ten

| Digit | Script | Wylie | Said |
|---|---|---|---|
| ༡ 1 | གཅིག | gcig | chik |
| ༢ 2 | གཉིས | gnyis | nyi |
| ༣ 3 | གསུམ | gsum | sum |
| ༤ 4 | བཞི | bzhi | shi |
| ༥ 5 | ལྔ | lnga | nga |
| ༦ 6 | དྲུག | drug | druk |
| ༧ 7 | བདུན | bdun | dün |
| ༨ 8 | བརྒྱད | brgyad | gyé |
| ༩ 9 | དགུ | dgu | gu |
| ༡༠ 10 | བཅུ | bcu | chu |

- 11 is བཅུ་གཅིག, "ten one"; 20 ཉི་ཤུ; 30 སུམ་ཅུ; 100 བརྒྱ; 1000 སྟོང.
- The number follows the noun: ཁྱི་གསུམ "three dogs".

- 3 → [[གསུམ]]
- 5 → [[ལྔ]]

:::

## Days

| Script | Wylie | Named after | Day |
|---|---|---|---|
| གཟའ་ཉི་མ | gza' nyi ma | the sun | Sunday |
| གཟའ་ཟླ་བ | gza' zla ba | the moon | Monday |
| གཟའ་མིག་དམར | gza' mig dmar | Mars, "red eye" | Tuesday |
| གཟའ་ལྷག་པ | gza' lhag pa | Mercury | Wednesday |
| གཟའ་ཕུར་བུ | gza' phur bu | Jupiter | Thursday |
| གཟའ་པ་སངས | gza' pa sangs | Venus | Friday |
| གཟའ་སྤེན་པ | gza' spen pa | Saturn | Saturday |

གཟའ means "planet". Many Tibetans are named after the day of their birth: *Nyima*, *Dawa*, *Pasang*, *Penpa*.

::: exercise days
Which day?

- གཟའ་ཟླ་བ → ((Sunday / Monday* / Friday))
- གཟའ་ཉི་མ → ((Sunday* / Wednesday / Saturday))
- གཟའ་པ་སངས → ((Tuesday / Friday* / Thursday))
- གཟའ་སྤེན་པ → ((Monday / Thursday / Saturday*))
:::

## Family and time

| Script | Wylie | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ཨ་མ་ལགས | a ma lags | mother |
| པ་ལགས | pa lags | father |
| བུ་མོ | bu mo | daughter, girl |
| ཇོ་ལགས | jo lags | elder brother |
| ཨ་ཅག | a cag | elder sister |
| ནུ་བོ | nu bo | younger brother |
| དེ་རིང | de ring | today |
| ཁ་སང | kha sang | yesterday |
| སང་ཉིན | sang nyin | tomorrow |

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- ཁྱི་གསུམ → {{Three dogs.}}
- སང་ཉིན་གཟའ་ཟླ་བ་རེད། → {{Tomorrow is Monday.}}
- ང་ལ་ཇོ་ལགས་གཉིས་ཡོད། → {{I have two elder brothers.}}
- དེ་རིང་གཟའ་ཉི་མ་རེད། → {{Today is Sunday.}}
:::

::: summary
- གཅིག *gcig* 1, གཉིས *gnyis* 2, གསུམ *gsum* 3, བཞི *bzhi* 4, ལྔ *lnga* 5, དྲུག *drug* 6, བདུན *bdun* 7, བརྒྱད *brgyad* 8, དགུ *dgu* 9, བཅུ *bcu* 10; digits ༡ to ༩, ༠.
- The number follows the noun: ཁྱི་གསུམ.
- Days are planets: གཟའ་ཉི་མ Sunday to གཟའ་སྤེན་པ Saturday.
- ཨ་མ་ལགས *a ma lags*, པ་ལགས *pa lags*; དེ་རིང *de ring* today, ཁ་སང *kha sang* yesterday, སང་ཉིན *sang nyin* tomorrow.
:::
