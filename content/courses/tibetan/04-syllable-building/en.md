# 4. Building a syllable

A Tibetan syllable has one root letter, the one that carries the vowel. Around it, up to six letters may stand: one before it, one above it, one or two below it, two after it. བསྒྲུབས *bsgrubs*, "accomplished", uses nearly all of them. This lesson names each place and shows how to find the root.

## The places

| Place | Letters | Example | Wylie | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| prefix, before | ག ད བ མ འ | དགུ | dgu | nine |
| superscript, above | ར ལ ས | རྟ | rta | horse |
| subscript, below | ya, ra, la, wa | ཁྱི | khyi | dog |
| suffix, after | ག ང ད ན བ མ འ ར ལ ས | ལུག | lug | sheep |
| second suffix | ས | གངས | gangs | snow, glacier |

- A superscript sits on top of the root, which is written smaller beneath it: རྟ is ར over ཏ.
- Subscripts take a reduced shape: ya ྱ, ra ྲ, la ླ, wa ྭ, as in ཁྱི, སྒྲ "sound", ཟླ་བ "moon".
- Wylie writes the letters in the order of the script: prefix, superscript, root, subscripts, vowel, suffixes: *b-s-g-r-u-b-s*.

- the ya in ཁྱི → ((superscript / subscript* / second suffix))

:::

## Finding the root

The root carries the vowel sign, or the vowel *a* when there is none: in དགུ the *u* is under *g*, in ལུག under *l*. In a stack, the root is the letter between superscript and subscript: སྒྲ has *s* above, *g* as root, *r* below.

| Syllable | Wylie | Root | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| རྟ | rta | t | ta | horse |
| ཁྱི | khyi | kh | khyi, close to *chi* | dog |
| ལུག | lug | l | luk | sheep |
| མཚོ | mtsho | tsh | tsho | lake |
| གངས | gangs | g | kang | snow, glacier |
| བསྒྲུབས | bsgrubs | g | drup | accomplished |

Most letters around the root are not said in Lhasa: མཚོ is *tsho*, རྟ *ta*. Lesson 5 gives the rules.

::: summary
- One root carries the vowel; around it: prefix, superscript, subscripts, suffix, second suffix.
- Prefixes ག ད བ མ འ; superscripts ར ལ ས; subscripts ya, ra, la, wa; ten suffixes; second suffix ས.
- Wylie keeps the order of the script: *b-s-g-r-u-b-s*.
- རྟ *rta* horse, ཁྱི *khyi* dog, ལུག *lug* sheep, མཚོ *mtsho* lake, གངས *gangs* snow.
:::
