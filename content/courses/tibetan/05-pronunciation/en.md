# 5. From spelling to speech

Tibetan spelling was fixed more than a thousand years ago, and Lhasa speech has since simplified it: letters fell silent, stacks merged into new sounds, final letters coloured the vowel. A few rules cover most words. This lesson gives them, so that བཀྲ་ཤིས *bkra shis* can be read *tashi* and བོད *bod* *pö*.

## Silent letters

Prefixes and superscripts are not said: only the root and what follows it is heard.

| Script | Wylie | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| མཚོ | mtsho | tsho | lake |
| རྟ | rta | ta | horse |
| དགུ | dgu | gu | nine |
| སྐད | skad | ké | speech, language |

## Subscripts that change the sound

| Stack | Said | Example | Wylie | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| k, g + ya | ky, gy | ཁྱི | khyi | khyi | dog |
| p, ph, b + ya | c, ch, j | བྱ | bya | ja | bird |
| k, t, p + ra | tr | བཀྲ་ཤིས | bkra shis | tashi | good fortune |
| g, d, b + ra | dr | འབྲས | 'bras | dré | rice |
| z + la | d | ཟླ་བ | zla ba | dawa | moon |
| g, b + la | l | གླང | glang | lang | ox |

*tr* and *dr* are said with the tongue tip curled back, as in Indian English.

## Final letters

| Final | Effect | Example | Wylie | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ད ས ལ ན | a → é, o → ö, u → ü | བོད | bod | pö | Tibet |
| | | ཡུལ | yul | yü | country |
| | | སྐད | skad | ké | language |
| ག བ | a short, clipped *k*, *p* | ལུག | lug | luk | sheep |
| ང མ | a nasal | གངས | gangs | kang | snow |

- *d* and *s* are not said; *l* and *n* lengthen or nasalise the vowel.
- A second suffix ས is silent: གངས *kang*.
- These are guides to Lhasa speech; tone and length are best learnt by ear.

::: exercise final
How is it said?

- ཡུལ → ((yul / yü* / yu))
- ལུག → ((luk* / lu / lü))
- སྐད → ((skad / ké* / ka))
:::

::: summary
- Prefixes and superscripts are silent: མཚོ *tsho*, རྟ *ta*.
- Subscripts: *ky*, *ch*, *tr*, *dr*: བྱ *ja*, བཀྲ་ཤིས *tashi*, འབྲས *dré*; *zl* is *d*: ཟླ་བ *dawa*.
- Final *d s l n* change the vowel: བོད *pö*, ཡུལ *yü*, སྐད *ké*; final *g b* are clipped *k p*.
:::
