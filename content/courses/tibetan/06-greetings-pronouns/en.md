# 6. Greetings and pronouns

Tibetan greetings wish good fortune and ask after the other's health with respectful words. Goodbye depends on who leaves: one says "go gently" to the one who goes and "stay gently" to the one who stays. This lesson gives the greetings, the pronouns, and the polite *lags*.

## Greetings

| Script | Wylie | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| བཀྲ་ཤིས་བདེ་ལེགས། | bkra shis bde legs | tashi delek | hello |
| ཁྱེད་རང་སྐུ་གཟུགས་བདེ་པོ་ཡིན་པས། | khyed rang sku gzugs bde po yin pas | khyerang kusuk depo yinpé | how are you? |
| ལགས་ཡིན། ང་བདེ་པོ་ཡིན། | lags yin, nga bde po yin | la yin, nga depo yin | yes, I am well |
| ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེ། | thugs rje che | thukjé ché | thank you |
| ག་ལེ་ཕེབས། | ga le phebs | kalé phep | goodbye (to the one leaving) |
| ག་ལེ་བཞུགས། | ga le bzhugs | kalé shuk | goodbye (to the one staying) |

- *bkra shis* is "good fortune", *bde legs* "well-being".
- *sku gzugs* is a respectful word for "body": one asks about another's health politely, and answers plainly about one's own.
- ལགས *la* makes a word polite: ལགས་སོ *lags so* "yes, of course", ལགས *lags* alone "yes?".

## Names

| Script | Wylie | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ཁྱེད་རང་གི་མཚན་ལ་ག་རེ་ཞུ་གི་ཡོད། | khyed rang gi mtshan la ga re zhu gi yod | What is your name? |
| ངའི་མིང་ལ་བསྟན་འཛིན་ཟེར། | nga'i ming la bstan 'dzin zer | My name is Tenzin. |

Two words for "name": མཚན *tsen*, respectful, for others; མིང *ming*, plain, for oneself.

## Pronouns

| | One | Several |
|---|---|---|
| I, we | ང *nga* | ང་ཚོ *nga tsho* |
| you (polite) | ཁྱེད་རང *khyed rang* | ཁྱེད་ཚོ *khyed tsho* |
| you (familiar) | ཁྱོད *khyod* | |
| he | ཁོ *kho* | ཁོ་ཚོ *kho tsho* |
| she | མོ *mo* | |
| he, she (respectful) | ཁོང *khong* | ཁོང་ཚོ *khong tsho* |

*tsho* makes the plural of pronouns. ཁྱེད་རང *khyed rang* is the usual "you" to an adult; ཁོང *khong* the usual "he, she" for someone respected.

::: summary
- བཀྲ་ཤིས་བདེ་ལེགས *bkra shis bde legs* hello; ཐུགས་རྗེ་ཆེ *thugs rje che* thank you; ག་ལེ་ཕེབས *ga le phebs* to the one leaving, ག་ལེ་བཞུགས *ga le bzhugs* to the one staying.
- མཚན name of others, མིང one's own: ངའི་མིང་ལ … ཟེར.
- ང *nga* I, ཁྱེད་རང *khyed rang* you, ཁོ *kho* he, མོ *mo* she, ཁོང *khong* he or she (respectful); plural *tsho*.
- ལགས *lags* *la* makes speech polite.
:::
