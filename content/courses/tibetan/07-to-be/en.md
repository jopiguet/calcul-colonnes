# 7. Five ways to be

Tibetan has two verbs for "to be", one for what something *is* and one for where or whether it *exists*, and each has forms that depend on how the speaker knows. "I am a student" uses ཡིན *yin*; "he is a student", said of someone else, རེད *red*. This lesson gives the five forms and when each is used.

## Being something: yin, red

| Script | Wylie | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ང་སློབ་མ་ཡིན། | nga slob ma yin | nga lopma yin | I am a student. |
| ཁོ་སློབ་མ་རེད། | kho slob ma red | kho lopma ré | He is a student. |

- ཡིན *yin* is used of oneself, from the inside: what I am, what we are.
- རེད *red* states a fact about others, as known to everyone.
- The verb comes last; the sentence is subject, then what it is, then the verb.

::: exercise yin-red
Which verb?

- I am a doctor: ང་སྨན་པ … → ((ཡིན* / རེད))
- She is a teacher: མོ་དགེ་རྒན … → ((ཡིན / རེད*))
- We are Tibetans: ང་ཚོ་བོད་པ … → ((ཡིན* / རེད))
- Tenzin is a student: བསྟན་འཛིན་སློབ་མ … → ((ཡིན / རེད*))
:::

## Being there, having: yod, 'dug, yod red

| Form | Use | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ཡོད | I know it as my own | ང་ལ་དེབ་ཡོད། | I have a book. |
| འདུག | I see it now | ཁང་པའི་ནང་ལ་ཁྱི་འདུག། | There is a dog in the house. |
| ཡོད་རེད | it is known, general | བོད་ལ་གཡག་མང་པོ་ཡོད་རེད། | In Tibet there are many yaks. |

- "I have" is "to me there is": ང་ལ "to me" with ཡོད.
- འདུག *'dug*, said *duk*, reports what the speaker sees or finds: the dog is there, look.
- ཡོད་རེད *yod red* reports what is known in general, not seen now.

::: exercise exist
Which form?

- You look into the room and see a cat → ((ཡོད / འདུག* / ཡོད་རེད))
- You say you have a brother → ((ཡོད* / འདུག / ཡོད་རེད))
- Lhasa has many temples, as everyone knows → ((ཡོད / འདུག / ཡོད་རེད*))
- You taste the tea: there is salt in it → ((ཡོད / འདུག* / ཡོད་རེད))
:::

::: summary
- Being something: ཡིན *yin* of oneself, རེད *red* of others.
- Being there, having: ཡོད *yod* my own knowledge, འདུག *'dug* seen now, ཡོད་རེད *yod red* known in general.
- "I have": ང་ལ … ཡོད.
- སློབ་མ *slob ma* student, དགེ་རྒན *dge rgan* teacher, སྨན་པ *sman pa* doctor, དེབ *deb* book.
:::
