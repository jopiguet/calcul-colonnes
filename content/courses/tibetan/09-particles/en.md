# 9. Of, to, from, and

Tibetan marks the role of a noun with a particle after it: "of", "to", "in", "from". The genitive "of" changes its spelling to match the last letter of the word before it, which every reader of Tibetan learns by heart. This lesson gives the genitive, ལ *la*, ནས *nas* and དང *dang*.

## The genitive: "of"

| Last letter | Genitive | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ག ང | གི | ཁོང་གི་དེབ | his book |
| ད བ ས | ཀྱི | བོད་ཀྱི་སྐད | the language of Tibet |
| ན མ ར ལ | གྱི | དགེ་རྒན་གྱི་དེབ | the teacher's book |
| a vowel | འི, joined | ངའི་དེབ | my book |

- The owner comes first, the thing owned after: བོད་ཀྱི་སྐད, "of Tibet the language".
- After a vowel, *'i* joins the syllable: ང, ངའི "my"; ཁོ, ཁོའི "his".
- Speech says them alike, *ki* or *gi*; the spelling follows the rule.

## To, in: la; from: nas

| Particle | Meaning | Example | |
|---|---|---|---|
| ལ | to, in, at | ཁོ་ལ | to him |
| ར, joined | to, after a vowel | ལྷ་སར | to Lhasa |
| ནས | from | ལྷ་ས་ནས | from Lhasa |
| དང | and, with | ང་དང་ཁྱེད་རང | you and I |

- ལ *la* is "to" and also "in, at": བོད་ལ "in Tibet"; and "to" a person, ང་ལ "to me", as in ང་ལ་དེབ་ཡོད.
- After a vowel, *r* may join the word instead: ལྷ་ས, ལྷ་སར "to Lhasa".
- ནས *nas* is "from", of place and of time.

::: summary
- Genitive: གི after *g ng*, ཀྱི after *d b s*, གྱི after *n m r l*, འི joined after a vowel.
- ལ *la* to, in, at; after a vowel *r* may join: ལྷ་སར.
- ནས *nas* from; དང *dang* and, with.
:::
