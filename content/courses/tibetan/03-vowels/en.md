# 3. The four vowel signs

A letter alone is read with *a*; four signs, written above or below it, change that vowel to *i*, *u*, *e* or *o*. At the start of a word, the vowel is written on the carrier letter ཨ. This lesson reads and writes syllables with all five vowels.

## The signs

| Sign | Name | Vowel | With ཀ | With ཨ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ི | gigu | i | ཀི ki | ཨི i |
| ུ | zhabkyu | u | ཀུ ku | ཨུ u |
| ེ | drengbu | e | ཀེ ke | ཨེ e |
| ོ | naro | o | ཀོ ko | ཨོ o |

- *gigu*, *drengbu* and *naro* sit above the letter; *zhabkyu*, "foot", sits below.
- Without a sign, the vowel is *a*: ཀ *ka*.
- The carrier ཨ is silent when it carries a sign: ཨོ is *o*.

::: summary
- No sign: *a*. ི *i*, ུ *u*, ེ *e*, ོ *o*.
- At the start of a word the vowel sits on ཨ: ཨོ *o*.
- ཆུ *chu* water, རི *ri* mountain, མེ *me* fire, མི *mi* person, བུ *bu* son, ཞོ *zho* yoghurt.
:::
