# 10. Doing and going to do

A Tibetan verb does not change for the person; it is followed by a particle and a form of "to be", and these carry the person and how the speaker knows. "I eat" ends in ཡོད *yod*, "he is eating, I see" in འདུག *'dug*. This lesson gives the present and the future.

## The present: verb, gi, yod

| Who | Ending | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| I, we | གི་ཡོད | ང་ཁ་ལག་ཟ་གི་ཡོད། | I eat, I am eating. |
| he, she: seen now | གི་འདུག | ཁོ་ཁ་ལག་ཟ་གི་འདུག། | He is eating (I see him). |
| he, she: known | གི་ཡོད་རེད | ཁོ་ཁ་ལག་ཟ་གི་ཡོད་རེད། | He eats (as is known). |

- The object comes before the verb: *nga* "I", *kha lag* "food", *za* "eat".
- The particle is spelt like the genitive: གི after a vowel or *g ng*, ཀྱི after *d b s*, གྱི after *n m r l*: སློབ་ཀྱི་ཡོད "I learn".

::: exercise present
Which ending?

- I am drinking tea: ང་བོད་ཇ་འཐུང … → ((གི་ཡོད* / གི་འདུག))
- Look, she is reading: མོ་དེབ་ཀློག … → ((གི་ཡོད / གི་འདུག*))
- Tibetans drink butter tea (everyone knows): བོད་པ་ཚོ་བོད་ཇ་འཐུང … → ((གི་འདུག / གི་ཡོད་རེད*))
- I learn Tibetan: ང་བོད་སྐད་སློབ … → ((ཀྱི་ཡོད* / གྱི་ཡོད))
:::

## The future: verb, gi, yin

| Who | Ending | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| I, we | གི་ཡིན | ང་ལྷ་སར་འགྲོ་གི་ཡིན། | I will go to Lhasa. |
| he, she | གི་རེད | ཁོ་ལྷ་སར་འགྲོ་གི་རེད། | He will go to Lhasa. |

The future uses the two forms of "to be something", ཡིན *yin* of oneself and རེད *red* of others, as in lesson 7.

## Everyday verbs

| Script | Wylie | Said | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ཟ | za | sa | eat |
| འཐུང | 'thung | thung | drink |
| འགྲོ | 'gro | dro | go |
| སློབ | slob | lop | learn |
| ཀློག | klog | lok | read |
| འབྲི | 'bri | dri | write |
| ཉལ | nyal | nyé | sleep |

::: exercise who
Who does it, and how do we know?

- ཁོ་ཉལ་གྱི་འདུག། → ((he is sleeping, I see him* / I am sleeping / he will sleep))
- ང་འབྲི་གི་ཡོད། → ((I am writing* / he is writing / I will write))
- མོ་ལྷ་སར་འགྲོ་གི་རེད། → ((she goes to Lhasa / she will go to Lhasa* / I will go to Lhasa))
- ང་དེབ་ཀློག་གི་ཡིན། → ((I am reading / I will read a book* / he reads))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- ང་བོད་ཇ་འཐུང་གི་ཡོད། → {{I drink butter tea. / I am drinking Tibetan tea. / I drink Tibetan tea.}}
- ཁོ་ཁ་ལག་ཟ་གི་འདུག། → {{He is eating. / He is eating food.}}
- ང་སློབ་གྲྭར་འགྲོ་གི་ཡིན། → {{I will go to school.}}
- ང་བོད་སྐད་སློབ་ཀྱི་ཡོད། → {{I learn Tibetan. / I am learning Tibetan.}}
:::

::: summary
- Present: verb + གི་ཡོད (I), གི་འདུག (others, seen), གི་ཡོད་རེད (others, known).
- Future: verb + གི་ཡིན (I), གི་རེད (others).
- The particle is spelt གི, ཀྱི or གྱི by the verb's last letter.
- ཟ *za* eat, འཐུང *'thung* drink, འགྲོ *'gro* go, སློབ *slob* learn, ཀློག *klog* read, འབྲི *'bri* write, ཉལ *nyal* sleep.
:::
