# 17. Malayalam as it is spoken

Malayalam is strongly diglossic: the written language of books, news and this course is not what people say at the tea shop. Spoken forms are shorter, vary from district to district, and are mixed with English. You will be understood in the written forms; you will understand people only once you know the spoken ones.

## Written and spoken

| Written | Spoken | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| എന്താണ് *entāṇŭ* | എന്താ *entā* | what is it |
| ശരിയാണ് *śariyāṇŭ* | ശരിയാ *śariyā* | that's right |
| പോകുകയാണ് *pōkukayāṇŭ* | പോവാ *pōvā*, പോകുവാ | (I) am going |
| എന്ത് ചെയ്യുന്നു? | എന്താ ചെയ്യുന്നേ? *entā ceyyunnē?* | what are you doing? |
| വരുന്നു *varunnu* | വരണു *varaṇu* (south) | comes |
| ഇല്ലേ? *illē?* | ഇല്ലേ? | isn't it?, didn't you? |

Speech drops the ആണ് of identity, softens -ക- between vowels to -വ- (പോകുക, പോവുക) and loves the tag ഇല്ലേ?. English nouns take Malayalam endings without fuss: ബസ്സിൽ "on the bus", ഫോണിൽ "on the phone", ഓഫീസിലേക്ക് "to the office".

## Calling people

Kinship words do the work of "sir" and "madam", and take a vocative ending when you call someone: -ആ for words in -അൻ, -ഏ or a long vowel for others.

| Word | Meaning | Calling |
|---|---|---|
| ചേട്ടൻ *cēṭṭan* | elder brother; a man a little older | ചേട്ടാ *cēṭṭā* |
| ചേച്ചി *cēcci* | elder sister; a woman a little older | ചേച്ചീ *cēccī* |
| അനിയൻ *aniyan* | younger brother | അനിയാ |
| അനിയത്തി *aniyatti* | younger sister | അനിയത്തീ |
| മോൻ *mōn*, മോൾ *mōḷ* | son, daughter; any child | മോനേ *mōnē*, മോളേ |
| അമ്മ *amma*, അച്ഛൻ *acchan* | mother, father | അമ്മേ *ammē*, അച്ഛാ *acchā* |

So ചേട്ടാ, ഒരു ചായ is how one orders tea from the man at the counter.

## Districts and Manglish

The south (Thiruvananthapuram), the centre (Thrissur, Kochi) and the north (Malabar, with many Arabic and Urdu words) each have their own sound and turns of phrase; films use them to place a character at once. Online, Malayalis write in Manglish, Malayalam in Latin letters: *Njan varaam* "I'll come", *Sughamano?* "are you well?".

::: summary
- Speech drops and shortens: എന്താ, ശരിയാ, പോവാ, എന്താ ചെയ്യുന്നേ?; tag ഇല്ലേ?
- Kinship words address people: ചേട്ടാ, ചേച്ചീ, മോനേ, അമ്മേ, അച്ഛാ.
- English nouns take Malayalam endings (ബസ്സിൽ); districts differ; Manglish online.
:::
