# 8. Roots and binyanim

Like Arabic, Hebrew builds its words from **roots** of three consonants poured into **patterns**. The root כ־ת־ב *k-t-v* "writing" gives כָּתַב *katav* "he wrote", מִכְתָּב *mikhtav* "letter", כְּתֹבֶת *ktovet* "address", כַּתָּב *katav* "reporter". The root ל־מ־ד *l-m-d* "learning" gives לָמַד "he learned", לִמֵּד "he taught", תַּלְמִיד *talmid* "pupil" and the Talmud. Verbs come in seven patterns, the **binyanim** ("buildings"), each with its own shade of meaning.

## The seven binyanim

| Binyan | Past (he) | Present (m.) | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pa'al | כָּתַב *katav* | כּוֹתֵב *kotev* | basic | wrote |
| nif'al | נִכְתַּב *nikhtav* | נִכְתָּב *nikhtav* | passive of pa'al | was written |
| pi'el | דִּבֵּר *diber* | מְדַבֵּר *medaber* | active, often intensive | spoke |
| pu'al | דֻּבַּר *dubar* | מְדֻבָּר *medubar* | passive of pi'el | was spoken of |
| hif'il | הִכְתִּיב *hikhtiv* | מַכְתִּיב *makhtiv* | causative | dictated (made write) |
| huf'al | הֻכְתַּב *hukhtav* | מֻכְתָּב *mukhtav* | passive of hif'il | was dictated |
| hitpa'el | הִתְכַּתֵּב *hitkatev* | מִתְכַּתֵּב *mitkatev* | reflexive, reciprocal | corresponded |

The dictionary lists a verb by its root and binyan, or by its past form "he": כָּתַב. Not every root is used in every binyan, and the meanings must sometimes be learnt: לָמַד "learned", לִמֵּד "taught" (pi'el), but הִתְלַבֵּשׁ "got dressed" (hitpa'el of ל־ב־ש "wear").

## Recognising the binyan

| Mark | Binyan |
|---|---|
| נ before the root in the past | nif'al |
| doubled middle letter, מְ in the present | pi'el |
| ה before the root, מַ in the present, often י before the last letter | hif'il |
| הִתְ / מִתְ before the root | hitpa'el |

::: summary
- Root + pattern: כ־ת־ב → כתב, מכתב, כתובת; ל־מ־ד → למד, לימד, תלמיד.
- Seven binyanim: pa'al, nif'al (passive), pi'el, pu'al (passive), hif'il (causative), huf'al (passive), hitpa'el (reflexive).
- Marks: נ (nif'al), doubled middle and מְ (pi'el), ה / מַ (hif'il), הִתְ / מִתְ (hitpa'el).
:::
