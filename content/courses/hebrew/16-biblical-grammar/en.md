# 16. Biblical Hebrew: how the narrative works

You can now read Modern Hebrew; Biblical Hebrew uses the same letters, roots and most of the same words, but its verbs tell a story differently. The text below is the Masoretic text, the vocalised Hebrew Bible fixed by scholars in Tiberias around the 9th–10th centuries, and it is in the public domain.

## The vav-consecutive

Biblical prose chains its events with *va-* + the prefix form: וַיֹּאמֶר *vayomer* "and he said", וַיֵּלֶךְ *vayelekh* "and he went", וַיַּרְא *vayar* "and he saw". The prefix form, which is the future in Modern Hebrew, means the past after this *va-*, and the *va-* doubles the next consonant. The reverse also happens: וְ + suffix form looks forward, וְאָמַרְתָּ *ve'amarta* "and you shall say".

| Form | Modern Hebrew | Biblical narrative |
|---|---|---|
| יֹאמַר | he will say | he will say |
| וַיֹּאמֶר | — | and he said |
| אָמַר | he said | he said, he has said |
| וְאָמַר | and he said | and he will say |

## Order and object

The usual order is verb, subject, object: וַיֹּאמֶר מֹשֶׁה אֶל הָעָם "and Moses said to the people". A definite direct object is marked by אֵת (אֶת־ before a hyphen-like *maqaf*): וַיַּרְא אֶת־הָאוֹר "and he saw the light". The particle has no translation; Modern Hebrew keeps it.

## Other features

| Biblical | Modern | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| אָנֹכִי *anokhi* | אֲנִי | I |
| הִנֵּה *hine* | הִנֵּה, הָנֵּה | behold, here is |
| אֲשֶׁר *asher* | ־שֶׁ | who, which |
| וַיְהִי *vayhi* | וְהָיָה | and it came to pass |
| הַ־ question | הַאִם | yes-no question |

The small marks above and below the letters that are neither vowels nor dagesh are cantillation signs (*te'amim*). They show how to chant the verse and where it divides; for reading you can ignore them. The verse ends with ׃ (*sof pasuq*).

::: summary
- Narrative: ־וַ + prefix form = past (ויאמר, וילך, וירא); ־וְ + suffix form = future (ואמרת).
- Order verb–subject–object; את marks a definite object.
- Biblical אנכי, הנה, אשר, ויהי; cantillation marks guide chanting; ׃ ends the verse.
:::
