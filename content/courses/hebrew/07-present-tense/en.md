# 7. The present tense

The Hebrew present tense is really a participle, like English "writing": it changes for gender and number, not for person. אֲנִי כּוֹתֵב *ani kotev* is "I (a man) write"; a woman says אֲנִי כּוֹתֶבֶת *ani kotevet*. So there are four forms, and the pronoun is always needed.

## Four forms

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| singular | כּוֹתֵב *kotev* | כּוֹתֶבֶת *kotevet* |
| plural | כּוֹתְבִים *kotvim* | כּוֹתְבוֹת *kotvot* |

The root is כ ת ב *k-t-v* "write"; the pattern of the present in the basic group of verbs (*pa'al*) is *CoCeC*: כּוֹתֵב, לוֹמֵד *lomed* "learn", הוֹלֵךְ *holekh* "go", אוֹהֵב *ohev* "love", יוֹשֵׁב *yoshev* "sit, live".

| Hebrew | Meaning |
|---|---|
| אֲנִי לוֹמֵד עִבְרִית. | I (m.) am learning Hebrew. |
| הִיא כּוֹתֶבֶת מִכְתָּב. | She is writing a letter. |
| אֲנַחְנוּ גָּרִים בְּחֵיפָה. | We live in Haifa. |
| הֵן אוֹהֲבוֹת מוּזִיקָה. | They (f.) love music. |

## Other common verbs

| Verb | m. sg. | f. sg. | m. pl. | f. pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| live (גוּר) | גָּר *gar* | גָּרָה *gara* | גָּרִים *garim* | גָּרוֹת *garot* |
| want (רוֹצֶה) | רוֹצֶה *rotse* | רוֹצָה *rotsa* | רוֹצִים *rotsim* | רוֹצוֹת *rotsot* |
| know (יוֹדֵעַ) | יוֹדֵעַ *yode'a* | יוֹדַעַת *yoda'at* | יוֹדְעִים *yod'im* | יוֹדְעוֹת *yod'ot* |
| speak (מְדַבֵּר) | מְדַבֵּר *medaber* | מְדַבֶּרֶת *medaberet* | מְדַבְּרִים | מְדַבְּרוֹת |

מְדַבֵּר belongs to another verb group, *pi'el*, with a מ prefix in the present (lesson 8). The negative is לֹא *lo*: אֲנִי לֹא יוֹדֵעַ "I don't know".

::: exercise who
Who could say it?

- אֲנִי כּוֹתֶבֶת → ((a man / a woman* / a group))
- אֲנַחְנוּ לוֹמְדִים → ((only men / men, or a mixed group* / only women))
- אֲנִי הוֹלֵךְ → ((a man* / a woman))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- אֲנִי לוֹמֵד עִבְרִית. → {{I am learning Hebrew. / I study Hebrew.}}
- הִיא גָּרָה בִּירוּשָׁלַיִם. → {{She lives in Jerusalem.}}
- אֲנַחְנוּ לֹא יוֹדְעִים. → {{We don't know.}}
- מָה אַתָּה רוֹצֶה? → {{What do you want?}}
:::

::: summary
- The present is a participle: four forms by gender and number, not by person; the pronoun is needed.
- Pa'al pattern CoCeC: כותב, כותבת, כותבים, כותבות; גר, רוצה, יודע.
- Negation לא: אני לא יודע.
:::
