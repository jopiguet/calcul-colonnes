# 5. Adjectives and "this"

A Hebrew adjective follows its noun and agrees with it in gender, number and definiteness: the article goes on both. That last rule is what distinguishes "the big house" from "the house is big". The demonstratives "this, these" follow the same pattern.

## The forms of the adjective

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| singular | גָּדוֹל *gadol* | גְּדוֹלָה *gdola* |
| plural | גְּדוֹלִים *gdolim* | גְּדוֹלוֹת *gdolot* |

The endings are those of the nouns: ה *-a*, ים *-im*, וֹת *-ot*. Common adjectives: קָטָן *katan* small, חָדָשׁ *chadash* new, יָשָׁן *yashan* old, יָפֶה *yafe* beautiful (f. יָפָה *yafa*), טוֹב *tov* good, רַע *ra* bad.

## Phrase or sentence

| Hebrew | Structure | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| בַּיִת גָּדוֹל *bayit gadol* | both indefinite | a big house |
| הַבַּיִת הַגָּדוֹל *ha-bayit ha-gadol* | article on both | the big house |
| הַבַּיִת גָּדוֹל *ha-bayit gadol* | article on the noun only | the house is big |
| עִיר יָפָה *ir yafa* | feminine | a beautiful city |

## "This" and "these"

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| this | זֶה *ze* | זֹאת *zot* |
| these | אֵלֶּה *ele* | אֵלֶּה *ele* |

זֶה סֵפֶר *ze sefer* is a sentence, "this is a book". הַסֵּפֶר הַזֶּה *ha-sefer ha-ze* is a phrase, "this book": the demonstrative comes after the noun and takes the article like an adjective.

Which form agrees?

- עִיר … "beautiful" → ((יָפֶה / יָפָה* / יָפִים))

- מוֹרוֹת … "good" → ((טוֹבִים / טוֹבוֹת* / טוֹבָה))
- the dual יָדַיִם "hands" takes → ((feminine plural adjectives* / masculine singular))
:::

::: exercise phrase
Phrase or sentence?

- הַבַּיִת הַגָּדוֹל → ((the big house* / the house is big))
- הַבַּיִת גָּדוֹל → ((the big house / the house is big*))
- זֶה סֵפֶר → ((this book / this is a book*))
- הַסֵּפֶר הַזֶּה → ((this book* / this is a book))

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- הָעִיר הַזֹּאת יָפָה. → {{This city is beautiful.}}
- יֵשׁ לִי מַחְבֶּרֶת חֲדָשָׁה. → {{I have a new notebook.}}
- אֵלֶּה סְפָרִים טוֹבִים. → {{These are good books.}}
- הַיֶּלֶד הַקָּטָן בַּבַּיִת. → {{The little boy is at home. / The small child is in the house.}}
:::

::: summary
- Adjective after the noun, agreeing: גדול, גדולה, גדולים, גדולות.
- Article on both: הבית הגדול "the big house"; on the noun only: הבית גדול "the house is big".
- זה, זאת, אלה: זה ספר "this is a book", הספר הזה "this book".
:::
