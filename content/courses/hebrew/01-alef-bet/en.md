# 1. The alef-bet

Hebrew is written from right to left with 22 letters, all consonants. The letters do not join, which makes them easier to learn than Arabic ones; five of them have a special form at the end of a word. The same alphabet has written Hebrew for some 2,500 years, from the Dead Sea Scrolls to today's newspapers; the square letters you will learn were borrowed from Aramaic. In this course Hebrew comes with a transliteration in italics: שָׁלוֹם *shalom* "peace, hello".

## The letters

| Letter | Name | Sound | Transliteration |
|---|---|---|---|
| א | alef | silent; carries a vowel | ' or nothing |
| ב | bet, vet | b with a dot (בּ), v without | b, v |
| ג | gimel | g as in *go* | g |
| ד | dalet | d | d |
| ה | he | h; silent at the end of a word | h |
| ו | vav | v; also the vowels *o*, *u* | v, o, u |
| ז | zayin | z | z |
| ח | chet | as in German *Bach* | ch |
| ט | tet | t | t |
| י | yod | y; also the vowel *i* | y, i |
| כ ך | kaf, khaf | k with a dot (כּ), *kh* without | k, kh |
| ל | lamed | l | l |
| מ ם | mem | m | m |
| נ ן | nun | n | n |
| ס | samekh | s | s |
| ע | ayin | silent today (a throat sound in the Bible) | ' or nothing |
| פ ף | pe, fe | p with a dot (פּ), f without | p, f |
| צ ץ | tsadi | ts | ts |
| ק | qof | k | k, q |
| ר | resh | a throaty r, as in French | r |
| ש | shin, sin | sh (dot on the right, שׁ), s (dot on the left, שׂ) | sh, s |
| ת | tav | t | t |

Several letters sound the same in modern Hebrew: ב and ו (*v*), כ and ח (*kh*), ט and ת (*t*), כּ and ק (*k*), ס and שׂ (*s*), א and ע (silent). Spelling must be learnt word by word.

## Final forms

| Letter | Final | Example |
|---|---|---|
| כ | ך | מֶלֶךְ *melekh* king |
| מ | ם | שָׁלוֹם *shalom* |
| נ | ן | כֵּן *ken* yes |
| פ | ף | כֶּסֶף *kesef* money, silver |
| צ | ץ | אֶרֶץ *erets* land |

## Typing Hebrew in this course

Answers can be typed in Hebrew letters, with or without vowel points, or in transliteration: שלום, שָׁלוֹם and *shalom* are one answer. They are compared by their consonants: letters with the same sound fold together, so those differences are tested by choices. The buttons under each exercise write the letters.
