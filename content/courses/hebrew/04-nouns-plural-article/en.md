# 4. Nouns: gender, plural and the article

Every Hebrew noun is masculine or feminine. Most feminines end in ה *-a* or ת *-et*; most masculines in a consonant. The plural adds ים *-im* to masculines and וֹת *-ot* to feminines. The article ה *ha-* "the" is a prefix, written joined to the word. With these three rules you can build most noun phrases of the language.

## Gender and plural

| Singular | Plural | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| יֶלֶד *yeled* (m.) | יְלָדִים *yeladim* | boy, boys |
| יַלְדָּה *yalda* (f.) | יְלָדוֹת *yeladot* | girl, girls |
| סֵפֶר *sefer* (m.) | סְפָרִים *sfarim* | book, books |
| מוֹרָה *mora* (f.) | מוֹרוֹת *morot* | teacher (f.) |
| מַחְבֶּרֶת *machberet* (f.) | מַחְבָּרוֹת *machbarot* | notebook |
| בַּיִת *bayit* (m.) | בָּתִּים *batim* | house |

The vowels inside the word often change in the plural; learn the plural with the noun. A few nouns take the "wrong" ending: אִשָּׁה *isha* "woman", plural נָשִׁים *nashim*; אָב *av* "father", plural אָבוֹת *avot*; עִיר *ir* "city" (f.), plural עָרִים *arim*. A mixed group is masculine.

## The dual

Things that come in pairs and some units of time take ַיִם *-ayim*: יָדַיִם *yadayim* "(two) hands", עֵינַיִם *einayim* "eyes", שְׁבוּעַיִם *shvu'ayim* "two weeks".

## The article

| Hebrew | Meaning |
|---|---|
| סֵפֶר *sefer* | a book |
| הַסֵּפֶר *ha-sefer* | the book |
| בְּבַיִת *be-vayit* | in a house |
| בַּבַּיִת *ba-bayit* | in the house: בְּ + הַ → בַּ |
| לַיֶּלֶד *la-yeled* | to the boy: לְ + הַ → לַ |

There is no indefinite article: סֵפֶר is "a book". After the prefixes בְּ "in", לְ "to" and כְּ "like", the ה of the article disappears and leaves its vowel.

::: summary
- Feminine in ה -a or ת -et; plural ים -im (m.), ות -ot (f.); irregular נשים, אבות, ערים.
- Dual -ayim: ידיים, עיניים.
- Article הַ prefixed; בְּ + הַ → בַּ, לְ + הַ → לַ; no indefinite article.
:::
