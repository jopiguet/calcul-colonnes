# 18. The Shema and the Priestly Blessing

Two short passages carry more weight in Jewish life than almost any others. The Shema is said morning and evening and is traditionally the last thing said before death. The Priestly Blessing was spoken by the priests in the Temple and is still given in synagogues and by parents to their children on Friday night.

## The divine name

The four letters יהוה are the name of the God of Israel. Jewish tradition does not pronounce it. The Masoretes wrote it with the vowels of אֲדֹנָי *Adonai* "my Lord", and that is how it is read in prayer and in the reading of the Torah. Outside prayer many people say הַשֵּׁם *HaShem* "the Name" and write ה׳. Read יְהוָה below as *Adonai*.

## The Shema

**Deuteronomy 6:4** שְׁמַע יִשְׂרָאֵל יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵינוּ יְהוָה אֶחָד׃

**6:5** וְאָהַבְתָּ אֵת יְהוָה אֱלֹהֶיךָ בְּכָל־לְבָבְךָ וּבְכָל־נַפְשְׁךָ וּבְכָל־מְאֹדֶךָ׃

| Word | Parts | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| שְׁמַע *shma* | imperative of ש־מ־ע | hear! |
| אֱלֹהֵינוּ *eloheinu* | אֱלֹהִים + ־ֵינוּ our | our God |
| וְאָהַבְתָּ *ve'ahavta* | וְ + suffix form, "you loved" | and you shall love |
| בְּכָל־לְבָבְךָ *bekhol levavkha* | בְּ + כָּל all + לֵבָב heart + ־ְךָ your | with all your heart |
| נַפְשְׁךָ *nafshekha* | נֶפֶשׁ soul, life + ־ְךָ | your soul |
| מְאֹדֶךָ *me'odekha* | מְאֹד "very" used as a noun + ־ֶךָ | your might |

וְאָהַבְתָּ is the forward-looking vav from lesson 16: וְ + suffix form = a future that works as a command.

## The Priestly Blessing

**Numbers 6:24** יְבָרֶכְךָ יְהוָה וְיִשְׁמְרֶךָ׃

**6:25** יָאֵר יְהוָה פָּנָיו אֵלֶיךָ וִיחֻנֶּךָּ׃

**6:26** יִשָּׂא יְהוָה פָּנָיו אֵלֶיךָ וְיָשֵׂם לְךָ שָׁלוֹם׃

Each verse is longer than the last: three, five, then seven words. The verbs are short prefix forms (*jussives*): יְבָרֶכְךָ "may he bless you", יָאֵר "may he make shine", יִשָּׂא "may he lift". פָּנָיו is "his face", from פָּנִים, a noun that is always plural.

## Translation

Hear, Israel: the LORD is our God, the LORD is one. And you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your might.

May the LORD bless you and keep you. May the LORD make his face shine upon you and be gracious to you. May the LORD lift up his face towards you and give you peace.

::: summary
- יהוה is not pronounced: read Adonai in prayer, HaShem outside it.
- Shema (Deut 6:4–5): שמע, אלהינו, ואהבת = vav + suffix form as command; כל, לבב, נפש, מאד.
- Priestly Blessing (Num 6:24–26): jussives יברכך, יאר, ישא; 3, 5, 7 words; ends in שלום.
:::
