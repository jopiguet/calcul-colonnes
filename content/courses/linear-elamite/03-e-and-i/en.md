# 3. e and i

Linear Elamite tells *e* from *i* in every series: *he* and *hi*, *ke* and *ki*, *re* and *ri*. Cuneiform, which wrote Elamite for much longer, often did not. This lesson gives the signs in *e* and in *i*, and explains why older tables number some of them instead.

## Numbered values

The 2025 plate of the inscriptions writes many of these signs with numbers: what this course reads *he* is *hi* there, and what it reads *hi* is *hi₂*. Desset's latest table replaces the numbers with the vowel *e*, following the same rule in every series.

| This course | 2025 plate |
|---|---|
| *he*, *hi* | *hi*, *hi₂* |
| *le*, *li* | *li₂*, *li* and *li₃* |
| *re*, *ri* | *ri*, *ri₂* |
| *ke* | *ke* (*ki₂* in 2022) |

Both readings are accepted in the answers, but the cards and corrections use those of this course.
