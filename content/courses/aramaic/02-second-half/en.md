# 2. The second half of the alphabet

The last eleven letters complete the alphabet. Among them are the second throat sound, ʿ (ayin), and three kinds of s. With all twenty-two you can read any word of the empire's documents.

## From lamedh to taw

| Letter | Sound | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 𐡋 | l | lamedh |
| 𐡌 | m | mem |
| 𐡍 | n | nun |
| 𐡎 | s | samekh |
| 𐡏 | ʿ | ayin |
| 𐡐 | p | pe |
| 𐡑 | ṣ | sadhe |
| 𐡒 | q | qoph |
| 𐡓 | r | resh |
| 𐡔 | š | shin |
| 𐡕 | t | taw |

- ʿ is a voiced sound squeezed in the throat, as Arabic ع. It is a letter of its own, not a vowel: 𐡏𐡁𐡃 *ʿbd* and 𐡀𐡁𐡃 *ʾbd* are two words.
- Three s: 𐡎 *s*, 𐡑 *ṣ*, an emphatic s, and 𐡔 *š*, the sound of English *sh*. You may type š as *sh*, ṣ as *s*.
- 𐡒 *q* is a k said deep in the throat.
- No letter changes its shape at the end of a word, unlike the later Hebrew script.

## Words of the documents

| Letters | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 𐡌𐡋𐡊 | mlk | king |
| 𐡊𐡎𐡐 | ksp | silver, money |
| 𐡎𐡐𐡓 | spr | document, letter, book |
| 𐡔𐡋𐡌 | šlm | peace, welfare |
| 𐡏𐡁𐡃 | ʿbd | servant |
| 𐡀𐡍𐡔 | ʾnš | man, person |

- 𐡊𐡎𐡐 *ksp* is silver, and money: the papyri weigh payments in silver.
- 𐡔𐡋𐡌 *šlm*, *šǝlām*, is the same word as Hebrew *šālōm* and Arabic *salām*. It opens the letters: the writer wishes the reader's welfare.
- A writer calls himself 𐡏𐡁𐡃 *ʿbd*, "your servant", when he writes to a superior.

::: summary
- 𐡋 l, 𐡌 m, 𐡍 n, 𐡎 s, 𐡏 ʿ, 𐡐 p, 𐡑 ṣ, 𐡒 q, 𐡓 r, 𐡔 š, 𐡕 t.
- ʿ is a consonant of the throat, kept apart from ʾ; three s: s, ṣ, š.
- 𐡌𐡋𐡊 *mlk* king, 𐡊𐡎𐡐 *ksp* silver, 𐡎𐡐𐡓 *spr* document, 𐡔𐡋𐡌 *šlm* peace, 𐡏𐡁𐡃 *ʿbd* servant, 𐡀𐡍𐡔 *ʾnš* man.
