# 3. A king, the king: the states of the noun

Aramaic has no word for "the". A noun takes an ending instead, and the script shows it: a final 𐡀 makes "a king" into "the king". This lesson reads the three forms of a noun and the little word that links two nouns.

## The emphatic state

| Form | Letters | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| absolute | 𐡌𐡋𐡊 | mlk | a king |
| emphatic | 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡀 | mlkʾ | the king |
| plural absolute | 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡉𐡍 | mlkyn | kings |
| plural emphatic | 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡉𐡀 | mlkyʾ | the kings |

- The *emphatic state* adds *-ā*, written 𐡀: 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡀 *mlkʾ*, *malkā*, "the king". Here ʾ is only the sign of the vowel.
- The plural adds *-īn*, written 𐡉𐡍; with "the", *-ayyā*, written 𐡉𐡀.
- Some nouns are found almost only in the emphatic: 𐡔𐡌𐡉𐡀 *šmyʾ*, *šǝmayyā*, "heaven".
- Middle Persian, written in a script that comes from Aramaic, kept the Aramaic word 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡀 as a written sign: scribes wrote *MLKʾ* and read the Persian word *šāh*.

- 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡉𐡀 → ((the kings* / a king / kings))

## Feminine nouns

| Letters | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 𐡀𐡂𐡓𐡄 | ʾgrh | a letter |
| 𐡀𐡂𐡓𐡕𐡀 | ʾgrtʾ | the letter |

- A feminine noun ends in *-ā*, written here with 𐡄: 𐡀𐡂𐡓𐡄 *ʾgrh*, *ʾiggǝrā*, "a letter". With "the", the *-t-* comes back: 𐡀𐡂𐡓𐡕𐡀 *ʾgrtʾ*, *ʾiggartā*.
- 𐡀𐡂𐡓𐡄 *ʾgrh* came from Akkadian *egirtu*, a word of the Assyrian chancery; 𐡎𐡐𐡓 *spr* is any written document.

## "Of": zy

| Letters | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 𐡁𐡉𐡕𐡀 𐡆𐡉 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡀 | bytʾ zy mlkʾ | the house of the king |
| 𐡁𐡉𐡕 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡀 | byt mlkʾ | the king's house |

- 𐡆𐡉 *zy* links a noun to the one that owns it, and opens a relative clause: "which, who". Imperial Aramaic writes it with 𐡆; later Aramaic writes 𐡃𐡉, *dī*.
- Two nouns side by side say the same: the first without an ending, the second with it.

::: summary
- No article: the emphatic state adds *-ā*, written ʾ: 𐡌𐡋𐡊 *mlk* a king, 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡀 *mlkʾ* the king.
- Plural 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡉𐡍 *mlkyn* kings, 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡉𐡀 *mlkyʾ* the kings; 𐡔𐡌𐡉𐡀 *šmyʾ* heaven.
- Feminine 𐡀𐡂𐡓𐡄 *ʾgrh* a letter, 𐡀𐡂𐡓𐡕𐡀 *ʾgrtʾ* the letter.
- 𐡆𐡉 *zy* of, which: 𐡁𐡉𐡕𐡀 𐡆𐡉 𐡌𐡋𐡊𐡀 *bytʾ zy mlkʾ* the house of the king.
:::
