# 4. The construct state and adjectives

"The king of the land", "the house of the god": Akkadian expresses "of" mostly by **shortening** the first noun. The possessed noun loses its case ending and stands in the **construct state**; the possessor follows in the genitive: *šar mātim* "king of the land". This is the Semitic genitive, the same as Hebrew *bēt lèḥem* "house of bread". Adjectives, on the other hand, follow their noun in full and agree with it.

## The construct state

| Free form | Construct | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *šarrum* | *šar* | *šar mātim* | king of the land |
| *bēlum* | *bēl* | *bēl ālim* | lord of the city |
| *bītum* | *bīt* | *bīt ilim* | house of the god, temple |
| *mārum* | *mār* | *mār awīlim* | son of a free man |
| *libbum* | *libbi* | *libbi ālim* | heart (centre) of the city |
| *šarratum* | *šarrat* | *šarrat šamê* | queen of heaven |

Rules: the case ending goes; a final double consonant takes *-i* (*libbi*); a feminine keeps *-at* (*šarrat*). In the plural the masculine keeps *-ū* / *-ī* without change: *mārū awīlim* "the sons of the man".

The construct is the tighter link; *ša* "that of" is looser and can separate the two nouns: *bītum ša šarrim* "the house of the king", *ṭuppum ša aḫīja* "my brother's tablet".

## Adjectives

An adjective follows its noun and agrees in gender, number and case. The masculine plural adjective has its own ending, *-ūtum*:

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| singular | *šarrum dannum* a mighty king | *šarratum dannatum* a mighty queen |
| plural | *šarrū dannūtum* mighty kings | *šarrātum dannātum* mighty queens |
| genitive singular | *ana šarrim dannim* | *ana šarratim dannatim* |

A construct chain with an adjective: *šar mātim rabītim* "king of the great land" (the adjective agrees with *mātum*), *šarrum rabûm ša mātim* "the great king of the land".

::: summary
- Construct state: the first noun loses its ending: *šar*, *bēl*, *bīt*, *mār*; *libbi*; feminine *šarrat*. The possessor follows in the genitive.
- *ša*: a looser "of": *bītum ša šarrim*.
- Adjectives follow and agree: *dannum*, *dannatum*, *dannūtum*, *dannātum*.
:::
