# 5. Pronouns and pronominal suffixes

Akkadian has independent pronouns for emphasis and, much more often, **pronominal suffixes** attached to nouns, prepositions and verbs. On a noun they mean "my, your, his"; on a verb they are its object. Every Semitic language works this way; the forms are close to their Hebrew and Arabic cousins (*-ka* "your", *-ni* "our").

## Independent pronouns

| Person | Pronoun | Person | Pronoun |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *anāku* | 1st pl. | *nīnu* |
| 2nd sg. m. | *atta* | 2nd pl. m. | *attunu* |
| 2nd sg. f. | *atti* | 2nd pl. f. | *attina* |
| 3rd sg. m. | *šū* | 3rd pl. m. | *šunu* |
| 3rd sg. f. | *šī* | 3rd pl. f. | *šina* |

They are used as subjects of sentences without a verb, *anāku šarrum* "I am the king", and for emphasis.

## Possessive suffixes on nouns

| Person | Suffix | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *-ī* (after a vowel *-ja*) | *bēlī* | my lord |
| 2nd sg. m. | *-ka* | *bēlka* | your lord |
| 2nd sg. f. | *-ki* | *bēlki* | your lord (to a woman) |
| 3rd sg. m. | *-šu* | *bēlšu* | his lord |
| 3rd sg. f. | *-ša* | *bēlša* | her lord |
| 1st pl. | *-ni* | *bēlni* | our lord |
| 2nd pl. | *-kunu* | *bēlkunu* | your lord |
| 3rd pl. m. | *-šunu* | *bēlšunu* | their lord |

In the nominative and accusative singular, the suffix is added to the short form of the noun (as in the construct): *bēl-šu*. In the genitive, the case vowel *-i-* stays: *ana bēli-šu* "to his lord". The plural keeps its ending: *mārū-šu* "his sons".

Some consonants assimilate: *bīt-šu* is written *bīssu* "his house"; *qāt-šu* becomes *qāssu* "his hand".

## Object suffixes on verbs

| Person | Suffix | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| me | *-anni*, *-ni* | *iṣbatanni* | he seized me |
| you (m.) | *-ka* | *iṣbatka* | he seized you |
| him | *-šu* | *iṣbassu* | he seized him |
| her | *-ši* | *iṣbassi* | he seized her |
| them | *-šunūti* | *iṣbassunūti* | he seized them |

You will meet the verb *iṣbat* "he seized" in lesson 7.

::: summary
- Independent pronouns: *anāku, atta, atti, šū, šī; nīnu, attunu, attina, šunu, šina*.
- Possessive suffixes: *-ī/-ja, -ka, -ki, -šu, -ša, -ni, -kunu, -šunu*; short noun in nominative and accusative (*bēlšu*), genitive vowel kept (*ana bēlišu*).
- Object suffixes on verbs: *-anni* me, *-ka* you, *-šu* him, *-ši* her, *-šunūti* them.
:::
