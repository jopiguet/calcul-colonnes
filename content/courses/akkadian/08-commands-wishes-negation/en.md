# 8. Commands, wishes and negation

Akkadian letters are full of orders and wishes: "send me…", "may the gods keep you", "do not neglect…". The **imperative** gives an order to "you"; the **precative** expresses a wish or an order for "he", "I" and "we"; the **prohibitive** forbids. And two negations divide the work: *ul* for statements, *lā* for everything else.

## The imperative

| Class | Masculine sg. | Feminine sg. | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| *u* (*iprus*) | *purus* | *pursī* | *pursā* |
| *a* (*iṣbat*) | *ṣabat* | *ṣabtī* | *ṣabtā* |
| *i* (*ipqid*) | *piqid* | *piqdī* | *piqdā* |
| *u* (*iškun*) | *šukun* | *šuknī* | *šuknā* |

The imperative is the preterite without its prefix, with the vowel repeated before the first radicals: *iprus* → *purus*, *iṣbat* → *ṣabat*. In letters it opens the request: *šupram* "write to me!" (*šupur* + *-am*, the ventive "to me").

## The precative

The precative is the preterite with the prefix *l-*:

| Person | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 3rd sg. | *liprus* | may he decide; let him decide |
| 3rd pl. | *liprusū* | may they decide |
| 1st sg. | *luprus* | let me decide; I want to decide |
| 1st pl. | *i niprus* | let us decide |

Letters greet with it: *ilū liballiṭūka* "may the gods keep you alive"; Šamaš *lišallimka* "may Shamash keep you well".

## Negation and prohibition

| Negation | Where | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *ul* | main clauses that state | *ul iprus* | he did not decide |
| *lā* | subordinate clauses, nouns, adjectives | *ša lā iprusu* | who did not decide |
| *lā* + durative | prohibition, "do not!" | *lā taparras* | do not decide! |
| *ē* + preterite | vetitive, "may … not" | *ē taprus* | you must not decide |

*lā* also makes negative words: *lā šēmûm* "disobedient" (not hearing).

::: summary
- Imperative: *purus, ṣabat, piqid, šukun*; feminine *-ī*, plural *-ā*; *šupram* write to me.
- Precative: *liprus* may he, *luprus* let me, *i niprus* let us.
- *ul* in main statements; *lā* elsewhere; *lā* + durative: do not!; *ē* + preterite: must not.
:::
