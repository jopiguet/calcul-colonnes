# 9. The stative

"I am well", "the city is taken", "you are king": Akkadian expresses a **state** with a special conjugation, the **stative**. It is built on the adjective or the verbal adjective and takes short endings of its own, which are related to the independent pronouns: *šalmāku* "I am well" (*anāku*). Almost every letter opens with it: *šalmāku* "I am fine", *ālum šalim* "the city is well".

## The forms

| Person | Ending | *damqum* good | *šalmum* well |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3rd sg. m. | none | *damiq* he is good | *šalim* he is well |
| 3rd sg. f. | *-at* | *damqat* | *šalmat* |
| 2nd sg. m. | *-āta* | *damqāta* | *šalmāta* |
| 2nd sg. f. | *-āti* | *damqāti* | *šalmāti* |
| 1st sg. | *-āku* | *damqāku* | *šalmāku* |
| 3rd pl. m. | *-ū* | *damqū* | *šalmū* |
| 3rd pl. f. | *-ā* | *damqā* | *šalmā* |
| 1st pl. | *-ānu* | *damqānu* | *šalmānu* |

The 3rd masculine singular has the pattern *paris*: *damiq*, *šalim*, *rabi* "is great". Before an ending the second vowel drops: *dam(i)q-at* → *damqat*.

## Stative of verbs

| Verb | Stative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *ṣabātum* seize | *ṣabit* | is seized; holds |
| *šakānum* place | *šakin* | is placed, lies |
| *parāsum* decide | *paris* | is decided, is separated |
| *wašābum* sit | *wašib* | is sitting, dwells |

With a transitive verb the stative is usually passive ("is seized"); with an intransitive one it is the resulting state ("is seated").

## Stative of nouns

Any noun can take stative endings: *šarrāku* "I am king", *mārāku* "I am a son", *awīlāta* "you are a man (behave like one!)".

::: summary
- Stative: a state; endings none, *-at*, *-āta*, *-āti*, *-āku*; *-ū*, *-ā*, *-ānu*.
- Pattern *paris*: *damiq*, *šalim*; vowel dropped before endings: *damqat*.
- Of verbs: passive or resulting state (*ṣabit* is seized); of nouns: *šarrāku* I am king.
:::
