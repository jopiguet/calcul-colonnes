# 7. The durative and the perfect

Akkadian has three tenses built on the same prefixes as the preterite. The **durative** *iparras*, with a doubled middle radical, says what happens, keeps happening or will happen: present and future. The **perfect** *iptaras*, with an infixed *-ta-*, says what has just happened, or what happened next in a sequence. Together with the preterite, they cover the time of every sentence in the letters and laws.

## The three tenses

| Tense | Pattern | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preterite | *iPRuS* | *iprus* | he decided |
| Durative | *iPaRRaS* | *iparras* | he decides, he will decide |
| Perfect | *iPtaRaS* | *iptaras* | he has decided |

| Person | Durative | Perfect |
|---|---|---|
| 3rd sg. | *iparras* | *iptaras* |
| 2nd sg. m. | *taparras* | *taptaras* |
| 1st sg. | *aparras* | *aptaras* |
| 3rd pl. m. | *iparrasū* | *iptarsū* |
| 1st pl. | *niparras* | *niptaras* |

The durative vowel follows the class: *iṣabbat* "he seizes" (class *a*), *ipaqqid* "he entrusts" (class *i*), *imaqqut* "he falls" (class *u*). The perfect keeps the vowel of the durative: *iṣṣabat* (*iṣtabat*, the *t* assimilates after *ṣ*), *iptaqid*, *imtaqut*.

## Using them

| Tense | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Preterite | a past event, narrative | *ālam iṣbat* he seized the city |
| Durative | present, habit, future; obligation in laws | *ālam iṣabbat* he will seize the city |
| Perfect | an event just past, or the next step | *ālam iṣṣabat* he has seized the city |

In letters, the perfect often reports the latest news: *ṭuppaka ešteme* "I have heard your tablet". In the laws, the durative gives the penalty: *iddâk* "he shall be killed".

::: summary
- Preterite *iprus* (past), durative *iparras* (present-future, doubled middle radical), perfect *iptaras* (just past, *-ta-* infix).
- The vowel follows the class: *iṣabbat*, *ipaqqid*, *imaqqut*; *iṣṣabat*, *iptaqid*, *imtaqut*.
- Narrative preterite, letters' perfect, laws' durative.
:::
