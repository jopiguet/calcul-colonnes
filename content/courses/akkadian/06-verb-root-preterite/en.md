# 6. The verb: roots and the preterite

The Akkadian verb is built like the Hebrew and Arabic one: a **root** of (usually) three consonants carries the meaning, and patterns of vowels, prefixes and doubled consonants carry the grammar. From *p-r-s* "decide" come *iprus* "he decided", *iparras* "he decides", *purus* "decide!", *parsum* "decided". Grammars use *p-r-s* as the model verb. This lesson gives the roots and the first tense, the **preterite**, the narrative past.

## Roots

| Root | Meaning | Infinitive |
|---|---|---|
| *p-r-s* | decide, separate | *parāsum* |
| *š-k-n* | place, set | *šakānum* |
| *ṣ-b-t* | seize, take | *ṣabātum* |
| *m-q-t* | fall | *maqātum* |
| *š-p-r* | send, write | *šapārum* |
| *k-š-d* | reach, conquer | *kašādum* |
| *p-q-d* | entrust, take care of | *paqādum* |

The infinitive pattern is *paRāSum*: the first and second radicals with *a*, the third with *ā*.

## The preterite iprus

| Person | Prefix + ending | *parāsum* | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3rd sg. | *i-* | *iprus* | he, she decided |
| 2nd sg. m. | *ta-* | *taprus* | you decided |
| 2nd sg. f. | *ta- … -ī* | *taprusī* | you (f.) decided |
| 1st sg. | *a-* | *aprus* | I decided |
| 3rd pl. m. | *i- … -ū* | *iprusū* | they decided |
| 3rd pl. f. | *i- … -ā* | *iprusā* | they (f.) decided |
| 2nd pl. | *ta- … -ā* | *taprusā* | you (pl.) decided |
| 1st pl. | *ni-* | *niprus* | we decided |

## Vowel classes

The vowel between the second and third radicals depends on the verb; dictionaries give it.

| Class | Preterite | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *u* | *iprus* | *imqut* | he fell |
| *a* | *iṣbat* | *iṣbat* | he seized |
| *i* | *ipqid* | *ipqid* | he entrusted |
| *u* | *iškun* | *iškun* | he placed |

The preterite tells what happened: *šarrum ālam iṣbat* "the king seized the city". The word order is subject, object, **verb last**.

::: summary
- Root of three radicals (*p-r-s*); infinitive *parāsum*.
- Preterite *iprus*: *i-*, *ta-*, *ta-…-ī*, *a-*; *i-…-ū*, *i-…-ā*, *ta-…-ā*, *ni-*.
- Vowel classes: *iprus*, *iṣbat*, *ipqid*, *imqut*. Verb last: *šarrum ālam iṣbat*.
:::
