# 14. Doer, done, to be done; when and while

Quechua makes nouns out of verbs with a handful of suffixes, and uses them where English has relative clauses, infinitives and "when". *takiq* is "one who sings", *mikhuna* "something to eat", *paraqtin* "when it rains". This lesson gives five of these forms.

## Nouns from verbs

| Suffix | Meaning | Example | |
|---|---|---|---|
| -q | one who does | takiq | singer |
| -sqa | done | yanusqa papa | cooked potatoes |
| -na | to be done; thing or place for | mikhuna | food |
| -y | the doing | yachay | knowing, learning |

- *-q* gives the doer: *llamk'aq* "worker", *yachachiq* "teacher".
- *-sqa* gives what was done: *yanusqa* "cooked", from *yanuy* "to cook".
- *-na* gives what is to be done, or what serves for it: *puñuna* "bed", *tiyana* "seat". With a possessive and *kan*, it says "have to": *Llamk'anay kan* "I have to work".
- *-y* with *-ta* is "to do" after *munay*: *Runasimita yachayta munani* "I want to learn Quechua".

## When and while

| Suffix | Use | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| -spa | same subject: while, by | Takispa llamk'ani. | I work singing. |
| -qti- | other subject: when, if | Paraqtin wasipi kasaq. | If it rains, I will stay at home. |

- *-spa* is used when both verbs have the same subject; it takes no person.
- *-qti-* is used when the subjects differ, and takes the possessive: *hamuqtiy* "when I come", *hamuqtiyki* "when you come", *hamuqtin* "when he comes".

::: exercise spa-qti
-spa or -qti-?

- Singing, I work. → ((Takispa* / Takiqtiy))
- When it rains, we stay. → ((Paraspa / Paraqtin*))
- When my mother comes, we eat. → ((Mamay hamuspa / Mamay hamuqtin*))
- I walk, listening. → ((Uyarispa* / Uyariqtiy))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- Runasimita yachayta munani. → {{I want to learn Quechua.}}
- Llamk'anay kan. → {{I have to work. / I must work.}}
- Paraqtin wasipi kasaq. → {{If it rains, I will stay at home. / When it rains, I'll be at home.}}

:::

::: summary
- *-q* doer (*takiq*), *-sqa* done (*yanusqa*), *-na* to be done (*mikhuna*, *Llamk'anay kan*), *-y* the doing (*yachayta munani*).
- *-spa* while, same subject: *Takispa llamk'ani*.
- *-qti-* when, if, other subject: *Paraqtin*, *hamuqtiy*.
:::
