# 10. Two pasts: -rqa- and -sqa-

Quechua has two past tenses, and the choice between them follows the same logic as the evidentials. *-rqa-* tells what one witnessed; *-sqa-* tells what one did not see, learned afterwards, or discovers with surprise. Every tale begins with *-sqa-*.

## The witnessed past: -rqa-

| Person | puñuy, to sleep | |
|---|---|---|
| ñuqa | puñurqani | I slept |
| qam | puñurqanki | you slept |
| pay | puñurqan | he, she slept |
| ñuqanchik | puñurqanchik | we slept (you included) |
| ñuqayku | puñurqayku | we slept (you excluded) |
| qamkuna | puñurqankichik | you all slept |
| paykuna | puñurqanku | they slept |

*-rqa-* comes between the stem and the person endings of the present.

## The past not witnessed: -sqa-

| Person | kay, to be | |
|---|---|---|
| ñuqa | kasqani | I was (without knowing it) |
| qam | kasqanki | you were |
| pay | kasqa | he, she, it was |
| ñuqanchik | kasqanchik | we were (you included) |
| ñuqayku | kasqayku | we were (you excluded) |
| qamkuna | kasqankichik | you all were |
| paykuna | kasqaku | they were |

- The third persons are *-sqa* and *-sqaku*, with no *-n*.
- *-sqa-* is the past of tales, of what one was told, and of what one finds out: the cat *had eaten* the meat, one sees only the empty plate.
- With *ñuqa*, it says one did not notice: *Puñusqani* "I fell asleep (without realising)".
- A tale opens with *Huk kutis … kasqa* "Once, they say, there was …".

::: exercise sqa
Write the form with -sqa-.

- there was, in a tale → [[kasqa]]
- they had gone, it turns out → [[risqaku]]
- he had eaten → [[mikhusqa]]
- I had slept, without knowing → [[puñusqani]]
:::

::: exercise choose

:::

## Words of time

| Quechua | Meaning |
|---|---|
| qayna p'unchaw | yesterday |
| p'unchaw | day |
| wata | year |
| ñawpaq | before, formerly |
| huk kuti | once, one time |

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- Qayna p'unchaw Qusquman rirqani. → {{Yesterday I went to Cusco. / I went to Cusco yesterday.}}
- Huk kutis huk atuq kasqa. → {{Once upon a time there was a fox. / Once there was a fox.}}
- Misi aychata mikhusqa. → {{The cat had eaten the meat. / The cat ate the meat.}}
- Ñawpaqqa urqupi tiyarqayku. → {{Before, we lived in the mountains. / We used to live in the mountains.}}
:::

::: summary
- *-rqa-*, the past one witnessed: *puñurqani, puñurqanki, puñurqan …*.
- *-sqa-*, the past not witnessed, found out or told: *kasqa, kasqaku*; the past of tales.
- *Huk kutis huk atuq kasqa* "Once upon a time there was a fox".
:::
