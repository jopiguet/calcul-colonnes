# 12. Suffixes inside the verb

Between the stem and the tense, a Quechua verb can take suffixes that change its meaning: do it to oneself, make someone do it, do it for someone, bring it here. From *yachay* "to know", *yacha-chi-y* "to make know" is "to teach", and *yacha-chi-q*, "one who makes know", is a teacher. This lesson gives the most useful of these suffixes.

## Seven suffixes

| Suffix | Effect | From | To |
|---|---|---|---|
| -ku- | to or for oneself | armay, to bathe someone | armakuy, to bathe |
| -naku- | each other | maqay, to hit | maqanakuy, to fight |
| -chi- | to make someone do | mikhuy, to eat | mikhuchiy, to feed |
| -pu- | for someone | rantiy, to buy | rantipuy, to buy for someone |
| -mu- | towards here, go and do | apay, to carry | apamuy, to bring |
| -ysi- | to help to do | llamk'ay, to work | llamk'aysiy, to help work |
| -ri- | to begin, a little | rimay, to speak | rimariy, to start speaking |

- These suffixes come before tense and person: *mikhu-chi-chka-ni* "I am feeding".
- Some meanings have grown apart: *rantikuy* "to sell", from *rantiy* "to buy".
- *kuyay* "to love", *kuyanakuy* "to love each other".

## Building a verb

| Quechua | Pieces | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Yakuta apamuy! | apa-mu-y | Bring water! |
| Wawata mikhuchichkani. | mikhu-chi-chka-ni | I am feeding the child. |
| Kuyanakuyku. | kuya-naku-yku | We love each other. |
| Runasimita yachachin. | yacha-chi-n | She teaches Quechua. |

:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- Mamay t'antata rantikun. → {{My mother sells bread.}}
- Wawakuna maqanakunku. → {{The children fight. / The children are fighting each other.}}
- Taytay papata apamun. → {{My father brings potatoes.}}
- Yachachiqqa wawakunata asichin. → {{The teacher makes the children laugh.}}
:::

::: summary
- *-ku-* oneself, *-naku-* each other, *-chi-* make do, *-pu-* for someone, *-mu-* towards here, *-ysi-* help, *-ri-* begin.
- They come before tense and person: *mikhu-chi-chka-ni*.
- *yachay* know, *yachachiy* teach, *yachachiq* teacher; *rantiy* buy, *rantikuy* sell.
:::
