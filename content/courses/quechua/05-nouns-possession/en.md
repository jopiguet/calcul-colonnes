# 5. Plural and possession

Where English puts "my" before a noun, Quechua adds a suffix after it: *wasi* "house", *wasiy* "my house". Suffixes then follow each other in a fixed order, which is the heart of the language. This lesson gives the plural, the seven possessive suffixes, and how to say "I have".

## The plural: -kuna

*-kuna* makes a plural: *wasikuna* "houses", *runakuna* "people", *allqukuna* "dogs". After a number, the plural is not needed: *huk wasi* "one house, a house"; lesson 15 gives the other numbers.

## The possessive suffixes

| Owner | After a vowel: wasi | After a consonant: atuq |
|---|---|---|
| my | wasiy | atuqniy |
| your | wasiyki | atuqniyki |
| his, her | wasin | atuqnin |
| our (you included) | wasinchik | atuqninchik |
| our (you excluded) | wasiyku | atuqniyku |
| your (all) | wasiykichik | atuqniykichik |
| their | wasinku | atuqninku |

- The endings are those of the verb (lesson 4), with *-y*, *-yki*, *-ykichik* in place of *-ni*, *-nki*, *-nkichik*.
- After a consonant, *-ni-* comes between: *atuq-ni-y* "my fox". Few Quechua nouns end in a consonant.
- The possessive comes before the plural: *wasi-y-kuna* "my houses", *allqu-yki-kuna* "your dogs".

## To have

Quechua has no verb "to have": it says that the thing owned exists, with *kan* "there is":

| Quechua | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Huk wasiymi kan. | I have a house (a my-house there is). |
| Huk allquykichu kan? | Do you have a dog? |
| Mana wasiy kanchu. | I do not have a house. |
| Wawanmi kan. | She has a child. |

::: exercise plural
Write the Quechua.

- my houses → [[wasiykuna]]
- people → [[runakuna]]
- your dogs → [[allquykikuna]]
- towns → [[llaqtakuna]]
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- Huk allquymi kan. → {{I have a dog.}}
- Mana wasiy kanchu. → {{I don't have a house. / I do not have a house.}}
- Wasiykiqa sumaqmi. → {{Your house is beautiful.}}
- Misinqa huch'uymi. → {{Her cat is small. / His cat is small.}}
:::

::: summary
- Plural *-kuna*; none after a number: *huk wasi*.
- Possessive: *-y, -yki, -n, -nchik, -yku, -ykichik, -nku*; after a consonant *-ni-* first: *atuqniy*.
- Order: noun, possessive, plural: *wasiykuna*.
- "I have": *Huk wasiymi kan*; "I don't have": *Mana wasiy kanchu*.
:::
