# 15. Numbers, family and time

The Inca state counted in tens, and recorded its counts on knotted cords, the *khipu*. Quechua numbers are still entirely decimal. The family words, by contrast, depend on who speaks: a man and a woman do not use the same word for "my sister". This lesson gives both, and the words of the day.

## Numbers

| 1–4 | 5–8 | 9, 10 | 100, 1000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 huk | 5 pichqa | 9 isqun | 100 pachak |
| 2 iskay | 6 suqta | 10 chunka | 1000 waranqa |
| 3 kimsa | 7 qanchis | | |
| 4 tawa | 8 pusaq | | |

- Tens: *iskay chunka* 20, *kimsa chunka* 30.
- Units after ten take *-yuq* "having", or *-niyuq* after a consonant: *chunka hukniyuq* 11, *chunka iskayniyuq* 12, *chunka kimsayuq* 13, *iskay chunka pichqayuq* 25.
- After a number, no plural: *kimsa wasi* "three houses".
- Age is said with *-yuq*: *Iskay chunka watayuqmi kani* "I am twenty (having twenty years)".

## The family

| | Said by a man | Said by a woman |
|---|---|---|
| brother | wawqi | turi |
| sister | pana | ñaña |
| son | churi | wawa |
| daughter | ususi | wawa |

*tayta* and *mama* are father and mother; *hatun tayta* and *hatun mama* grandfather and grandmother; *qhari* man, *warmi* woman, wife.

::: exercise sibling
Juan is a man, Rosa a woman. Which word?

- Juan's sister → ((pana* / ñaña))
- Rosa's sister → ((pana / ñaña*))
- Rosa's brother → ((wawqi / turi*))
- Juan's brother → ((wawqi* / turi))
:::

## The day

| Quechua | Meaning |
|---|---|
| tutamanta | in the morning |
| chawpi p'unchaw | noon |
| ch'isi | evening |
| tuta | night |

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- Kimsa wawqiymi kan. → {{I have three brothers.}}
- Pachak wata. → {{A hundred years. / One hundred years.}}
- Tutamanta llamk'ani. → {{I work in the morning.}}
- Iskay chunka watayuqmi kani. → {{I am twenty years old. / I am twenty.}}
:::

::: summary
- huk, iskay, kimsa, tawa, pichqa, suqta, qanchis, pusaq, isqun, chunka; pachak 100, waranqa 1000.
- 11 *chunka hukniyuq*, 20 *iskay chunka*; no plural after a number.
- A man says *wawqi, pana, churi, ususi*; a woman *turi, ñaña, wawa*.
- *tutamanta, chawpi p'unchaw, ch'isi, tuta*.
:::
