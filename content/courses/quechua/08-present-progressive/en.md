# 8. The present and the progressive

A Quechua verb is a stem followed by suffixes: tense, then person. The dictionary form ends in *-y*: *rimay* "to speak", stem *rima-*. This lesson gives the present, which every other tense builds on, and *-chka-*, which says that something is going on right now.

## The present

| Person | rimay, to speak | |
|---|---|---|
| ñuqa | rimani | I speak |
| qam | rimanki | you speak |
| pay | riman | he, she speaks |
| ñuqanchik | rimanchik | we speak (you included) |
| ñuqayku | rimayku | we speak (you excluded) |
| qamkuna | rimankichik | you all speak |
| paykuna | rimanku | they speak |

Every verb follows this pattern; there are almost no irregular verbs. The present covers what one does now and what one does as a habit: *Runasimita rimani* "I speak Quechua".

## Going on now: -chka-

*-chka-* comes between the stem and the person: *rima-chka-ni* "I am speaking". With *kay*, it gives "to be, right now, somewhere": *Wasipin kachkani* "I am at home"; *Imaynallan kachkanki?* "How are you?" Bolivian Quechua often says *-sha-*: *rimashani*.

| Verb | Meaning | Progressive |
|---|---|---|
| upyay | to drink | upyachkani, I am drinking |
| puñuy | to sleep | puñuchkan, she is sleeping |
| ruway | to do, make | ruwachkanki, you are doing |
| takiy | to sing | takichkanku, they are singing |
| tusuy | to dance | tusuchkanchik, we are dancing |
| rikuy | to see | rikuchkayku, we are seeing |

::: summary
- Present: stem + *-ni, -nki, -n, -nchik, -yku, -nkichik, -nku*: *rimani, rimanki, riman*.
- *-chka-* "right now": *rimachkani* "I am speaking"; *kachkani* "I am (here, now)".
- Verbs: *rimay, llamk'ay, upyay, puñuy, ruway, takiy, tusuy, rikuy*.
:::
