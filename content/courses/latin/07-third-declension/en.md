# 7. The third declension

The third declension is the largest and the least predictable at first sight: its nominatives take every shape (*rēx*, *corpus*, *nōmen*, *homō*, *cīvitās*). The rest is regular. The key is the genitive: drop its **-is** and you have the stem for every other case.

## Finding the stem

| Nominative | Genitive | Stem | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| rēx | rēg**is** | rēg- | king |
| mīles | mīlit**is** | mīlit- | soldier |
| homō | homin**is** | homin- | human being |
| cīvitās | cīvitāt**is** | cīvitāt- | state, community |
| pater | patr**is** | patr- | father |
| corpus | corpor**is** | corpor- | body |
| nōmen | nōmin**is** | nōmin- | name |

This is why a third-declension noun must be learnt with its genitive: from *nōmen* alone you cannot guess *nōmin-*.

## The endings

| Case | Masc. / fem. sg. | Masc. / fem. pl. | Neuter sg. | Neuter pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | rēx | rēg**ēs** | corpus | corpor**a** |
| Genitive | rēg**is** | rēg**um** | corpor**is** | corpor**um** |
| Dative | rēg**ī** | rēg**ibus** | corpor**ī** | corpor**ibus** |
| Accusative | rēg**em** | rēg**ēs** | corpus | corpor**a** |
| Ablative | rēg**e** | rēg**ibus** | corpor**e** | corpor**ibus** |

Watch three traps: the ablative singular *-e* (not *-ō*); the nominative and accusative plural *-ēs* (identical); the genitive plural *-um* (not *-ōrum*). Neuters follow the neuter rule of lesson 5: nominative = accusative, plural in *-a*.

## Gender

The gender of a third-declension noun must be learnt, but the nominative often hints at it:

| Ending | Usually | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| *-or*, *-ōs*, *-er*, *-es* (gen. *-itis*) | masculine | *amor*, *mōs*, *pater*, *mīles* |
| *-tās*, *-tūs*, *-tiō*, *-dō*, *-gō* | feminine | *cīvitās*, *virtūs*, *ōrātiō*, *multitūdō*, *virgō* |
| *-us* (gen. *-oris*, *-eris*), *-men*, *-e*, *-al*, *-ar* | neuter | *corpus*, *genus*, *nōmen*, *mare*, *animal* |

Persons follow their sex: *pater* and *frāter* are masculine, *māter* and *soror* feminine.

## Adjectives with third-declension nouns

An adjective in *-us, -a, -um* keeps its own endings: it agrees in gender, number and case, not in sound. *rēx bonus*, *rēgis bonī*, *rēgī bonō*; *corpus magnum*, *corpora magna*; *māter cāra*, *mātrem cāram*.

:::

::: summary
- Third declension: genitive singular in *-is*; the stem is the genitive minus *-is*. Learn every noun with its genitive.
- Masc./fem.: -, -is, -ī, -em, -e; -ēs, -um, -ibus, -ēs, -ibus. Neuter: nom. = acc., plural *-a*.
- Gender: *-or, -er, -ōs* masc.; *-tās, -tūs, -tiō, -dō* fem.; *-us, -men, -e, -al* neut.
- Adjectives keep their own declension: *rēx bonus*, *rēgem bonum*.
:::
