# 13. Third-declension i-stems and adjectives

Some third-declension nouns once had a stem ending in *-i*. It survives in a few cases: a genitive plural in **-ium**, and for neuters an ablative in **-ī** and a plural in **-ia**. The same *i* runs through the adjectives of the third declension: *omnis* (all), *ācer* (sharp), *fēlīx* (lucky), *ingēns* (huge).

## Which nouns are i-stems

| Rule | Examples | Genitive plural |
|---|---|---|
| Same number of syllables in nom. and gen. (parisyllables), nom. in *-is* or *-ēs* | *cīvis, cīvis*; *hostis, hostis*; *nāvis, nāvis* | *cīvium, hostium, nāvium* |
| Nominative in *-s* or *-x* after two consonants | *urbs, urbis*; *mōns, montis*; *nox, noctis* | *urbium, montium, noctium* |
| Neuters in *-e*, *-al*, *-ar* | *mare, maris*; *animal, animālis* | *marium, animālium* |

Masculine and feminine i-stems differ from *rēx* only in the genitive plural (and sometimes an accusative plural *-īs* for *-ēs*). Neuter i-stems differ more:

| Case | cīvis (m.) sg. | cīvis pl. | mare (n.) sg. | mare pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | cīvis | cīvēs | mare | mar**ia** |
| Genitive | cīvis | cīv**ium** | maris | mar**ium** |
| Dative | cīvī | cīvibus | marī | maribus |
| Accusative | cīvem | cīvēs (-īs) | mare | mar**ia** |
| Ablative | cīve | cīvibus | mar**ī** | maribus |

## Adjectives of the third declension

They decline like i-stems, with an ablative singular in **-ī** in all genders. They come with three, two or one nominative forms:

| Kind | Masc. | Fem. | Neut. | Dictionary entry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| three endings | ācer | ācris | ācre | *ācer, ācris, ācre* |
| two endings | omnis | omnis | omne | *omnis, omne* |
| one ending | fēlīx | fēlīx | fēlīx | *fēlīx, gen. fēlīcis* |

| Case | omnis (m./f.) sg. | omne (n.) sg. | omnēs (m./f.) pl. | omnia (n.) pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | omnis | omne | omnēs | omn**ia** |
| Genitive | omnis | omnis | omn**ium** | omn**ium** |
| Dative | omnī | omnī | omnibus | omnibus |
| Accusative | omnem | omne | omnēs (-īs) | omn**ia** |
| Ablative | omn**ī** | omn**ī** | omnibus | omnibus |

One-ending adjectives show their stem in the genitive: *fēlīx, fēlīcis*; *ingēns, ingentis*; *pār, paris* (equal); *vetus, veteris* (old, which is not an i-stem: *vetere*, *veterum*, *vetera*).

::: exercise adjectives
Make the adjective agree.

- *cum mīlite* … (brave: *fortis, forte*) → ((fortī* / forte / fortem))
- *… animālia* (all: *omnis, omne*) → ((omnēs / omnia* / omnium))
- *urbis* … (huge: *ingēns, ingentis*) → ((ingentis* / ingentī / ingēns))
- *… cīvēs* (lucky: *fēlīx, fēlīcis*) → ((fēlīcia / fēlīcēs* / fēlīx))
- *in marī* … (sharp, fierce: *ācer, ācris, ācre*) → ((ācrī* / ācre / ācrem))
:::

## Mixing declensions in agreement

An adjective of the third declension can go with a noun of any declension, and the reverse; endings need not rhyme. *Puella fortis*, *puellae fortēs*; *vir fortis*, *virō fortī*; *cīvis bonus*, *cīvēs bonī*; *omnia bella*.

:::

:::

::: summary
- i-stems: parisyllables in *-is, -ēs*; nominatives in *-s, -x* after two consonants; neuters in *-e, -al, -ar*.
- Genitive plural *-ium*; neuters: ablative *-ī*, plural *-ia*.
- Third-declension adjectives: three endings (*ācer, ācris, ācre*), two (*omnis, omne*), one (*fēlīx, -īcis*); ablative singular *-ī*.
- Any adjective can go with any noun: agreement is in gender, number and case.
:::
