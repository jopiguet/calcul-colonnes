# 16. The fourth and fifth declensions

Two small declensions complete the noun system. The fourth (genitive in **-ūs**) holds many words for actions and groups: *exercitus* (army), *senātus*, *manus* (hand; band), *domus* (house). The fifth (genitive in **-eī**) is the smallest, but it contains two of the most frequent nouns of all: *rēs* (thing, matter) and *diēs* (day).

## The fourth declension

| Case | manus (f.) sg. | manus pl. | cornū (n.) sg. | cornū pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | man**us** | man**ūs** | corn**ū** | corn**ua** |
| Genitive | man**ūs** | man**uum** | corn**ūs** | corn**uum** |
| Dative | man**uī** | man**ibus** | corn**ū** | corn**ibus** |
| Accusative | man**um** | man**ūs** | corn**ū** | corn**ua** |
| Ablative | man**ū** | man**ibus** | corn**ū** | corn**ibus** |

Most fourth-declension nouns are masculine (*exercitus, senātus, cursus, metus, impetus, frūctus, ūsus*); *manus* and *domus* are feminine; a few neuters end in *-ū* (*cornū*, horn; *genū*, knee).

*Manus* and *dominus* both end in *-us*: only the genitive (*manūs, dominī*) tells them apart. The long *u* of *manūs* (genitive singular, nominative and accusative plural) is invisible without macrons; read the context.

*Domus* mixes the second and fourth declensions; three forms are fixed expressions: *domī* (at home), *domum* (home, homeward), *domō* (from home).

## The fifth declension

| Case | rēs (f.) sg. | rēs pl. | diēs (m.) sg. | diēs pl. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominative | r**ēs** | r**ēs** | di**ēs** | di**ēs** |
| Genitive | r**eī** | r**ērum** | di**ēī** | di**ērum** |
| Dative | r**eī** | r**ēbus** | di**ēī** | di**ēbus** |
| Accusative | r**em** | r**ēs** | di**em** | di**ēs** |
| Ablative | r**ē** | r**ēbus** | di**ē** | di**ēbus** |

Fifth-declension nouns are feminine, except *diēs* (usually masculine) and *merīdiēs* (noon). Others: *spēs* (hope), *fidēs* (faith, trust), *aciēs* (battle line), *faciēs* (face).

*Rēs* means almost anything; the context and an adjective decide:

| Latin | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *rēs pūblica* | the state, the commonwealth |
| *rēs gestae* | deeds, achievements |
| *rēs mīlitāris* | warfare |
| *rē vērā* | in fact, really |
| *quam ob rem* | for which reason, therefore |
| *rēs adversae, secundae* | misfortune, good fortune |

::: summary
- Fourth declension: genitive *-ūs*; *-us, -ūs, -uī, -um, -ū; -ūs, -uum, -ibus, -ūs, -ibus*; neuters in *-ū*. Mostly masculine; *manus, domus* feminine.
- *Domī* at home, *domum* homeward, *domō* from home.
- Fifth declension: genitive *-eī*; *-ēs, -eī, -eī, -em, -ē; -ēs, -ērum, -ēbus, -ēs, -ēbus*. Feminine except *diēs*.
- *Rēs pūblica, rēs gestae, rē vērā, quam ob rem*.
:::
