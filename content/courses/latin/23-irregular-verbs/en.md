# 23. Irregular verbs: volō, nōlō, mālō, ferō, eō, fīō

A few of the most frequent verbs break the patterns of the four conjugations, mostly in the present system. Like *sum* and *possum*, they are learned as paradigms. Good news: their perfect systems are regular (*voluī, tulī, iī*), so the irregularity is limited to a few tenses.

## Volō, nōlō, mālō

*Volō* "I want", *nōlō* "I do not want" (*nē* + *volō*), *mālō* "I prefer" (*magis* + *volō*).

| Person | volō | nōlō | mālō |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | volō | nōlō | mālō |
| 2nd sg. | vīs | nōn vīs | māvīs |
| 3rd sg. | vult | nōn vult | māvult |
| 1st pl. | volumus | nōlumus | mālumus |
| 2nd pl. | vultis | nōn vultis | māvultis |
| 3rd pl. | volunt | nōlunt | mālunt |
| Infinitive | velle | nōlle | mālle |
| Present subjunctive | velim, velīs, velit | nōlim | mālim |
| Imperfect subjunctive | vellem | nōllem | māllem |
| Perfect | voluī | nōluī | māluī |

The imperfect and future are regular: *volēbam, volam, volēs*. *Nōlī, nōlīte* + infinitive is the polite negative command: *Nōlī timēre*, do not be afraid (lesson 24).

## Ferō

*Ferō, ferre, tulī, lātum*, "to carry, bear, endure". The present drops the connecting vowel in a few forms:

| Person | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | ferō | feror |
| 2nd sg. | **fers** | **ferris** |
| 3rd sg. | **fert** | **fertur** |
| 1st pl. | ferimus | ferimur |
| 2nd pl. | **fertis** | feriminī |
| 3rd pl. | ferunt | feruntur |

Infinitive *ferre* (passive *ferrī*), imperative *fer, ferte*; imperfect subjunctive *ferrem*. The principal parts are suppletive: *tulī* and *lātum* come from other roots. Compounds are common: *afferō* (bring), *auferō* (take away), *cōnferō* (bring together; compare), *referō* (bring back, report), *offerō* (offer). *Fertur* often means "he is said to".

## Eō and fīō

*Eō, īre, iī (īvī), itum*, "to go". The stem is *i-*, which becomes *e-* before a vowel:

| Person | Present | Imperfect | Future | Present subjunctive |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | eō | ībam | ībō | eam |
| 2nd sg. | īs | ībās | ībis | eās |
| 3rd sg. | it | ībat | ībit | eat |
| 1st pl. | īmus | ībāmus | ībimus | eāmus |
| 2nd pl. | ītis | ībātis | ībitis | eātis |
| 3rd pl. | eunt | ībant | ībunt | eant |

Present participle *iēns, euntis*. Its compounds are everywhere: *abeō* (go away), *adeō* (approach), *exeō* (go out), *redeō* (go back), *pereō* (perish), *trānseō* (cross).

*Fīō, fierī, factus sum*, "to become, happen; be made", serves as the passive of *faciō* in the present system: *fit, fīunt, fīēbat, fiet; fieret*. *Quid fit?* What is happening? *Cōnsul factus est.* He was made consul.

::: summary
- *Volō, vīs, vult; velle; velim*. *Nōlō* (*nōn vīs, nōn vult*), *mālō* (*māvīs, māvult*).
- *Ferō, fers, fert; ferre, tulī, lātum*; compounds *afferō, auferō, cōnferō, referō, offerō*.
- *Eō, īs, it, īmus, ītis, eunt; ībam, ībō; eam*; compounds *abeō, exeō, redeō, pereō, trānseō*.
- *Fīō, fierī, factus sum*: become, happen; passive of *faciō*.
:::
