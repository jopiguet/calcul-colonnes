# 6. The imperfect and the future

Two more tenses come from the same stem as the present: the imperfect, for what was going on or used to happen, and the future. For the first two conjugations, both are made with a syllable containing **b**. *Sum* and *possum* have their own forms.

## The imperfect: *-ba-*

The imperfect adds **-ba-** to the present stem, then the personal endings (with *-m* in the first person).

| Person | amāre | monēre |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | amā**bam** | monē**bam** |
| 2nd sg. | amā**bās** | monē**bās** |
| 3rd sg. | amā**bat** | monē**bat** |
| 1st pl. | amā**bāmus** | monē**bāmus** |
| 2nd pl. | amā**bātis** | monē**bātis** |
| 3rd pl. | amā**bant** | monē**bant** |

The imperfect describes an action in progress or repeated in the past: *ambulābam* "I was walking", "I used to walk", "I would walk". It sets the scene: *Puerī in agrīs labōrābant* "the boys were working in the fields".

## The future: *-bō, -bis, -bit*

The first and second conjugations make their future with **-b-** and the vowels *ō, i, i, i, i, u*:

| Person | amāre | monēre |
|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | amā**bō** | monē**bō** |
| 2nd sg. | amā**bis** | monē**bis** |
| 3rd sg. | amā**bit** | monē**bit** |
| 1st pl. | amā**bimus** | monē**bimus** |
| 2nd pl. | amā**bitis** | monē**bitis** |
| 3rd pl. | amā**bunt** | monē**bunt** |

Learn the vowels as a chant: *bō, bis, bit, bimus, bitis, bunt*. Careful: the third and fourth conjugations make their future differently (lesson 8).

## *Sum* and *possum*

*Sum* has the stem *er-* in both tenses: imperfect *eram*, future *erō*. *Possum* puts *pot-* in front.

| Person | Imperfect of sum | Future of sum | Imperfect of possum | Future of possum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | eram | erō | poteram | poterō |
| 2nd sg. | erās | eris | poterās | poteris |
| 3rd sg. | erat | erit | poterat | poterit |
| 1st pl. | erāmus | erimus | poterāmus | poterimus |
| 2nd pl. | erātis | eritis | poterātis | poteritis |
| 3rd pl. | erant | erunt | poterant | poterunt |

## Time words

| Latin | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *nunc* | now |
| *tum* | then, at that time |
| *iam* | already, now |
| *mox* | soon |
| *crās* | tomorrow |
| *herī* | yesterday |
| *diū* | for a long time |

*Herī in forō erāmus; crās in agrīs labōrābimus.* Yesterday we were in the forum; tomorrow we shall work in the fields.

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- *Ōlim agricolae in agrīs labōrābant.* → {{Once the farmers worked in the fields. / Once upon a time the farmers were working in the fields. / Once the farmers used to work in the fields.}}
- *Crās amīcōs vidēbimus.* → {{Tomorrow we will see our friends. / Tomorrow we shall see the friends. / We will see the friends tomorrow.}}
- *Puer laetus nōn erat.* → {{The boy was not happy.}}
- *Mox poterit cantāre.* → {{Soon he will be able to sing. / Soon she will be able to sing.}}
:::

::: summary
- Imperfect, 1st and 2nd conjugations: stem + *-bam, -bās, -bat, -bāmus, -bātis, -bant*: an action going on or repeated in the past.
- Future, 1st and 2nd conjugations: stem + *-bō, -bis, -bit, -bimus, -bitis, -bunt*.
- *Sum*: *eram…* and *erō, eris, erit, erimus, eritis, erunt*; *possum*: *poteram…*, *poterō…*.
:::
