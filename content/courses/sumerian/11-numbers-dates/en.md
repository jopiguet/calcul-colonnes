# 11. Numbers, measures and year names

The Sumerians counted in sixties. Two wedges are enough: a vertical wedge for 1 and a corner wedge (*Winkelhaken*) for 10, and their position tells their size, as in our place-value system but with base 60 and without zero. Our 60 minutes and 360 degrees come from it. This lesson gives the numerals, the words for the numbers, the main units of measure, and the way years were named, the key to dating any Sumerian tablet.

## Signs and place value

| Signs | Value |
|---|---|
| 𒁹 | 1 |
| 𒁹𒁹𒁹 | 3 |
| 𒌋 | 10 |
| 𒌋𒌋𒁹𒁹 | 22 |
| 𒁹 𒌋 (1 in the sixties place, then 10) | 60 + 10 = 70 |
| 𒁹𒁹 𒌋𒌋𒌋 | 2 × 60 + 30 = 150 |

A vertical wedge can be 1, 60, 3,600: the place, and the context, decide. Scribes calculated with tables of reciprocals and multiplication in base 60; school tablets from the *e₂-dub-ba* are full of them.

## Number words

| Value | Word | Value | Word |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *diš*, *aš* | 10 | *u* |
| 2 | *min* | 20 | *niš* |
| 3 | *eš₅* | 30 | *ušu₃* |
| 4 | *limmu₂* | 40 | *nimin* |
| 5 | *ia₂* | 50 | *ninnu* |
| 6 | *aš₃* | 60 | *ĝeš₂* |
| 7 | *imin* | 600 | *ĝeš₂-u* |
| 8 | *ussu* | 3,600 | *šar₂* |
| 9 | *ilimmu* | | |

*6* is *ia₂* + *aš* (5 + 1), *7* *imin* (5 + 2), *9* *ilimmu* (5 + 4): traces of an older counting by fives.

## Measures

| Unit | Measures | Value |
|---|---|---|
| *sila₃* | capacity | about 1 litre |
| *gur* | capacity | 300 *sila₃* in the Ur III period |
| *gin₂* | weight, the shekel | about 8.3 g |
| *ma-na* | weight, the mina | 60 *gin₂*, about 500 g |
| *gu₂* | weight, the talent | 60 *ma-na*, about 30 kg |
| *še* | barley; also the smallest weight, the grain | 1/180 *gin₂* |

Barley (*še*) and silver (*ku₃-babbar*) served as money: a worker's ration was counted in *sila₃* of barley a day.

## Year names

Before eras, a year was named after an event of the year before: *mu* "year" + a sentence.

| Year name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *mu bad₃ ma-da ba-du₃* | Year: the wall of the land was built |
| *mu* ᵈ*nanna* … *ba-ḫun* | Year: the priestess of Nanna was installed |
| *mu us₂-sa* … | the year after (the year in which) … |

Months are *iti*, days *u₄*: *iti še-kin-ku₅* "month of the barley harvest".

::: summary
- Base 60 with place value, no zero: 𒁹 1 (or 60, 3,600), 𒌋 10.
- *diš* 1, *min* 2, *eš₅* 3, *limmu₂* 4, *ia₂* 5, *u* 10, *ĝeš₂* 60, *šar₂* 3,600.
- *sila₃* ≈ 1 l; *gin₂* shekel ≈ 8.3 g; *ma-na* = 60 *gin₂*; *gu₂* = 60 *ma-na*; barley and silver as money.
- Years named after events: *mu* … *ba-du₃*; months *iti*, days *u₄*.
:::
