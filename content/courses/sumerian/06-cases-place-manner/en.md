# 6. Cases II: place, direction, company, likeness

Six more case endings say **where**, **where to**, **where from**, **with whom** and **like what**. They close the noun chain like the cases of lesson 5, so a whole phrase, adjective and possessive included, stands in the case: *e₂ gibil-a-ni-a* "in his new house". Several of these cases are also repeated inside the verb as prefixes, which you will meet in lesson 10.

## The cases

| Case | Ending | Meaning | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| locative | *-a* | in, at, on | *e₂-a* | in the house |
| terminative | *-še₃* | to, towards, up to | *uru-še₃* | to the city |
| ablative | *-ta* | from, out of; with (a tool) | *kur-ta* | from the mountains |
| comitative | *-da* | with, together with | *lugal-da* | with the king |
| equative | *-gin₇* | like, as | *ur-saĝ-gin₇* | like a hero |
| directive | *-e* | at, by, next to (non-human) | *e₂-e* | at the house, to the house |

The equative *-gin₇* is written with the sign 𒁶, read *gin₇* in this use.

## Complete system

| Case | Ending | Human | Non-human |
|---|---|---|---|
| ergative | *-e* | yes | yes |
| absolutive | none | yes | yes |
| genitive | *-ak* | yes | yes |
| dative | *-ra* | yes | (directive *-e* instead) |
| locative | *-a* | rare | yes |
| terminative | *-še₃* | yes | yes |
| ablative | *-ta* | rare | yes |
| comitative | *-da* | yes | yes |
| equative | *-gin₇* | yes | yes |
| directive | *-e* | no | yes |

The directive and the ergative both end in *-e*: the verb tells them apart (lesson 8).

::: summary
- Locative *-a* in; terminative *-še₃* to; ablative *-ta* from; comitative *-da* with; equative *-gin₇* like; directive *-e* at (non-human).
- The case closes the whole chain: *e₂ gibil-a-ni-a* "in his new house".
- Dative *-ra* for people; directive *-e* for things.
:::
