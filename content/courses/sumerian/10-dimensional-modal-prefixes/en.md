# 10. Dimensional and modal prefixes

Two more groups of prefixes complete the verbal chain. **Dimensional prefixes** repeat inside the verb the cases of the noun phrases: if the sentence has "for the god" (*-ra*), the verb has *-na-* "for him". **Modal prefixes**, at the very front, turn a statement into a negation, a wish, an exhortation or a prohibition. With them you can read the key verb of nearly every building inscription: *mu-na-du₃* "he built (it) for him".

## Dimensional prefixes

| Case in the sentence | Prefix in the verb | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| dative *-ra* (3rd sg.) | *-na-* | *mu-na-du₃* | he built (it) for him |
| dative, "for me" | *-a-* (*mu-* + *-a-* = *ma-*) | *ma-an-šum₂* | he gave (it) to me |
| dative, "for you" | *-ra-* | *mu-ra-an-šum₂* | he gave (it) to you |
| comitative *-da* | *-da-* | *mu-da-ĝen* | he went with (him) |
| terminative *-še₃* | *-ši-* | *mu-ši-ĝen* | he went towards (it) |
| ablative *-ta* | *-ta-* | *ba-ta-e₃* | he went out of (it) |
| locative *-a* | *-ni-* | *ba-ni-in-du₃* | he built (it) in (it) |

The noun phrase and the prefix agree: ᵈ*nin-ĝir₂-su-ra e₂-a-ni mu-na-du₃* "for Ningirsu, he built his temple for him". English drops the "for him"; Sumerian says it twice.

## Modal prefixes

| Prefix | Meaning | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| *nu-* | negation | *nu-un-du₃* | he did not build |
| *ḫe₂-* | wish, "may"; also "indeed" | *ḫe₂-en-du₃* | may he build |
| *ga-* | "let me", "let us" | *ga-an-du₃* | let me build |
| *na-* (with the imperfective) | prohibition | *na-an-du₃-e* | he must not build |

*nu-* becomes *la-* or *li-* before *ba-* and *bi₂-*: *la-ba-an-du₃* "he did not build". *ḫe₂-* is written *ḫa-* before *a*: *ḫa-ma-an-šum₂* "may he give it to me".

What does the prefix add?

- ga- in ga-an-du₃ → ((may he / let me* / he must not))
- -da- in mu-da-ĝen → ((for him / with him* / from it))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- ᵈ*nin-ĝir₂-su-ra e₂-a-ni mu-na-du₃* → {{For Ningirsu he built his temple. / He built his temple for Ningirsu.}}
- *lugal-e e₂ nu-un-du₃* → {{The king did not build the house.}}

- *ga-an-du₃* → {{Let me build (it)! / I will build it!}}
:::

::: summary
- Dimensional prefixes repeat the cases: *-na-* for him (dative), *-da-* with, *-ši-* towards, *-ta-* from, *-ni-* in; *ma-* for me, *-ra-* for you.
- Modal prefixes: *nu-* not, *ḫe₂-* may, *ga-* let me, *na-* + imperfective must not.
- ᵈ*nin-ĝir₂-su-ra e₂-a-ni mu-na-du₃*: the formula of building inscriptions.
:::
