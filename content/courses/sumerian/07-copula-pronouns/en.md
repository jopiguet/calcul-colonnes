# 7. The copula and the personal pronouns

"I am king", "he is my son", "it is a god": Sumerian makes such sentences with an **enclitic copula**, a small form of "to be" attached to the end of the predicate. In the third person it is *-am₃*, one of the most frequent endings in Sumerian; it also builds many personal names, which are often whole sentences. This lesson gives the copula, the independent pronouns, and the negative "is not".

## The copula

| Person | Form | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *-me-en* | *lugal-me-en* | I am king |
| 2nd sg. | *-me-en* | *lugal-me-en* | you are king |
| 3rd sg. | *-am₃* (after a vowel *-m*) | *lugal-am₃* | he is king, it is a king |
| 1st pl. | *-me-en-de₃-en* | *lugal-me-en-de₃-en* | we are kings |
| 2nd pl. | *-me-en-ze₂-en* | *lugal-me-en-ze₂-en* | you are kings |
| 3rd pl. | *-me-eš* | *lugal-me-eš* | they are kings |

After a vowel the 3rd person loses its *a*: *dumu-ĝu₁₀-um* "he is my son" (*dumu-ĝu₁₀* + *-m*, written with 𒌝). *-am₃* is written with the sign 𒀭, here read *am₃*.

## Independent pronouns

| Person | Pronoun | Person | Pronoun |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *ĝe₂-e*, *ĝa₂-e* | 1st pl. | *me* |
| 2nd sg. | *ze₂-e*, *za-e* | 2nd pl. | *me-en-ze₂-en* |
| 3rd sg. human | *a-ne*, *e-ne* | 3rd pl. human | *a-ne-ne*, *e-ne-ne* |

They are used for emphasis or contrast: *ĝe₂-e lugal-me-en* "I am the king (and no one else)". With a verb, the pronoun is usually left out: the verb already shows the person.

## The negative

The prefix *nu-* negates the copula: *nu-me-en* "I am not", *nu-um* "he is not" (written *nu-um*, from *nu-am*). *lugal nu-um* "he is not king".

## Names that are sentences

Many Sumerian names are copula sentences: *Lugal-zi* "the king is true", *Ur-*ᵈ*nanše* "servant of Nanshe" (a genitive), *Ninĝal-ama-ĝu₁₀* "Ningal is my mother". Reading a name means reading its grammar.

::: summary
- Copula: *-me-en* (I, you), *-am₃* (he, she, it; after a vowel *-m*), *-me-en-de₃-en* (we), *-me-en-ze₂-en* (you pl.), *-me-eš* (they).
- Pronouns: *ĝe₂-e* I, *ze₂-e* you, *a-ne* he/she, *me* we, *a-ne-ne* they; for emphasis.
- Negative *nu-*: *nu-me-en*, *nu-um*. Many names are copula sentences.
:::
