# 2. Word signs, syllables and determinatives

Sumerian writing mixes three kinds of sign, as Egyptian does. **Logograms** write whole words, mostly nouns and verb roots. **Syllabic signs** write sounds: grammatical prefixes and suffixes, names, and words with no logogram. **Determinatives** are silent and classify the word. A line of Sumerian is a row of logograms held together by syllables, and reading it means deciding, sign by sign, which of the three you are looking at.

## Logograms and compounds

| Signs | Reading | Meaning | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 𒈗 | *lugal* | king | "big man", *lu₂* + *gal* in one sign |
| 𒂍𒃲 | *e₂-gal* | palace | house + big |
| 𒌉𒊩 | *dumu-munus* | daughter | child + woman |
| 𒁾𒊬 | *dub-sar* | scribe | tablet + to write |
| 𒂍𒁾𒁀 | *e₂-dub-ba* | school | house that gives out tablets |
| 𒋀 | *šeš* | brother | |
| 𒌉 | *dumu* | child, son | |

## Syllabic signs

| Shape | Examples | Use |
|---|---|---|
| vowel | 𒀀 *a*, 𒂊 *e*, 𒄿 *i*, 𒌋 *u* | endings, prefixes |
| consonant + vowel | 𒁀 *ba*, 𒈬 *mu*, 𒈾 *na*, 𒉌 *ni*, 𒍪 *zu* | prefixes, suffixes |
| vowel + consonant | 𒀊 *ab*, 𒅔 *in*, 𒅋 *il*, 𒌝 *um* | case and person markers |

A verb such as 𒈬𒈾𒆕 *mu-na-du₃* "he built (it) for him" is two syllables and a logogram: the root *du₃* "build" in the middle of its chain.

## Determinatives

| Sign | Written | Class | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 𒀭 | ᵈ before | god | ᵈ*utu* the sun god |
| 𒄑 | ᵍⁱˢ before | wood, tree | ᵍⁱˢ*tukul* weapon |
| 𒇽 | ˡᵘ² before | profession | ˡᵘ²*kurun₂-na* innkeeper |
| 𒌷 | ᵘʳᵘ before | town | ᵘʳᵘ*ki* |
| 𒆠 | ᵏⁱ after | place | *unug*ᵏⁱ Uruk |
| 𒄷 | ᵐᵘˢᵉⁿ after | bird | *uga*ᵐᵘˢᵉⁿ raven |
| 𒌆 | ᵗᵘᵍ² before | garment | ᵗᵘᵍ²*niĝ₂-lam₂* a fine robe |

Names show the gap between sign and reading: Nippur is written EN.LIL₂ᵏⁱ, "the place of Enlil", but read *nibru*ᵏⁱ.

::: summary
- Logograms for words (*lugal*, *e₂-gal*, *dub-sar*); syllables for grammar and names (*mu-na-du₃*); determinatives silent (ᵈ, ᵍⁱˢ, ˡᵘ², ᵘʳᵘ before; ᵏⁱ, ᵐᵘˢᵉⁿ after).
- Compounds: *e₂-gal* palace, *dumu-munus* daughter, *dub-sar* scribe, *e₂-dub-ba* school.
- A name may not read as its signs: EN.LIL₂ᵏⁱ is *nibru*ᵏⁱ, Nippur.
:::
