# 8. The verbal chain and the intransitive verb

The Sumerian verb is a **chain**, like the noun phrase, but much longer: a root surrounded by prefixes and suffixes in a fixed order. One verb form can say who acted, on what, for whom, from where, whether the action is wished, forbidden or denied. This lesson gives the order of the chain and the simplest case: an **intransitive** verb ("go", "sit", "come"), whose subject is marked by a suffix.

## The order of the chain

| Slot | Element | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | modal prefix | *nu-* not, *ḫe₂-* may, *ga-* let me |
| 2 | conjugation prefix | *mu-*, *i₃-*, *ba-* |
| 3 | dimensional prefixes | *-na-* for him, *-ši-* towards, *-da-* with |
| 4 | person prefix (transitive agent) | *-n-*, *-b-*, *-e-* |
| 5 | **root** | *du₃* build, *ĝen* go, *tuš* sit |
| 6 | person suffix | *-en*, *-eš* |

Most forms use only a few slots: *i₃-ĝen* "he went" is slot 2 + root.

## The conjugation prefixes

Almost every finite verb begins with one of them:

| Prefix | Use |
|---|---|
| *mu-* | the action comes towards the speaker, or is done for someone important: the usual prefix of building inscriptions |
| *i₃-* | neutral |
| *ba-* | away from the speaker; often the meaning "was done", "became" |

Their exact meanings are still debated; for reading, treat them as markers of a finite verb.

## The intransitive verb

The subject of an intransitive verb is absolutive (lesson 5), and the verb marks its person with a **suffix**:

| Person | Suffix | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st sg. | *-en* | *i₃-ĝen-en* | I went |
| 2nd sg. | *-en* | *i₃-ĝen-en* | you went |
| 3rd sg. | none | *i₃-ĝen* | he, she, it went |
| 1st pl. | *-en-de₃-en* | *i₃-ĝen-en-de₃-en* | we went |
| 2nd pl. | *-en-ze₂-en* | *i₃-ĝen-en-ze₂-en* | you went |
| 3rd pl. | *-eš* | *i₃-ĝen-ne₂-eš* | they went |

These are the copula endings of lesson 7 without *-me*: *lugal-me-en* "I am king", *i₃-ĝen-en* "I went".

::: summary
- The chain: modal prefix, conjugation prefix (*mu-*, *i₃-*, *ba-*), dimensional prefixes, person prefix, root, person suffix.
- Intransitive subject in the absolutive; person by suffix: *-en* (I, you), none (he), *-en-de₃-en* (we), *-en-ze₂-en* (you pl.), *-eš* (they).
- *i₃-ĝen* he went; *i₃-ĝen-en* I went; *i₃-ĝen-ne₂-eš* they went; *ba-ug₇* he died.
:::
