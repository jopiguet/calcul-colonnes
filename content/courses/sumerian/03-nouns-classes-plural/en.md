# 3. Nouns: classes, plural and the noun chain

Sumerian nouns have no gender. Instead they belong to one of two **classes**: **human** (people and gods) and **non-human** (animals, things, ideas). The class decides the plural ending and, later, the pronouns and verb forms. Sumerian is **agglutinative**: grammatical elements are added one after another to the end of the noun phrase, in a fixed order, like beads on a string. This lesson gives the classes, the plural, adjectives, and that order: the **noun chain**.

## Two classes

| Class | Contains | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| human | people, gods | *lugal* king, *dumu* child, *diĝir* god, *dub-sar* scribe |
| non-human | animals, things, places, abstracts | *e₂* house, *udu* sheep, *kur* mountain, *nam-lugal* kingship |

The prefix *nam-* makes abstracts: *nam-lugal* "kingship", *nam-dumu* "childhood", *nam-ti* "life".

## The plural

| Way | Class | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| *-ene* (after a vowel *-ne*) | human | *lugal-e-ne* | kings |
| | | *dumu-ne* | children |
| reduplication | mostly non-human | *kur-kur* | all the lands |
| *-ḫi-a* | non-human | *udu-ḫi-a* | sheep of all kinds |
| no ending | non-human | *e₂* | house or houses |

The non-human plural is often left unmarked: the number is clear from a numeral or the context.

## Adjectives and the noun chain

The adjective follows its noun: *lugal gal* "great king", *e₂ gibil* "new house", *dumu tur* "small child". Then come the other elements, always in this order:

| Position | Element | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | noun | *e₂* house |
| 2 | adjective | *gibil* new |
| 3 | genitive noun phrase | *lugal-la(k)* of the king |
| 4 | possessive | *-a-ni* his |
| 5 | plural | *-ene* |
| 6 | case | *-e*, *-ra*, *-še₃*… |

*e₂ gibil lugal-la* "the new house of the king". Because the case ending comes at the very end of the chain, it can be far from the noun it belongs to; reading Sumerian means finding the end of each chain.

::: summary
- Two classes, human and non-human; no gender. *nam-* makes abstracts (*nam-lugal* kingship).
- Plural: human *-ene* (*lugal-e-ne*, *dumu-ne*); reduplication (*kur-kur*); *-ḫi-a*; non-human often unmarked.
- Noun chain: noun, adjective, genitive, possessive, plural, case: the case comes last.
:::
