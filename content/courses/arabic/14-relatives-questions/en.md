# 14. Relative clauses and questions

The last verse of al-Fatiha contains a relative clause: صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ "the path of those whom You have blessed", literally "those whom You have blessed upon them". Arabic relative pronouns agree with their noun, are used only after a definite noun, and the clause repeats the noun with a pronoun. Questions are simpler: a question word or هَلْ in front of an ordinary sentence.

## Relative pronouns

| | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | الَّذِي *alladhī* | الَّذِينَ *alladhīna* |
| feminine | الَّتِي *allatī* | اللَّاتِي *allātī* |

| Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| الرَّجُلُ الَّذِي يَكْتُبُ | the man who is writing |
| الكِتَابُ الَّذِي قَرَأْتُهُ | the book (that) I read (lit. read it) |
| المَدِينَةُ الَّتِي سَكَنْتُ فِيهَا | the city I lived in (lit. in it) |
| رَأَيْتُ رَجُلًا يَكْتُبُ | I saw a man (who was) writing |

After an indefinite noun there is no relative word. The pronoun ـهُ، ـهَا، ـهِمْ inside the clause, the *ʿāʾid*, points back to the noun whenever it is not the subject. مَنْ "he who, whoever" and مَا "what, that which" need no noun: مَنْ جَدَّ وَجَدَ "whoever strives, finds" (a proverb).

## Question words

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| مَا / مَاذَا *mā / mādhā* | what | مَاذَا تُرِيدُ؟ what do you want? |
| مَنْ *man* | who | مَنْ أَنْتَ؟ who are you? |
| أَيْنَ *ayna* | where | أَيْنَ تَسْكُنُ؟ where do you live? |
| مَتَى *matā* | when | مَتَى وَصَلْتَ؟ when did you arrive? |
| كَيْفَ *kayfa* | how | كَيْفَ حَالُكَ؟ how are you? |
| لِمَاذَا *limādhā* | why | لِمَاذَا تَدْرُسُ العَرَبِيَّةَ؟ why do you study Arabic? |
| كَمْ *kam* | how many (+ singular accusative) | كَمْ كِتَابًا عِنْدَكَ؟ how many books do you have? |
| أَيّ *ayy* | which | أَيُّ كِتَابٍ؟ which book? |
| هَلْ / أَ *hal / a-* | (yes-no question) | هَلْ أَنْتَ طَالِبٌ؟ are you a student? |

مَا before a noun is "what is": مَا اسْمُكَ؟ "what is your name?". Before a verb in the perfect, مَا is a negation (lesson 9).

Write in Arabic.

- "why" → [[لماذا]]
- "when" → [[متى]]
- "who" (relative, m. sg.) → [[الذي]]
- "those who" (m. pl.) → [[الذين]]
:::

::: exercise relative
Which relative?

- المَدْرَسَةُ … بَنَوْهَا "the school they built" → ((الَّذِي / الَّتِي* / الَّذِينَ))
- الطُّلَّابُ … نَجَحُوا "the students who passed" → ((الَّذِي / الَّتِي / الَّذِينَ*))
- رَأَيْتُ رَجُلًا … يَكْتُبُ "I saw a man writing" → ((الَّذِي / no relative word*))
:::

::: exercise question
Which question word?

- … أَنْتَ؟ "who are you?" → ((مَا / مَنْ* / أَيْنَ))
- … كِتَابًا عِنْدَكَ؟ "how many books?" → ((كَمْ* / كَيْفَ / أَيّ))
- … أَنْتَ طَالِبٌ؟ "are you a student?" → ((هَلْ* / مَاذَا / مَتَى))
- … اسْمُكَ؟ "what is your name?" → ((مَا* / مَنْ / كَمْ))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- الكِتَابُ الَّذِي قَرَأْتُهُ جَمِيلٌ. → {{The book I read is beautiful. / The book that I read is beautiful.}}
- صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ → {{the path of those whom You have blessed / the way of those You have favoured}}
- لِمَاذَا تَدْرُسُ العَرَبِيَّةَ؟ → {{Why do you study Arabic? / Why are you studying Arabic?}}
- هَلْ أَنْتَ طَالِبٌ؟ → {{Are you a student?}}
:::

::: summary
- الذي، التي، الذين، اللاتي after a definite noun; none after an indefinite one; a pronoun repeats the noun (قرأتُهُ).
- مَن whoever, ما what; ماذا، مَن، أين، متى، كيف، لماذا، كم، أيّ.
- هل / أ for yes-no questions; كم + singular accusative.
:::
