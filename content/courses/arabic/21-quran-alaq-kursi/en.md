# 21. The Quran: the first revelation and the Throne Verse

Two famous passages close the reading of the Quran. The opening of sura 96, al-ʿAlaq, is by tradition the first revelation, received in a cave near Mecca: it begins with the command "Read!". The Throne Verse, آيَةُ ٱلْكُرْسِيّ (2:255), is the best-known single verse, often written above doorways and recited for protection. Both use only grammar you have learnt.

## Sura 96, al-ʿAlaq, verses 1–5

| Verse | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ۝١ | Read, in the name of your Lord who created, |
| 2 | خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنْسَانَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ ۝٢ | created man from a clinging clot. |
| 3 | ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ ۝٣ | Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous, |
| 4 | ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ ۝٤ | who taught by the pen, |
| 5 | عَلَّمَ ٱلْإِنْسَانَ مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ ۝٥ | taught man what he did not know. |

ٱقْرَأْ: imperative of قَرَأَ (lesson 12). ٱلْأَكْرَمُ: the elative "most generous", pattern أَفْعَل, from كَرِيم. عَلَّمَ is form II of the root of عَلِمَ (lesson 10): knowing and teaching, joined in one verse. مَا here is "what": مَا لَمْ يَعْلَمْ "what he did not know".

## The Throne Verse, 2:255 (beginning)

| Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ٱللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْحَيُّ ٱلْقَيُّومُ | God: there is no god but He, the Living, the Self-Subsisting. |
| لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ | Neither slumber nor sleep overtakes Him. |
| لَهُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي ٱلْأَرْضِ | To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. |
| وَسِعَ كُرْسِيُّهُ ٱلسَّمَاوَاتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ | His Throne extends over the heavens and the earth. |

لَا إِلَٰهَ: the **lā of absolute negation**, followed by a noun in the accusative without tanwīn: "there is no god at all"; إِلَّا "except". ٱلْقَيُّوم is an intensive pattern, *faʿʿūl*. لَهُ مَا: "to Him (is) what": possession with لِـ (lesson 5). كُرْسِيّ is "seat, throne", the word that gives the verse its name.

The phrase لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّهُ "there is no god but God" is the first half of the Muslim profession of faith, the شَهَادَة *shahāda*.

::: summary
- Al-ʿAlaq 96:1–5: ٱقْرَأْ; elative ٱلْأَكْرَم; عَلِمَ / عَلَّمَ; مَا "what".
- Throne Verse: لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ, lā of absolute negation + accusative; ٱلْحَيّ ٱلْقَيُّوم.
- لَهُ مَا فِي ٱلسَّمَاوَاتِ "His is what is in the heavens"; كُرْسِيّ throne; the shahāda.
