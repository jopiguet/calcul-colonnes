# 7. Plurals and the dual

Arabic has three numbers: singular, dual for exactly two, and plural for three or more. The plural comes in two kinds. The **sound plurals** add an ending to the word. The **broken plurals** rebuild the word inside, keeping its consonants and changing its vowels: كِتَاب *kitāb* "book", كُتُب *kutub* "books". Broken plurals are the more common, and each noun's plural is learnt with it, as English learns *mouse, mice*.

## Sound plurals and the dual

| Kind | Nominative | Accusative / genitive | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| sound masculine | ـُونَ *-ūna* | ـِينَ *-īna* | مُدَرِّسُونَ *mudarrisūna* teachers |
| sound feminine | ـَاتٌ *-ātun* | ـَاتٍ *-ātin* | مُدَرِّسَات *mudarrisāt*; سَيَّارَات *sayyārāt* cars |
| dual | ـَانِ *-āni* | ـَيْنِ *-ayni* | كِتَابَانِ *kitābāni* two books |

The sound masculine plural is used mainly for male people described by participles and adjectives; the sound feminine for women, and for many nouns in ة. The Quran's sura 23 opens قَدْ أَفْلَحَ المُؤْمِنُونَ "the believers have succeeded"; sura 103 speaks of those who do الصَّالِحَات "good deeds".

## Broken plurals

| Singular | Plural | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| كِتَاب *kitāb* book | كُتُب *kutub* | fuʿul |
| وَلَد *walad* boy | أَوْلَاد *awlād* | afʿāl |
| قَلَم *qalam* pen | أَقْلَام *aqlām* | afʿāl |
| رَجُل *rajul* man | رِجَال *rijāl* | fiʿāl |
| بَيْت *bayt* house | بُيُوت *buyūt* | fuʿūl |
| مَدْرَسَة *madrasa* school | مَدَارِس *madāris* | mafāʿil |
| طَالِب *ṭālib* student | طُلَّاب *ṭullāb* | fuʿʿāl |

## Agreement of non-human plurals

A plural that does not refer to people takes its adjectives and verbs in the **feminine singular**: الكُتُبُ جَدِيدَةٌ "the books are new", not *jadīdūn*. Human plurals agree normally: الطُّلَّابُ جُدُدٌ.

Which form agrees?

- الكُتُبُ … "new" → ((جُدُدٌ / جَدِيدَةٌ* / جَدِيدُونَ))

::: summary
- Sound plurals: -ūna/-īna (men), -āt (women, many nouns in ة); dual -āni/-ayni.
- Broken plurals change the vowels inside: كتاب كُتُب، ولد أولاد، رجل رجال، بيت بيوت، مدرسة مدارس.
- Non-human plurals agree in the feminine singular: الكتب جديدة.
:::
