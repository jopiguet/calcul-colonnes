# 3. The article, pronouns and greetings

Three pieces let you say your first sentences. The article ال *al-* "the" is joined to its noun. The personal pronouns distinguish "you" for a man and for a woman. And the simplest Arabic sentence has no verb at all: أَنَا طَالِب *anā ṭālib* "I (am a) student".

## The article and the sun letters

ال *al-* is written the same before every noun, but before fourteen letters, the **sun letters**, the *l* is not pronounced: the next consonant is doubled instead.

| Kind | Letters | Example |
|---|---|---|
| moon letters: *l* heard | ا ب ج ح خ ع غ ف ق ك م ه و ي | القَمَر *al-qamar* the moon; الكِتَاب *al-kitāb* |
| sun letters: *l* assimilated | ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن | الشَّمْس *ash-shams* the sun; الرَّجُل *ar-rajul* the man |

The names come from the two examples, *shams* and *qamar*. After a vowel, the *a* of *al-* drops: فِي البَيْت *fi l-bayt* "in the house".

## Personal pronouns

| Pronoun | Meaning | Pronoun | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| أَنَا *anā* | I | نَحْنُ *naḥnu* | we |
| أَنْتَ *anta* | you (m.) | أَنْتُمْ *antum* | you (m. pl.) |
| أَنْتِ *anti* | you (f.) | أَنْتُنَّ *antunna* | you (f. pl.) |
| هُوَ *huwa* | he, it | هُمْ *hum* | they (m.) |
| هِيَ *hiya* | she, it | هُنَّ *hunna* | they (f.) |

## The nominal sentence

| Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| أَنَا طَالِب. *anā ṭālib.* | I am a student. |
| البَيْتُ كَبِير. *al-baytu kabīr.* | The house is big. |
| هُوَ فِي المَدْرَسَة. *huwa fi l-madrasa.* | He is at school. |

The present of "to be" is not expressed: subject and predicate stand side by side, the subject definite, the predicate usually not.

## Greetings

| Arabic | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ *as-salāmu ʿalaykum* | peace be upon you | وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَام *wa-ʿalaykumu s-salām* and upon you peace |
| صَبَاحُ الخَيْر *ṣabāḥ al-khayr* | good morning | صَبَاحُ النُّور *ṣabāḥ an-nūr* morning of light |
| كَيْفَ حَالُكَ؟ *kayfa ḥāluka?* | how are you? | الحَمْدُ لِلَّه *al-ḥamdu lillāh* praise be to God (fine) |
| شُكْرًا *shukran* | thank you | عَفْوًا *ʿafwan* you're welcome |
| مَعَ السَّلَامَة *maʿa s-salāma* | goodbye | |

الحَمْدُ لِلَّه is also the second verse of the Quran's first sura; you will meet it again in lesson 19.

::: summary
- ال al-: *l* heard before moon letters (al-qamar), assimilated before sun letters (ash-shams).
- أنا، أنتَ، أنتِ، هو، هي، نحن، أنتم، أنتنّ، هم، هنّ.
- No "is" in the present: أنا طالب، البيت كبير; السلام عليكم، شكرا، الحمد لله.
:::
