# 5. The iḍāfa and possessive suffixes

"The student's book", "the door of the house", "the Lord of the worlds": Arabic links two nouns by putting them side by side, the first without article, the second in the genitive. This construction, the **iḍāfa** ("annexation"), is everywhere, in street names and in the Quran's first sura. For "my", "your", "his", Arabic adds short suffixes to the noun.

## The iḍāfa

| Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|
| كِتَابُ الطَّالِبِ *kitābu ṭ-ṭālibi* | the student's book |
| بَابُ البَيْتِ *bābu l-bayti* | the door of the house |
| مَدْرَسَةُ البَنَاتِ *madrasatu l-banāti* | the girls' school |
| رَبِّ العَالَمِينَ *rabbi l-ʿālamīna* | the Lord of the worlds (Quran 1:2) |
| يَوْمِ الدِّينِ *yawmi d-dīni* | the Day of Judgement (Quran 1:4) |

Three rules: the first noun never takes the article or tanwīn; it is definite if the second one is; and a ة at its end is pronounced *-at*: مَدْرَسَةُ *madrasatu*. An adjective describing the first noun comes after the whole group: كِتَابُ الطَّالِبِ الجَدِيدُ "the student's new book".

## Possessive suffixes

| Suffix | Meaning | كِتَاب book | مَدْرَسَة school |
|---|---|---|---|
| ـِي *-ī* | my | كِتَابِي *kitābī* | مَدْرَسَتِي *madrasatī* |
| ـكَ *-ka* | your (m.) | كِتَابُكَ *kitābuka* | مَدْرَسَتُكَ |
| ـكِ *-ki* | your (f.) | كِتَابُكِ *kitābuki* | مَدْرَسَتُكِ |
| ـهُ *-hu* | his | كِتَابُهُ *kitābuhu* | مَدْرَسَتُهُ |
| ـهَا *-hā* | her | كِتَابُهَا *kitābuhā* | مَدْرَسَتُهَا |
| ـنَا *-nā* | our | كِتَابُنَا *kitābunā* | مَدْرَسَتُنَا |
| ـكُمْ *-kum* | your (pl.) | كِتَابُكُمْ *kitābukum* | مَدْرَسَتُكُمْ |
| ـهُمْ *-hum* | their | كِتَابُهُمْ *kitābuhum* | مَدْرَسَتُهُمْ |

The ة becomes ت before a suffix. The same suffixes go on prepositions: لِي *lī* "to me", فِيهِ *fīhi* "in it", عَلَيْكُمْ *ʿalaykum* "upon you".

## "To have"

Arabic has no verb "to have": it says "with me", عِنْدِي *ʿindī*, or "to me", لِي *lī*. عِنْدِي كِتَابٌ "I have a book"; لَهُ أَخٌ "he has a brother".

::: summary
- Iḍāfa: noun without article + noun in the genitive: كتابُ الطالبِ; ة read -at; adjective after the group.
- Suffixes ـي ـكَ ـكِ ـهُ ـهَا ـنَا ـكُم ـهُم; ة → ت before them; also on prepositions: لي، فيه، عليكم.
- "To have": عندي، لي + noun.
:::
