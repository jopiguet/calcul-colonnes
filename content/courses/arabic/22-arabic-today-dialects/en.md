# 22. Arabic today: the standard and the dialects

The Arabic of this course, Modern Standard Arabic (الفُصْحَى *al-fuṣḥā*), is the language of books, newspapers, news broadcasts, speeches and the mosque, the same from Morocco to Oman. No one speaks it at home. Every Arab grows up in a spoken dialect, العَامِّيَّة *al-ʿāmmiyya* or الدَّارِجَة *ad-dārija*, and learns the standard at school. Linguists call this division of roles **diglossia**. The dialects share the roots and much of the grammar of the standard; what changes is pronunciation, a set of everyday words, and simplified endings.

## Five dialect areas, five words

| Meaning | Standard | Egypt | Levant | Gulf | Morocco |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| what? | مَاذَا *mādhā* | إِيه *ʾēh* | شُو *shū* | إِيش *ēsh* | شْنُو *shnu* |
| I want | أُرِيدُ *urīdu* | عَايِز *ʿāyiz* | بَدِّي *baddī* | أَبْغَى *abghā* | بْغِيت *bghīt* |
| now | الآنَ *al-āna* | دِلْوَقْتِي *dilwaʾti* | هَلَّق *halla'* | الحِين *al-ḥīn* | دَابَا *dāba* |
| how are you? | كَيْفَ حَالُكَ؟ | إِزَّيَّك؟ *izzayyak?* | كِيفَك؟ *kīfak?* | شْلُونَك؟ *shlōnak?* | لَبَاس؟ *labās?* |
| there is | يُوجَدُ *yūjadu* | فِيه *fīh* | فِي *fī* | فِيه *fīh* | كَايِن *kāyin* |

## Sounds and grammar

| Feature | Standard | Dialects |
|---|---|---|
| ق | *q* | *ʾ* in Cairo, Beirut, Damascus; *g* in the Gulf and the countryside |
| ج | *j* | *g* in Cairo: *gamīl* "beautiful" |
| ث ذ | *th*, *dh* | *t*/*s*, *d*/*z* in Egypt and the Levant |
| case endings | *-u*, *-a*, *-i* | dropped |
| negation | لَا، لَمْ، لَنْ | مَا … ـش in Egypt and North Africa: مَا عَرَفْتِش "I didn't know" |
| future | سَـ | حَـ *ḥa-* (Egypt), رَح *raḥ* (Levant) |

## Words heard everywhere

إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ *in shāʾa llāh* "if God wills" (for anything in the future), مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ *mā shāʾa llāh* "what God has willed" (in admiration), يَعْنِي *yaʿnī* "I mean", يَلَّا *yalla* "come on, let's go", خَلَاص *khalāṣ* "that's it, enough", حَبِيبِي *ḥabībī* "my dear". With the standard you will be understood everywhere; with a dialect you will be at home somewhere.

::: summary
- Diglossia: الفصحى for writing and formal speech, a dialect (العامية) for daily life.
- Dialects: Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf, Iraqi, Maghrebi; ق → ʾ or g, ج → g in Cairo, no case endings, مَا … ـش.
- Everywhere: إن شاء الله، ما شاء الله، يعني، يلا، خلاص، حبيبي.
:::
