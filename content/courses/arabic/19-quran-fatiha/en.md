# 19. The Quran: al-Fatiha

سُورَةُ الفَاتِحَة, "the Opening", is the first sura of the Quran and the most recited text in Arabic: it is said in every unit of the daily prayers, at least seventeen times a day. It has seven verses, and almost every word in it has appeared in this course. Read it first in full, then word by word.

## The text

| Verse | Arabic | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ۝١ | In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. |
| 2 | ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ ۝٢ | Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds, |
| 3 | ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ۝٣ | the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, |
| 4 | مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ ۝٤ | Master of the Day of Judgement. |
| 5 | إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝٥ | You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. |
| 6 | ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝٦ | Guide us on the straight path, |
| 7 | صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ ۝٧ | the path of those You have blessed, not of those who have earned anger, nor of those who are astray. |

The translations are of the meaning; they follow the common reading of Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim, in which the basmala counts as verse 1. Other canonical readings say مَلِكِ *maliki* "King" in verse 4.

## Word by word

| Word | Grammar |
|---|---|
| ٱلْحَمْدُ | the praise: nominative, subject of a nominal sentence (lesson 3) |
| لِلَّهِ | لِـ "to, for" + ٱللَّه, genitive (lesson 6) |
| رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ | iḍāfa, "Lord of the worlds"; ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ sound masculine plural in the genitive (lessons 5, 7) |
| مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ | a chain of iḍāfas: master of the day of judgement; مَٰلِك active participle of form I (lesson 16) |
| إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ | إِيَّا + كَ "you" placed first for emphasis: "You alone"; نَعْبُدُ imperfect, "we" (lesson 9) |
| نَسْتَعِينُ | form X of ع و ن "ask for help", imperfect (lesson 10) |
| ٱهْدِنَا | imperative of هَدَى "guide" + ـنَا "us" (lesson 12) |
| ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ | active participle of form X of ق و م, "straight", accusative agreeing with ٱلصِّرَٰطَ (lesson 16) |
| ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ | relative clause with the returning pronoun ـهِمْ (lesson 14); أَنْعَمْتَ form IV, "You have blessed" |
| ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ | passive participle of غَضِبَ "be angry": "those upon whom there is anger" |
| ٱلضَّآلِّينَ | active participle plural of ضَلَّ "go astray", a doubled verb (lesson 11) |

Listeners answer the recitation of al-Fatiha with آمِين *āmīn* "amen".

::: summary
- Al-Fatiha: seven verses, recited in every unit of prayer.
- Iḍāfa chains (رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ، مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ), emphatic إِيَّاكَ + imperfect, imperative ٱهْدِنَا.
- Participles ٱلْمُسْتَقِيم، ٱلْمَغْضُوب، ٱلضَّآلِّين; relative ٱلَّذِينَ … عَلَيْهِمْ.
:::
