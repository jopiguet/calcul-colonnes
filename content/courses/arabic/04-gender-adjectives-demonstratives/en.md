# 4. Gender, adjectives and "this"

Every Arabic noun is masculine or feminine, and adjectives, pronouns and verbs agree with it. The feminine is usually easy to see: it ends in ة *-a*. Adjectives follow their noun and copy it in four things: gender, number, case and definiteness. That last point is what turns "a big house" into "the big house" or "the house is big".

## Masculine and feminine

| Masculine | Feminine | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| طَالِب *ṭālib* | طَالِبَة *ṭāliba* | student |
| مُدَرِّس *mudarris* | مُدَرِّسَة *mudarrisa* | teacher |
| كَبِير *kabīr* | كَبِيرَة *kabīra* | big |
| جَمِيل *jamīl* | جَمِيلَة *jamīla* | beautiful |

Some feminines have no ة: female beings (أُمّ *umm* mother), most parts of the body that come in pairs (يَد *yad* hand, عَيْن *ʿayn* eye), and a handful of words to learn: شَمْس *shams* sun, أَرْض *arḍ* earth, دَار *dār* house, حَرْب *ḥarb* war, نَار *nār* fire.

## The adjective

| Arabic | Structure | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| بَيْتٌ كَبِيرٌ *baytun kabīrun* | both indefinite | a big house |
| البَيْتُ الكَبِيرُ *al-baytu l-kabīru* | both definite | the big house |
| البَيْتُ كَبِيرٌ *al-baytu kabīrun* | definite noun, indefinite adjective | the house is big |
| مَدْرَسَةٌ جَمِيلَةٌ *madrasatun jamīlatun* | feminine | a beautiful school |

## "This" and "that"

| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| this | هَذَا *hādhā* | هَذِهِ *hādhihi* |
| that | ذَلِكَ *dhālika* | تِلْكَ *tilka* |

هَذَا كِتَابٌ *hādhā kitābun* is a sentence, "this is a book"; هَذَا الكِتَابُ *hādha l-kitābu* is a phrase, "this book". The Quran's second sura begins ذَلِكَ الكِتَابُ لَا رَيْبَ فِيهِ "that is the Book, there is no doubt in it" (2:2).

::: summary
- Feminine in ة: طالبة، كبيرة; also أم، شمس، أرض، يد without ة.
- Adjective after the noun, agreeing in gender, number, case and definiteness: البيت الكبير; البيت كبير is a sentence.
- هذا، هذه; ذلك، تلك: هذا كتاب "this is a book", هذا الكتاب "this book".
:::
