# 13. كَانَ and إِنَّ and their sisters

The nominal sentence of lesson 3 has no verb. Two families of words change it. كَانَ "was" and its sisters put a time or an aspect on it and turn the **predicate** into the accusative. إِنَّ "indeed" and its sisters stress or link the sentence and turn the **subject** into the accusative. Both families are everywhere in the Quran: إِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَحِيمٌ "God is forgiving, merciful".

## كَانَ and its sisters: the predicate in the accusative

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| كَانَ *kāna* | was | كَانَ الجَوُّ جَمِيلًا the weather was beautiful |
| لَيْسَ *laysa* | is not | لَيْسَ البَيْتُ كَبِيرًا the house is not big |
| صَارَ *ṣāra* | became | صَارَ الوَلَدُ رَجُلًا the boy became a man |
| مَا زَالَ *mā zāla* | is still | مَا زَالَ مَرِيضًا he is still ill |
| أَصْبَحَ *aṣbaḥa* | became (lit. in the morning) | أَصْبَحَ الجَوُّ بَارِدًا it got cold |

كَانَ conjugates as a hollow verb: كُنْتُ "I was", يَكُونُ "he will be, he is (habitually)". كَانَ + imperfect is a past habit or continuous: كَانَ يَكْتُبُ "he used to write, he was writing".

## إِنَّ and its sisters: the subject in the accusative

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| إِنَّ *inna* | indeed (at the start) | إِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَحِيمٌ God is forgiving, merciful |
| أَنَّ *anna* | that (after a verb) | أَعْرِفُ أَنَّ البَيْتَ كَبِيرٌ I know that the house is big |
| لَكِنَّ *lākinna* | but | لَكِنَّ الوَقْتَ قَصِيرٌ but time is short |
| لَعَلَّ *laʿalla* | perhaps | لَعَلَّكُمْ تَعْقِلُونَ perhaps you will understand |
| كَأَنَّ *kaʾanna* | as if | كَأَنَّهُ أَسَدٌ as if he were a lion |
| لَيْتَ *layta* | if only | لَيْتَ الشَّبَابَ يَعُودُ if only youth would return |

A pronoun subject is attached: إِنَّهُ، إِنَّنِي، لَعَلَّكُمْ. The predicate may take لَـ for extra stress: إِنَّ الإِنْسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ "man is surely in loss" (103:2). لَكِنْ without shadda is the plain "but" before a verb.

::: summary
- كان، ليس، صار، ما زال، أصبح: predicate in the accusative: كان الجو جميلًا; كان + imperfect = used to.
- إنّ، أنّ، لكنّ، لعلّ، كأنّ، ليت: subject in the accusative: إنّ اللهَ غفور رحيم.
- Attached pronoun subjects: إنّه، لعلّكم; لَـ for stress: لفي خسر.
:::
