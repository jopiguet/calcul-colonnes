# 12. Subjunctive, jussive and imperative

The imperfect has three moods, told apart by the last vowel. The **indicative** ends in *-u* (يَكْتُبُ), the **subjunctive** in *-a* (يَكْتُبَ) after words of wish and purpose, the **jussive** with no vowel (يَكْتُبْ) after لَمْ "did not" and the prohibitive لَا. The **imperative** is built from the jussive. Sura 112 shows the jussive twice: لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ "He did not beget, nor was He begotten".

## The three moods

| Mood | Ending | After | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| indicative | *-u*, plural *-ūna* | (statement) | يَذْهَبُ he goes |
| subjunctive | *-a*, plural *-ū* | أَنْ that, لِـ / كَيْ in order to, حَتَّى until, لَنْ will not | أُرِيدُ أَنْ أَذْهَبَ I want to go |
| jussive | no vowel, plural *-ū* | لَمْ did not, لَا don't, لِـ let | لَمْ يَذْهَبْ he did not go |

In hollow verbs the jussive shortens the vowel: يَكُونُ → لَمْ يَكُنْ "he was not" (112:4 وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ).

## The imperative

Take the jussive, drop the prefix, and if the word then starts with two consonants, add a helping vowel with alif:

| Jussive | Imperative | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| تَكْتُبْ | اُكْتُبْ *uktub* | write! |
| تَقْرَأْ | اِقْرَأْ *iqraʾ* | read, recite! (96:1) |
| تَجْلِسْ | اِجْلِسْ *ijlis* | sit! |
| تَقُولْ → تَقُلْ | قُلْ *qul* | say! |
| تُرْسِلْ | أَرْسِلْ *arsil* | send! (form IV) |

The helping vowel is *u* if the stem vowel is *u*, otherwise *i*. To a woman add ـِي: اُكْتُبِي; to several, ـُوا: اُكْتُبُوا. The negative command is لَا + jussive: لَا تَكْتُبْ "don't write", لَا تَحْزَنْ "do not grieve" (9:40).

::: summary
- Indicative -u; subjunctive -a after أن، لـ، كي، حتى، لن; jussive (no vowel) after لم، لا (don't), لـ.
- Hollow jussive shortens: لم يكن.
- Imperative from the jussive: اكتب، اقرأ، اجلس، قل; f. -ī, pl. -ū; negative لا + jussive.
:::
