# 6. Cases and prepositions

Classical and Standard Arabic mark the role of a noun with a short vowel at its end: *-u* for the subject, *-a* for the object, *-i* after a preposition or in an iḍāfa. These endings, the **iʿrāb**, are written only in vowelled texts and are dropped at the end of a phrase when speaking; but they are always there in the Quran and in careful reading aloud, and they explain many spellings.

## The three cases

| Case | Definite | Indefinite | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| nominative | البَيْتُ *al-baytu* | بَيْتٌ *baytun* | subject; predicate of a nominal sentence |
| accusative | البَيْتَ *al-bayta* | بَيْتًا *baytan* | direct object; adverbs; after إِنَّ |
| genitive | البَيْتِ *al-bayti* | بَيْتٍ *baytin* | after a preposition; second noun of an iḍāfa |

The indefinite accusative adds an alif in writing: كِتَابًا *kitāban*, except after ة: مَدْرَسَةً *madrasatan*. At the end of a phrase, ordinary pronunciation drops the endings (*al-bayt*, *kitāb*) and says *-an* as *-ā*.

## In a sentence

| Arabic | Roles |
|---|---|
| قَرَأَ الطَّالِبُ الكِتَابَ. *qaraʾa ṭ-ṭālibu l-kitāba.* | the student (subject, -u) read the book (object, -a) |
| فِي البَيْتِ *fi l-bayti* | in the house (genitive, -i) |
| بِسْمِ اللَّهِ *bismi llāhi* | in the name of God (genitive after بِـ, and in an iḍāfa) |

Word order is usually verb, subject, object; the endings allow others.

## Prepositions

| Preposition | Meaning | Preposition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| فِي *fī* | in | مَعَ *maʿa* | with (someone) |
| مِنْ *min* | from, of | بِـ *bi-* | with (an instrument), in, by |
| إِلَى *ilā* | to | لِـ *li-* | for, to, belonging to |
| عَلَى *ʿalā* | on | بَيْنَ *bayna* | between |
| عَنْ *ʿan* | about, away from | قَبْلَ / بَعْدَ *qabla / baʿda* | before / after |

بِـ، لِـ and كَـ "like" are written joined to the next word. Quran 2:257: يُخْرِجُهُمْ مِنَ الظُّلُمَاتِ إِلَى النُّورِ "He brings them out of the darknesses into the light".

::: summary
- Nominative -u/-un (subject), accusative -a/-an (object), genitive -i/-in (after prepositions, in iḍāfa).
- Indefinite accusative written with alif: كتابًا; endings dropped at the end of a phrase in speech.
- في، من، إلى، على، عن، مع، بـ، لـ، بين، قبل، بعد, all followed by the genitive.
:::
