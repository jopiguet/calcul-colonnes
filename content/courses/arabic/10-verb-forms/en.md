# 10. The derived forms of the verb

A root does not give one verb but up to ten, each in a fixed pattern with its own shade of meaning: causing, intensifying, doing together, asking for. Western grammars number them with Roman numerals, Arab grammars name them by their pattern. Knowing them, you can guess a new verb from its root and its form: إِسْلَام *islām* is the verbal noun of form IV of س ل م, "to submit, make peace".

## The forms

| Form | Perfect | Imperfect | Typical meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | فَعَلَ | يَفْعَلُ | basic | عَلِمَ *ʿalima* know |
| II | فَعَّلَ | يُفَعِّلُ | causative, intensive | عَلَّمَ *ʿallama* teach |
| III | فَاعَلَ | يُفَاعِلُ | doing with someone | كَاتَبَ *kātaba* correspond with |
| IV | أَفْعَلَ | يُفْعِلُ | causative | أَسْلَمَ *aslama* submit; أَرْسَلَ send |
| V | تَفَعَّلَ | يَتَفَعَّلُ | reflexive of II | تَعَلَّمَ *taʿallama* learn |
| VI | تَفَاعَلَ | يَتَفَاعَلُ | reciprocal | تَكَاتَبَ *takātaba* write to each other |
| VII | اِنْفَعَلَ | يَنْفَعِلُ | passive, intransitive | اِنْكَسَرَ *inkasara* get broken |
| VIII | اِفْتَعَلَ | يَفْتَعِلُ | reflexive, middle | اِجْتَمَعَ *ijtamaʿa* meet, gather |
| X | اِسْتَفْعَلَ | يَسْتَفْعِلُ | asking for, considering | اِسْتَغْفَرَ *istaghfara* ask forgiveness |

Form IX is rare, for colours (اِحْمَرَّ "turn red"). In forms II, III, IV the imperfect prefix has *u*: يُعَلِّمُ *yuʿallimu*.

## In the Quran

| Text | Form |
|---|---|
| عَلَّمَ بِالقَلَمِ (96:4) "who taught by the pen" | II of ع ل م |
| إِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ (1:5) "You we ask for help" | X of ع و ن |
| أَرْسَلْنَا *arsalnā* "We sent" | IV of ر س ل |

## Verbal nouns

Each form has its verbal noun (*maṣdar*): II تَعْلِيم *taʿlīm* teaching; IV إِسْلَام *islām* submission; V تَعَلُّم *taʿallum* learning; VIII اِجْتِمَاع *ijtimāʿ* meeting; X اِسْتِغْفَار *istighfār* asking forgiveness.

::: summary
- Ten forms: I faʿala, II faʿʿala, III fāʿala, IV afʿala, V tafaʿʿala, VI tafāʿala, VII infaʿala, VIII iftaʿala, X istafʿala.
- II/IV causative, III with someone, V/VI reflexive and reciprocal, VII passive, X asking for.
- Verbal nouns: تعليم، إسلام، تعلّم، اجتماع، استغفار.
