# 16. Participles, verbal nouns and the passive

Each verb form produces nouns and adjectives with fixed patterns: the one who does (**active participle**), the one to whom it is done (**passive participle**), and the action itself (**verbal noun**). Many everyday words are just these: مُعَلِّم *muʿallim* "teacher" is "one who teaches", مُسْلِم *muslim* "one who submits". The internal **passive** of the verb changes only the vowels: كَتَبَ "he wrote", كُتِبَ *kutiba* "it was written".

## Participles

| Form | Active participle | Passive participle | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | فَاعِل | مَفْعُول | كَاتِب writer; مَكْتُوب written |
| II | مُفَعِّل | مُفَعَّل | مُعَلِّم teacher; مُعَلَّم taught |
| IV | مُفْعِل | مُفْعَل | مُسْلِم Muslim (one who submits); مُرْسَل sent, messenger |
| V | مُتَفَعِّل | | مُتَعَلِّم learner |
| VIII | مُفْتَعِل | مُفْتَعَل | مُجْتَمِع gathering; مُجْتَمَع society |
| X | مُسْتَفْعِل | مُسْتَفْعَل | مُسْتَقِيم straight (X of ق و م); مُسْتَقْبَل future |

All derived forms start with مُـ *mu-*; the active participle has *i* before the last radical, the passive *a*. اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ المُسْتَقِيمَ "guide us on the straight path" (1:6).

## Verbal nouns

Form I verbal nouns must be learnt (كِتَابَة writing, دَرْس studying, عِلْم knowledge); the others follow the form: تَعْلِيم (II), إِرْسَال (IV), اِسْتِقْبَال (X). The verbal noun often replaces a clause: أُحِبُّ القِرَاءَةَ "I like reading".

## The passive

| Active | Passive | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| كَتَبَ *kataba* | كُتِبَ *kutiba* | it was written |
| يَكْتُبُ *yaktubu* | يُكْتَبُ *yuktabu* | it is written |
| وَلَدَ *walada* | وُلِدَ *wulida*, يُولَدُ *yūladu* | was born, is born |
| قَالَ *qāla* | قِيلَ *qīla* | it was said |

Perfect: *u – i*; imperfect: *u – a*. The agent is rarely named. كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ "fasting has been prescribed for you" (2:183); وُلِدْتُ فِي تُونِسَ "I was born in Tunis".

::: summary
- Participles: فاعل / مفعول (I); مُـ + i (active), مُـ + a (passive) in derived forms: معلِّم، مسلِم، مستقيم.
- Verbal nouns: learnt in form I; fixed in others: تعليم، إرسال، استقبال.
- Passive by vowels: كُتِبَ، يُكْتَبُ، قِيلَ، وُلِدَ.
:::
