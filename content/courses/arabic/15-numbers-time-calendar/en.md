# 15. Numbers, time and the calendar

Arabic numbers are the source of the European digits, which Europe learnt from Arabic books in the Middle Ages; the Arab East itself writes another set, the "Indian" digits ٠١٢٣. Number words come with one famous difficulty: from three to ten, the number takes the feminine form with a masculine noun, and the masculine form with a feminine one.

## Digits and numbers

| Digit | Western | Number (with m. noun) | With f. noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| ١ | 1 | وَاحِد *wāḥid* | وَاحِدَة *wāḥida* |
| ٢ | 2 | اِثْنَان *ithnān* | اِثْنَتَان *ithnatān* |
| ٣ | 3 | ثَلَاثَة *thalātha* | ثَلَاث *thalāth* |
| ٤ | 4 | أَرْبَعَة *arbaʿa* | أَرْبَع *arbaʿ* |
| ٥ | 5 | خَمْسَة *khamsa* | خَمْس *khams* |
| ٦ | 6 | سِتَّة *sitta* | سِتّ *sitt* |
| ٧ | 7 | سَبْعَة *sabʿa* | سَبْع *sabʿ* |
| ٨ | 8 | ثَمَانِيَة *thamāniya* | ثَمَانٍ *thamānin* |
| ٩ | 9 | تِسْعَة *tisʿa* | تِسْع *tisʿ* |
| ١٠ | 10 | عَشَرَة *ʿashara* | عَشْر *ʿashr* |

Digits are written from left to right, like ours: ٢٠٢٦ is 2026. Twenty is عِشْرُون *ʿishrūn*, a hundred مِئَة *miʾa*, a thousand أَلْف *alf*. ثَلَاثَةُ كُتُبٍ "three books" (كِتَاب is masculine, so the number has ة); ثَلَاثُ سَيَّارَاتٍ "three cars". After 3–10 the noun is a genitive plural; after 11–99 an accusative singular: عِشْرُونَ كِتَابًا. Ordinals: الأَوَّل *al-awwal* first, الثَّانِي second, الثَّالِث third.

## The time and the week

كَمِ السَّاعَةُ؟ *kami s-sāʿa?* "What time is it?" – السَّاعَةُ الثَّالِثَةُ "It is three o'clock" (with the ordinal); وَالنِّصْف "and a half", وَالرُّبْع "and a quarter".

| Day | Arabic | Literally |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | الأَحَد *al-aḥad* | the first |
| Monday | الإِثْنَيْن *al-ithnayn* | the second |
| Friday | الجُمُعَة *al-jumuʿa* | the gathering: the day of congregational prayer |
| Saturday | السَّبْت *as-sabt* | the sabbath |

## The Hijri calendar

The Islamic calendar is lunar: twelve months of 29 or 30 days, about 354 days a year, so its months move through the seasons. It counts from the Hijra, the Prophet's emigration from Mecca to Medina in 622. Its months include مُحَرَّم *Muḥarram* (the first), رَمَضَان *Ramaḍān* (the month of fasting) and ذُو الحِجَّة *Dhū l-Ḥijja* (the month of pilgrimage). The year 1448 began in June 2026. Dates are written with هـ: ١٤٤٨ هـ.
