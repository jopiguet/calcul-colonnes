# 2. Vowels and the other signs

Arabic writes its three long vowels with letters, ا *ā*, ي *ī*, و *ū*, and its three short vowels with small marks above or below the consonant. Newspapers and books leave the short vowels out: a reader of كتب sees *kataba* "he wrote", *kutub* "books" or *kutiba* "it was written" from the context. The Quran, children's books and dictionaries print every mark, and this course does too while you learn.

## The vowel marks

| Mark | Name | Sound | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| ـَ | fatḥa | short *a* | كَتَبَ *kataba* he wrote |
| ـِ | kasra | short *i* | بِنْت *bint* girl |
| ـُ | ḍamma | short *u* | كُتُب *kutub* books |
| ـْ | sukūn | no vowel | قَلْب *qalb* heart |
| ـّ | shadda | doubled consonant | مُدَرِّس *mudarris* teacher |
| ـَا ـِي ـُو | long vowels | *ā*, *ī*, *ū* | كِتَاب *kitāb*, كَبِير *kabīr*, نُور *nūr* |

## Endings and special letters

| Sign | Name | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| ـٌ ـٍ ـً | tanwīn | *-un*, *-in*, *-an*: "a, an" | كِتَابٌ *kitābun* a book |
| ة | tāʾ marbūṭa | feminine ending, *-a*, *-at* before a vowel | مَدْرَسَة *madrasa* school |
| ى | alif maqṣūra | final *ā* written like ي without dots | عَلَى *ʿalā* on |
| ء | hamza | glottal stop, on a carrier أ إ ؤ ئ or alone | سَمَاء *samāʾ* sky; أُمّ *ʾumm* mother |
| آ | alif madda | *ʾā* | آخِر *ākhir* last |

A word never begins with a vowel in Arabic: an initial vowel is carried by alif with hamza, أ or إ. In transliteration the initial ʾ is usually not written: *umm*, *ana*.

::: summary
- Short vowels: fatḥa a, kasra i, ḍamma u; sukūn no vowel; shadda doubling.
- Long vowels ا ي و; tanwīn -un -in -an; ة tāʾ marbūṭa (-a); ى alif maqṣūra (-ā).
- Hamza ء on أ إ ؤ ئ; an initial vowel is carried by أ or إ.
:::
