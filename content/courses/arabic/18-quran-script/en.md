# 18. The Quran: its structure and its script

The Quran (القُرْآن, "the recitation") is the scripture of Islam and the first book written in Arabic. For Muslims it is the word of God revealed to the Prophet Muhammad between about 610 and 632; for every reader of Arabic it is also the model of the classical language, whose grammar was codified to read it. This lesson and the next three read short passages with the grammar of the course. The translations given are of the meaning only: the text is the Arabic.

## Structure

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| سُورَة *sūra* | chapter; there are 114 |
| آيَة *āya* | verse (also "sign"); 6,236 in the usual count |
| مَكِّيّ / مَدَنِيّ | revealed in Mecca / in Medina |
| جُزْء *juzʾ* | one of 30 equal parts, for reading over a month |
| مُصْحَف *muṣḥaf* | a written copy of the Quran |

After the opening sura, al-Fatiha, the suras are arranged roughly from the longest to the shortest. Every sura but the ninth begins with the **basmala**: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ *bismi llāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm* "In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful".

## The script of the muṣḥaf

Printed Qurans keep the spelling of the early copies, with extra signs for exact recitation.

| Sign | Name | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| ٱ | alif waṣla | alif not pronounced after a vowel | بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ *bismi llāh* |
| ـٰ | dagger alif | a long *ā* not written with a full alif | ٱلرَّحْمَٰن *ar-raḥmān*; ذَٰلِكَ *dhālika* |
| ۝ | end of verse | encloses the verse number | ۝١ |
| ۚ ۖ ۗ | pause signs | permitted, preferred or recommended pause | |
| ٓ | madda | lengthen the vowel | ٱلضَّآلِّينَ *aḍ-ḍāllīn* |

## Recitation

The rules of recitation, **tajwīd**, govern lengthening (*madd*), nasal sounds (*ghunna*), the merging of letters (*idghām*) and the slight echo of ق ط ب ج د at rest (*qalqala*). The Quran is meant to be heard: تَرْتِيل *tartīl*, slow and measured recitation, is how most people first meet it.

::: summary
- 114 suras, 6,236 verses (āyāt); Meccan and Medinan; 30 parts; every sura but the 9th opens with the basmala.
- Muṣḥaf spelling: ٱ alif waṣla, ـٰ dagger alif, ۝ verse end, pause signs, ٓ madda.
- Tajwīd: madd, ghunna, idghām, qalqala; tartīl, measured recitation.
:::
