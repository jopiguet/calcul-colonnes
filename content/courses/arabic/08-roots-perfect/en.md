# 8. Roots, patterns and the perfect

Most Arabic words are built from a **root** of three consonants carrying a general meaning, poured into a **pattern** of vowels and extra letters. The root ك ت ب *k-t-b* is "writing": كَتَبَ *kataba* "he wrote", كِتَاب *kitāb* "book", كَاتِب *kātib* "writer", مَكْتَب *maktab* "office", مَكْتَبَة *maktaba* "library", مَكْتُوب *maktūb* "written". Find the root and you are halfway to the meaning; this is how Arabic dictionaries are ordered.

## One root, many words

| Pattern | Meaning of the pattern | ك ت ب write | د ر س study | ع ل م know |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| فَعَلَ *faʿala* | he did | كَتَبَ *kataba* | دَرَسَ *darasa* | عَلِمَ *ʿalima* |
| فَاعِل *fāʿil* | doer | كَاتِب writer | دَارِس student | عَالِم scholar |
| مَفْعُول *mafʿūl* | done | مَكْتُوب written | مَدْرُوس studied | مَعْلُوم known |
| مَفْعَل(ة) *mafʿal(a)* | place | مَكْتَب office | مَدْرَسَة school | مَعْلَم landmark |

The grammarians name patterns with the root ف ع ل *f-ʿ-l* "do": *fāʿil* is "the pattern of the doer".

## The perfect (past)

The perfect adds suffixes to the stem *katab-*:

| Person | Form | Person | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | كَتَبْتُ *katabtu* | we | كَتَبْنَا *katabnā* |
| you (m.) | كَتَبْتَ *katabta* | you (m. pl.) | كَتَبْتُمْ *katabtum* |
| you (f.) | كَتَبْتِ *katabti* | you (f. pl.) | كَتَبْتُنَّ *katabtunna* |
| he | كَتَبَ *kataba* | they (m.) | كَتَبُوا *katabū* |
| she | كَتَبَتْ *katabat* | they (f.) | كَتَبْنَ *katabna* |

The silent alif after كَتَبُوا is a spelling rule. The dictionary form is the 3rd person masculine singular: كَتَبَ "he wrote" stands for "to write". The middle vowel varies: *faʿala* كَتَبَ, *faʿila* شَرِبَ *shariba* "he drank", *faʿula* كَبُرَ *kabura* "he grew big". The negation is مَا: مَا كَتَبَ "he did not write".

The first word revealed of the Quran, by tradition, is an imperative of the root ق ر أ "read, recite": اقْرَأْ (96:1); the same root gives قُرْآن *qurʾān*, "recitation".

::: summary
- Root of three consonants + pattern: ك ت ب → كَتَبَ، كِتَاب، كَاتِب، مَكْتَب، مَكْتُوب.
- Perfect: -tu, -ta, -ti, -a, -at; -nā, -tum, -tunna, -ū, -na; negation مَا.
- Patterns named with ف ع ل: fāʿil doer, mafʿūl done, mafʿal place.
:::
