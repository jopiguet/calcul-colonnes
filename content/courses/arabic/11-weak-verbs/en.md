# 11. Weak verbs

When one of the three root letters is و or ي, a hamza, or when the last two are the same, the verb bends: the weak letter becomes a long vowel, shortens or disappears. These verbs are among the most frequent in the language: قَالَ "he said", كَانَ "he was", جَاءَ "he came", رَأَى "he saw". Each class follows its own rule, and the root is still there under the surface.

## The classes

| Class | Root | Perfect | Imperfect | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hollow (middle و/ي) | ق و ل | قَالَ *qāla*, قُلْتُ *qultu* | يَقُولُ *yaqūlu* | say |
| hollow | ك و ن | كَانَ *kāna*, كُنْتُ *kuntu* | يَكُونُ *yakūnu* | be |
| hollow | س ي ر | سَارَ *sāra*, سِرْتُ *sirtu* | يَسِيرُ *yasīru* | walk, travel |
| defective (last و/ي) | د ع و | دَعَا *daʿā* | يَدْعُو *yadʿū* | call, pray |
| defective | م ش ي | مَشَى *mashā* | يَمْشِي *yamshī* | walk |
| assimilated (first و) | و ص ل | وَصَلَ *waṣala* | يَصِلُ *yaṣilu* | arrive |
| assimilated | و ج د | وَجَدَ *wajada* | يَجِدُ *yajidu* | find |
| hamzated | أ خ ذ | أَخَذَ *akhadha* | يَأْخُذُ *yaʾkhudhu* | take |
| doubled | م د د | مَدَّ *madda*, مَدَدْتُ *madadtu* | يَمُدُّ *yamuddu* | stretch |

In hollow verbs the long vowel of قَالَ shortens before a consonant ending: قُلْتُ "I said". In assimilated verbs the و of the root disappears in the imperfect: يَصِلُ. The imperative of قَالَ is قُلْ *qul* "say!", which opens four suras of the Quran: قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ (112:1).

## Finding the root

| Form | Root |
|---|---|
| قُلْتُ | ق و ل |
| يَجِدُ | و ج د |
| يَدْعُو | د ع و |
| كُنْتُ | ك و ن |

When a form shows only two consonants, look for a missing و, ي or hamza.

::: exercise root
Which root?

- يَقُولُ → ((ق ل ل / ق و ل* / ق ي ل))
- يَجِدُ → ((ج د د / و ج د* / ج ي د))
- دَعَا → ((د ع و* / د ع ي / د ع ع))
- كُنْتُ → ((ك ن ت / ك و ن* / ك ي ن))
:::

::: exercise translate
Translate.

- قَالَ لِي: أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا. → {{He said to me: welcome.}}
- كُنْتُ فِي القَاهِرَةِ. → {{I was in Cairo.}}
- وَجَدْتُ الكِتَابَ. → {{I found the book.}}
- قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ → {{Say: He is God, One. / Say: He is God, the One.}}
:::

::: summary
- Hollow: قال، قلت، يقول; كان، كنت، يكون. Defective: دعا يدعو، مشى يمشي.
- Assimilated: وصل يصل، وجد يجد. Hamzated: أخذ يأخذ. Doubled: مدّ، مددت، يمدّ.
- Two visible consonants: look for a missing و، ي or hamza; قُلْ "say!".
:::
