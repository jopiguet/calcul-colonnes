# 9. The imperfect and negation

The perfect is for completed actions; the **imperfect** is for everything not yet completed: the present, the habitual and, with a prefix, the future. Where the perfect adds suffixes, the imperfect adds prefixes: يَكْتُبُ *yaktubu* "he writes". The first sura of the Quran has two: إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ "You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help".

## The imperfect

| Person | Form | Person | Form |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | أَكْتُبُ *aktubu* | we | نَكْتُبُ *naktubu* |
| you (m.) | تَكْتُبُ *taktubu* | you (m. pl.) | تَكْتُبُونَ *taktubūna* |
| you (f.) | تَكْتُبِينَ *taktubīna* | they (m.) | يَكْتُبُونَ *yaktubūna* |
| he | يَكْتُبُ *yaktubu* | they (f.) | يَكْتُبْنَ *yaktubna* |
| she | تَكْتُبُ *taktubu* | | |

The prefixes are أَ *a-* I, تَ *ta-* you and she, يَ *ya-* he and they, نَ *na-* we. The vowel after the second radical must be learnt with the verb: يَكْتُبُ *u*, يَشْرَبُ *yashrabu a*, يَجْلِسُ *yajlisu i* "he sits". سَـ *sa-* or سَوْفَ *sawfa* before the imperfect makes a future: سَأَكْتُبُ "I will write".

## Negation

| Negation | With | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| لَا *lā* | imperfect | does not | لَا أَعْرِفُ I don't know |
| مَا *mā* | perfect | did not | مَا ذَهَبَ he didn't go |
| لَمْ *lam* | jussive (lesson 12) | did not (written style) | لَمْ يَذْهَبْ he didn't go |
| لَنْ *lan* | subjunctive (lesson 12) | will not | لَنْ يَذْهَبَ he will not go |
| لَيْسَ *laysa* | nominal sentence | is not | لَيْسَ هُنَا he is not here |

لَيْسَ conjugates like a perfect: لَسْتُ *lastu* "I am not", لَسْتَ, لَيْسَتْ, لَسْنَا, لَيْسُوا. Its predicate is in the accusative: لَسْتُ طَالِبًا "I am not a student".

::: exercise neg
Which negation?

- "I don't know" → ((لَا أَعْرِفُ* / مَا أَعْرِفُ / لَيْسَ أَعْرِفُ))
- "he didn't go" (perfect) → ((لَا ذَهَبَ / مَا ذَهَبَ* / لَنْ ذَهَبَ))
- "he will not go" → ((لَمْ يَذْهَبْ / لَنْ يَذْهَبَ* / لَا ذَهَبَ))
- "I am not a student" → ((لَسْتُ طَالِبًا* / لَا طَالِب / مَا طَالِب))
:::

::: summary
- Imperfect: prefixes أَ تَ يَ نَ + stem + -u; plurals -ūna; يَكْتُبُ، يَشْرَبُ، يَجْلِسُ.
- Future: سَـ / سَوْفَ + imperfect.
- لَا + imperfect, مَا + perfect, لَمْ + jussive, لَنْ + subjunctive, لَيْسَ "is not" + accusative.
:::
