# 17. Conditions and time clauses

Arabic has three words for "if": إِنْ for a real possibility, إِذَا for what is expected to happen (often "when"), and لَوْ for the unreal. All three usually take the **perfect**, whatever the time: the condition is seen as settled. Sura 110 opens with one: إِذَا جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ وَالْفَتْحُ "when God's help comes, and the victory".

## The three "if"s

| Word | Use | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| إِنْ *in* | real possibility | perfect or jussive | إِنْ دَرَسْتَ نَجَحْتَ if you study, you will succeed |
| إِذَا *idhā* | expected, "when" | perfect | إِذَا جَاءَ فَقُلْ لَهُ when he comes, tell him |
| لَوْ *law* | unreal | perfect; main clause with لَـ | لَوْ كُنْتُ غَنِيًّا لَسَافَرْتُ if I were rich, I would travel |

The main clause takes فَـ *fa-* "then" when it is not a plain verb: an imperative, a nominal sentence, a negation, a future: إِذَا جَاءَ فَقُلْ لَهُ. After لَوْ, the main clause usually has لَـ: لَسَافَرْتُ. "Unless" is إِلَّا إِذَا; "if not" with لَوْ is لَوْلَا + noun: لَوْلَا المَطَرُ "were it not for the rain".

## Time clauses

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| عِنْدَمَا *ʿindamā* | when | عِنْدَمَا وَصَلْتُ، نَامَ when I arrived, he was asleep (lit. slept) |
| حِينَ *ḥīna* | when, at the time | حِينَ سَمِعَ الخَبَرَ when he heard the news |
| بَعْدَ أَنْ *baʿda an* | after | بَعْدَ أَنْ أَكَلْنَا after we ate |
| قَبْلَ أَنْ *qabla an* | before (+ subjunctive) | قَبْلَ أَنْ تَذْهَبَ before you go |
| مُنْذُ *mundhu* | since, for | أَسْكُنُ هُنَا مُنْذُ سَنَتَيْنِ I have lived here for two years |
| حَتَّى *ḥattā* | until (+ subjunctive) | انْتَظِرْ حَتَّى أَرْجِعَ wait until I come back |

Translate.

- إِذَا جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ وَالْفَتْحُ → {{When God's help comes, and the victory. / When the help of God and victory come.}}

- أَسْكُنُ هُنَا مُنْذُ سَنَتَيْنِ. → {{I have lived here for two years. / I have been living here for two years.}}
- قَبْلَ أَنْ تَذْهَبَ، اتَّصِلْ بِي. → {{Before you go, call me.}}
:::

::: summary
- إنْ (possible), إذا (expected, "when"), لو (unreal), usually + perfect.
- فَـ before a main clause that is not a plain verb; لَـ after لو; لولا "were it not for".
- عندما، حين، بعد أن، قبل أن، منذ، حتى.
:::
