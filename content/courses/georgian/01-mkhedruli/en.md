# 1. The Georgian script

Georgian, *kartuli*, is spoken by about four million people, most of them in Georgia, between the Black Sea and the Caucasus. It belongs to the small Kartvelian family and is related to none of its neighbours. Its alphabet is its own too: this lesson tells its story and gives the five vowels and ten consonants.

## Three scripts, one alphabet

- Georgian has been written since the 5th century: the oldest dated inscription, at the Bolnisi Sioni church, is from 494. That first script is *asomtavruli*, "capital letters"; *nuskhuri*, a script of the church books, followed from the 9th century.
- Today's script, *mkhedruli*, appears in the 10th century. Its 33 letters are the alphabet in use; five older letters went out of use in the 19th century.
- Mkhedruli has no capital letters. For titles and signs, the *mtavruli* forms, taller, are used for whole words.
- In 2016 UNESCO listed the living culture of the three Georgian scripts as intangible heritage.
- Each letter has one sound, and each sound one letter: a word is read as it is written.

| Mkhedruli | Mtavruli | Asomtavruli | Nuskhuri | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ა | Ა | Ⴀ | ⴀ | a |
| ბ | Ბ | Ⴁ | ⴁ | b |
| გ | Გ | Ⴂ | ⴂ | g |

## Five vowels and ten consonants

| Letter | Sound | Letter | Sound | Letter | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ა | a | ბ | b | ლ | l |
| ე | e | გ | g | მ | m |
| ი | i | დ | d | ნ | n |
| ო | o | ვ | v | რ | r |
| უ | u | ზ | z | ს | s |

- The vowels are pure, as in Spanish or Italian: ა *a*, ე *e*, ი *i*, ო *o*, უ *u*.
- The letters sit on a line: some rise above it, like ბ and ლ, some fall below it, like გ and მ, and some do neither, like ა, ი, ო.
- რ is a rolled *r*; გ always a hard *g*.

## First words

| Georgian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| მამა | *mama* | father |
| დედა | *deda* | mother |
| მზე | *mze* | sun |
| ვარდი | *vardi* | rose |
| გული | *guli* | heart |
| ლომი | *lomi* | lion |

- მამა *mama* is "father", დედა *deda* "mother": the other way round from what many expect.
- Nouns end in a vowel in the form of the dictionary: ვარდი, გული, ლომი end in *-i*.

::: summary
- Georgian, a Kartvelian language, is written since the 5th century; three scripts, *asomtavruli*, *nuskhuri* and today's *mkhedruli*, of 33 letters, with no capitals (*mtavruli* for titles).
- One letter, one sound: ა a, ე e, ი i, ო o, უ u; ბ b, გ g, დ d, ვ v, ზ z, ლ l, მ m, ნ n, რ r, ს s.
- მამა father, დედა mother, მზე sun, ვარდი rose, გული heart, ლომი lion.
:::
